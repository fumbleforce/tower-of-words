Video round 2: three local options, same sources

Round 1 (proto2/video) was rejected: the train rippled like a worm and the women barely moved. Here every option gets the same three images: Rei and Mio from the approved RDBT sprites, and the monorail scene from round 1. All local on the RTX 3080 (10 GB) with 60 GB of RAM; no cloud. Times are ComfyUI's own execution time (queue waiting excluded). Peak GPU is the whole card, including about 1.4 GB used by the desktop. The notes under each clip come from checking extracted frames, not from the prompt.

  • Wan 2.2 5B, plain prompt. Round-1 setup: one sentence of vague motion. 20 steps, CFG 5, 57 frames at 24 fps. Baseline.
  • Wan 2.2 5B, timed prompt (option 4). Same model. Motion written out second by second, camera terms, 30 steps, CFG 6, 121 frames at 24 fps (5 s).
  • Wan 2.2 14B + Lightning LoRA (option 1). Wan 2.2 I2V A14B fp8 (two experts, 14 GB each, streamed from RAM), lightx2v 4-step LoRA, 4 steps, CFG 1, shift 5, 81 frames at 16 fps.
  • 14B + Lightning + anime LoRAs (option 1b). As above, plus the Civitai "Anime Style" Wan 2.2 LoRA (both experts) and, on portraits, the "live 2d dynamic wallpaper" LoRA (low-noise expert, 0.8).
  • DaSiWa Wan 2.2 14B Lightspeed (option 1c). Civitai merge of Wan 2.2 I2V 14B with the 4-step speed-up baked in and anime tuning. fp8, 4 steps, CFG 1, shift 5, 81 frames at 16 fps.

Workflows to open in ComfyUI: tools/workflows/video2-*.json and ~/ai/workflows/video2-*.json. The puppet frames: tools/workflows/puppet-face-inpaint.json.

By my frame checks the 14B options are clearly best: big, readable motion (laughs, yawns, head turns), faces and outfits stay on model, and the train keeps its shape. They also take about 3.5 minutes per 5-second clip, faster than the 5B with a long prompt, because the 4-step speed-up does the work. Most of round 1's stiffness came from the prompts: the same 5B model moves a lot once the motion is written out per second. No clip here shows the rippling-skin train from round 1, but I only checked still frames and short frame pairs, so watch them in motion. Peak GPU is about 9.8 GB for everything because ComfyUI fills whatever the card has free and streams the rest from RAM; it is not a measure of what each model needs.

Option 3: animated sprite (no video model)

Mio's approved RDBT sprite, cut into parts in code. The eyes swap between open, half and closed frames (the closed and half frames are face-only inpaints with RDBT, pasted back through a soft mask). The mouth frames are drawn to match her tiny line mouth, because the inpainted mouths came out as large glossy lips in a different style. A WebGL shader bends the loose strands and the bun around their roots, breathes (a slight stretch from the waist up) and tilts the head a little. Blinks come at random gaps of 2 to 6 seconds, sometimes twice. When a line plays, the loudness of her voice picks the mouth frame.
