# Hold this a second

Eric's second repair ticket puts him at the controls of a parked train while Mio watches doors neither of them trusts yet. A working test needs two people, and the player decides whether to spend the afternoon as Mio's second pair of hands or restore an old control pendant so she can finally stop organising her work around somebody else's availability.

## What the player does

Move a luggage trolley through a doorway, watch an obstruction test, and try a familiar command under controlled conditions. Choose between paired testing, with Eric at the controls and Mio moving the trolley, and repairing the original remote pendant so Eric can work beside the doorway. Either Eric and Mio return together or Eric demonstrates the pendant to Emi while Mio finishes her work downstairs. Both routes preserve the old controller and produce an honest report.

## Cast, places and language

Eric, Mio and Emi. The existing station platform and train interior; a small service cupboard beside the carriage doors contains the station's old diagnostic terminal. Later, B2 and a return walk to the station. The empty carriage is parked for maintenance throughout.

One new command: 閉めて (shimete, close). 待って (matte, wait) comes from the train arrival. Japanese, reading and English remain together. Mio says the new word, repeats it slowly, then Eric types or speaks it. Later uses keep supported typing or speech until the existing practice requirement is met. No timed responses or recall tests.

Bracketed paragraphs are staging instructions, never player-facing exposition. All spoken English below is literal English. Emi and Mio do not need overheard Japanese in this thread.

## Morning: the cupboard

[The player reaches the platform on foot. An empty carriage stands with its near doors open. A barrier across the far end prevents boarding from the public platform. Mio's tool bag props open the service cupboard, whose door hides her from the waist up. Her feet are visible underneath. Eric remains visible when the camera frames her. Talk starts the scene.]

Mio: "Oh, 外人 (gaijin, foreigner), you're here. Can you take my bag out? It's the black one, by your foot."

[The bag is marked. Eric picks it up; the cupboard door swings partway shut. Mio catches it with her heel.]

Mio: "Ah. Yeah, put it back. I forgot why it was there."

Eric: "How long have you been in there?"

Mio: "I was going to phone you, but then I remembered it's in my bag."

[She backs out carrying a wired control box. Its cable reaches only to the cupboard. The TEST button is the size of a thumb.]

Mio: "They've checked the door mechanism. Nothing broken, and nobody can make it do yesterday again. We get to check the old controller."

Eric: "What am I supposed to write if it works?"

Mio: "Write what you saw. Please don't put 'fixed' because the door opened once. I come here a lot for those."

[She puts the control box in his hand without letting go of it.]

Mio: "You hold this button, and it lets me run one test. When you let go, everything stops. It's meant to be like that."

Eric: "And you watch the doors?"

Mio: "Mm. Usually I hold the button and somebody tells me what happened. Then I ask what happened, like, exactly, and we do it again."

[She lets go of the box. For once, she takes the place beside the door.]

Mio: "Can you do this end first? I want to actually see it."

[Interaction control: Hold TEST. Releasing it pauses the demonstration immediately and safely; it does not lose progress. The scripted demonstration stops advancing whenever dialogue appears.]

[Eric holds TEST. Mio selects a cycle on the laptop resting on the luggage trolley. The doors travel inward, stop well apart and open again.]

Mio: "Okay, let go."

[Player releases TEST.]

Eric: "It stopped in the middle."

Mio: "Yeah, that part is supposed to happen. I told it to."

[She reaches for her laptop, then stops.]

Mio: "That is exactly what everybody tells me from this end. Okay. Sorry."

[She turns the laptop so its simple door diagram faces Eric. The next test is selected, but does not run.]

Mio: "Next one should close all the way. Keep your hands over there."

[Player holds TEST again. The doors close normally and reopen. The player releases the button.]

Mio: "Good. Now we put the trolley through, and they should open before they touch it."

Eric: "The one with your laptop on it?"

[She takes her laptop off the trolley.]

Mio: "Mm. Good point."

## A useful obstruction

[The camera shows the trolley, both sides of the doorway and the folded service barrier, which leaves ample space beside the trolley. Eric can release the controls and walk over. Mio holds the button while he positions the trolley at a marked spot. Positions snap into place; nothing requires precise dragging.]

Mio: "Bring it across until the little front wheel is inside. You can leave it there. We're only testing when you tell me."

[The player moves the trolley. Its swivelling front wheel turns sideways at the sill. It stops with the handle sticking out onto the platform.]

Eric: "Does it always do that?"

Mio: "Only when you want it to go somewhere. Pull it back a little, then try."

[The trolley action changes to Straighten the wheel. Eric rolls it back and forward; it settles across the sensor line. No repeated attempts are required.]

Mio: "Okay, stand beside me. We can watch from here."

[Eric returns to the cupboard. Mio presses the cycle key on her laptop with the hand that is not holding TEST. The doors start closing, sense the trolley and reopen.]

Eric: "That looked normal."

Mio: "It is normal. And yesterday, when you said wait, they stopped without opening again. So... we should try that too. Empty doorway first."

[She releases TEST. Eric wheels the trolley clear. Mio stays at the controls.]

Mio: "I can reset it from here if it gets stuck. Do you want to try?"

Choice:

- "Yes. Let's see if I can close them afterwards." Go to **The word test**.
- "I'd rather finish the ordinary checks." Go to **Ordinary checks**.

### The word test

Mio: "Okay. For close, it's 閉めて (shimete, close). Like this."

Narration: She brings her hands together, leaving a small gap between her palms.

Mio, slowly: "閉めて (shimete, close)."

[Supported input: Eric types or speaks shimete.]

Eric: "閉めて (shimete, close)."

[The empty doors close. Their metal edges shimmer; the motor falls silent before the leaves finish moving. Mio's finger is visibly off TEST. She looks down at it, then at Eric.]

Mio: "Mm. Okay, that's still happening."

Eric: "Your button wasn't down."

Mio: "I know. I was really hoping it was."

[She resets the controller and opens the doors with its ordinary controls.]

Mio: "We'll try wait while they're moving. Whenever you're ready. They won't go anywhere."

[The next cycle begins only when Eric selects the empty doorway. It pauses at a generous opening, then presents 待って (matte, wait). Eric says it. The usual shimmer and deep tone accompany an unnatural halt. No person or object is between the leaves.]

Eric: "待って (matte, wait)."

[The door has stopped; the controller's door diagram still shows motion.]

Mio: "There. The picture thinks it's moving. That's what I couldn't get yesterday from their report."

Eric: "Shall I close it?"

Mio: "Yeah. Same word, shimete."

[Supported input remains visible.]

Eric: "閉めて (shimete, close)."

[The doors finish closing with the unnatural effect. Mio resets again, and they reopen with ordinary motor noise.]

Mio: "Well, you can undo it. That's something. I still need the normal test, because you can't stand on every platform."

[Proceed to **The long cable**. The report will retain this witnessed discrepancy; it will not call the unexplained freeze repaired.]

### Ordinary checks

Mio: "Okay. I won't put words in your mouth. Especially here."

[She uses the controller for another ordinary cycle, then records the result. Eric holds TEST. The door moves normally.]

Eric: "That one closed all the way."

Mio: "Mm. I'll write that we couldn't reproduce the thing from yesterday with the controls. That's true."

[Proceed to **The long cable**. This route does not teach shimete or mark it known.]

## The long cable

[Mio bends to put the control box away. Its short lead tugs Eric towards the cupboard before she realises he still holds it.]

Mio: "Sorry. Give me that."

Eric: "Was there ever a longer one?"

Mio: "There is a longer one. It's underneath."

[She tips her head towards the cupboard's lower shelf. Eric opens it. An older pendant sits in a loop of thick orange cable. The moulded handle has a split at its strain relief; the cable itself is intact.]

Mio: "The cable slides in the handle, so it pulls on the connections. I brought the clamp for it last time, but then the train was needed, and... it's still in my bag."

Eric: "You kept bringing it?"

Mio: "It's a small clamp. I don't notice until I'm looking for something else."

[She unzips the bag. The clamp is in a transparent packet with the pendant's model number handwritten across it. She presses the packet flat against her thigh.]

Mio: "We have the carriage this afternoon too. We need to check it after the controller's been running a while. Emi's coming to sign for B2."

Eric: "And you?"

Mio: "I should finish something downstairs. I keep getting halfway through it, then somebody needs English, or my finger on a button."

[She looks back at the doorway.]

Mio: "But I'd like to finish the whole check myself once. Usually I only get somebody's notes."

Choice:

- "I'll take the cupboard again this afternoon. You can do the tests." Paired route: keep the current working controls; Mio returns and conducts the full test herself.
- "Let's fix the long one. I'll show Emi how to use it." Pendant route: repair the existing pendant now; Eric owns the afternoon demonstration and follow-up.

### Paired route: the second person

Mio: "You don't mind? It's really boring in there."

Eric: "You can tell me what's happening. Exactly."

[Mio gives him a look, then lets out a small laugh. She puts the clamp back in the bag.]

Mio: "Okay, I deserved that. Come find me after lunch. And bring your coat, I opened the wrong cupboard first and it was all outside."

[They stow the trolley, shut the service cupboard and walk through the covered walkway to B2. The morning scene ends when the player reaches the lift. Resume at **Afternoon, paired** when Eric chooses to return with Mio; reading or exploring cannot miss the appointment.]

### Pendant route: room to move

Mio: "You'll have to show her the whole check. You can't just hand it over and come back."

Eric: "I know. Show me how you do it."

[She sets the disconnected pendant on the platform bench. The open handle, cable and loose clamp are visible together. The player selects Hold the cable, then Fit the clamp after Mio positions it. No electrical work is performed while connected.]

Mio: "Leave a little slack inside. Like that, yeah. Now hold it while I close this."

[The player holds the cable steady. Mio tightens the two screws.]

Eric: "Am I allowed to be useful without talking?"

Mio: "Please."

[She finishes, gives the cable a checking tug, and hands him the pendant. They reconnect it at the cupboard. Eric carries it to the doorway; the orange cable pays out along the wall, outside the walking route.]

Mio: "Oh, that's nice. You can actually see both doors."

[The player holds TEST and watches a normal cycle from the platform. On release it stops; on the next deliberate press the cycle resumes. Eric then positions the trolley and repeats the obstruction check. Mio observes beside him, hands in her pockets.]

Eric: "What else do I need to show her?"

Mio: "That it stops when you let go. People like to tape the button down. Don't let her do that."

Eric: "Does Emi tape buttons down?"

Mio: "I don't know, she gets a lot done."

[They stow the pendant on its original hook and walk back through the walkway to B2. Resume at **Afternoon, pendant** when Eric chooses to return; he will not lose the appointment by exploring.]

## Afternoon, paired

[Eric and Mio walk from B2 to the station. Emi is on the platform, reading the ticket on her phone.]

Emi: "There you are. How are our doors?"

Mio: "We're checking again. Eric holds the button, I do the trolley."

Emi: "Do you need me to hold something?"

Eric: "You can take notes. I'm going into the cupboard."

[Eric takes his place; Mio parks the laptop on the bench and wheels the trolley over. Player holds TEST. Mio catches the sideways front wheel with her shoe and straightens it without stopping.]

Mio: "The trolley is across now. I'm starting it."

[Doors begin closing and reopen before touching the trolley.]

Mio: "They opened again. Neither side touched. You can let go."

[Player releases. Mio clears the trolley. The next deliberate press runs an empty cycle.]

Mio: "They're shut all the way. The little light is green. Now open again... yeah, both sides."

[Player releases TEST and comes out of the cupboard. Emi has put her phone away and written the observations on Mio's test sheet.]

Emi: "Would one of the station staff be able to do your bit?"

Eric: "Yes. We'll need a second person whenever they ask for this check. Put that on the request."

Emi: "All right. I'll arrange the pair before I send anyone over."

[She adds the requirement to the request. Mio checks the line before taking the sheet back.]

Mio: "And they stay for the whole thing. Sometimes they go away while I'm still talking."

Emi: "The whole thing."

[Proceed to **Signing off**.]

## Afternoon, pendant

[Eric walks from B2 to the station alone. Emi waits beside the closed cupboard.]

Emi: "Mio's staying downstairs?"

Eric: "She's finishing something. I'll show you the test."

Emi: "Good. I was about to ask her to come up."

[Eric opens the cupboard and pays out the repaired pendant's cable. Emi follows him to the doorway.]

Emi: "That looks older than the train."

Eric: "It reaches further than the new one. Here, hold the button."

[The player gives Emi the pendant and moves the trolley across the doorway. Eric selects Run obstruction check on the laptop. The doors reopen before reaching the trolley.]

Eric: "Now let go."

[Emi releases TEST. The controller pauses.]

Emi: "Oh. I have to keep holding it?"

Eric: "Yes. That's part of the test."

[Eric clears the trolley and returns. Player action Take the pendant lets him demonstrate an empty cycle, then release TEST and stop it deliberately. Emi watches his hand and the doors together.]

Emi: "Fine. Can you show the station team that when they take the carriage back?"

Eric: "I can show them. I'll need the instructions in Japanese."

Emi: "I'll do those. Send me your steps in English."

[She types the task under her own name. Eric coils the cable on its original hook, leaving the repaired handle facing out. His phone lights up with a text from Mio.]

Mio, text: "did she tape it down"

Eric, text: "No."

Mio, text: "good"

Mio, text: "finished my thing btw"

[Proceed to **Signing off**.]

## Signing off

[Emi reads the actual recorded result aloud.]

If the word test happened:

Emi: "Normal controls passed. Obstruction check passed. The unusual hold happened again during your additional test, so that part stays open. Is that right?"

Eric: "Yes. I don't know how to fix that part yet."

Emi: "Then that's what we send them. They can decide what further checks they need."

If ordinary checks were chosen:

Emi: "Normal controls passed. Obstruction check passed. We haven't reproduced yesterday's hold. Is that right?"

Eric: "Yes. I can't say we've fixed something we couldn't reproduce."

Emi: "Fair enough. I'll send that as the result."

[In either case, the controller checks are recorded as completed; the unexplained event is left for station review. The train stays in maintenance until station staff accept it. Nobody declares the whole railway safe or upgrades the equipment.]

On the paired route:

[Mio sets the tool bag on Eric's side of the bench while she shuts the cupboard.]

Mio: "You can carry that back. I think I did more walking."

Eric: "I had quite a difficult cupboard."

Mio: "Mm, I heard. You told us exactly."

[She waits while he picks up the bag, then walks back beside him.]

On the pendant route:

[Emi leaves for the lobby. Eric receives a final text before walking back.]

Mio, text: "leave the clamp packet in my bag"

Eric, text: "It's empty."

Mio, text: "yeah i want to throw it away myself"

[At B2, Mio's bag is open beside her desk. Eric places the packet inside. She pulls it straight out, checks it is empty and drops it in the bin without looking away from her screen.]

Mio: "Oh, good. Thank you."

## Continuity and consequences

The assigned station ticket is the only required part of day 1's revised ending. The first train scene supplies the shared experience of 待って; nothing depends on a particular greeting, gift or lunch choice. The two labour routes leave different practical changes: a recorded two-person requirement that Emi must arrange, or the repaired pendant and an English-to-Japanese handover owned by Eric and Emi. Mio either gets to conduct the tests herself with a reliable colleague or finishes her interrupted B2 work.

This thread occupies a morning visit and a short chosen afternoon return. Other day-2 subplots fit between them; neither is required to obtain a tool, learn a word or close a branch here. All appointments wait for deliberate player actions. The physical state of the old equipment and the ticket's honest status remain visible afterwards.
