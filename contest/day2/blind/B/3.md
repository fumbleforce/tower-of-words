# A room with a view

Eric wants to borrow the dorm's projector so he can look at something besides the concrete wall outside his window. Hamada has already wheeled it into the common room with a film and his dinner, and neither man has enough of the other's language to explain what sort of evening he wants.

## What the player does

Walk from the dorm corridor into its common room, move the projector trolley and aim the picture by standing at different viewing spots. Try a new command on the video deck, play with the projected image, then choose whether to borrow the equipment for a quiet evening or stay and watch with Hamada. Both choices have a playable ending and leave something in the world to revisit.

## Cast and place

Eric and Hamada carry the scene. Mio answers a short text exchange from elsewhere; she does not arrive to translate. The new dorm common room has a working television, a low table, mismatched chairs and an old projector on a trolley with a VHS deck beneath it. The island's residents can borrow the trolley, signing it out on a clipboard. Eric's room is down the same corridor: a bed, a desk and a window facing a concrete wall.

Hamada has brought a tape of a silent slapstick comedy, his dinner and his own cushion. He likes this room's large picture, but can watch on the television if Eric borrows the trolley. His evening has already begun when Eric enters.

## Words

One new command: 戻って, modotte, “go back.” Hamada uses it to ask Eric to move the trolley back. Eric later discovers that the video deck takes the same word as a request to reverse playback.

Reuse 待って, matte, “wait,” and 動いて, ugoite, “move/work,” both taught on day 1. English and romaji remain beside the Japanese. Each new-word use accepts typing or voice; three uses unlock the click shortcut. The reverse-play experiments are optional after the first attempt. No reading or typing timer runs.

## Script

Directions in square brackets are staging, not narration. Every Hamada sentence marked `overheard: true` receives the existing blur treatment, with only learned words clear. The English notes following his first two longer lines are writer glosses, never subtitles.

### 1. The borrowing cupboard

[Evening. The player walks along the dorm corridor. Eric's room and the common room are both accessible. Entering his room first changes one line below. The common room is visible through its open doorway: a projected beach crosses the wall, the television beside it and part of a chair. Hamada is finishing a voice call on his laptop. Its screen shows initials instead of his camera preview.]

Hamada [warm, overheard: true]: 「じゃあ、またね。おやすみ。」

[He closes the laptop, unwraps his dinner and notices Eric at the doorway.]

Hamada [surprised, overheard: true]: 「あっ。昨日の……どうぞ、どうぞ。」

[He points to Eric, mimes the train doors closing, then moves his dinner away from the empty chair. Writer gloss: “Oh. From yesterday... come in.”]

Eric [recognising]: “You got home, then.”

[If `hamada_friend` is set from day 1:]

Eric [warm]: “The crackers were good. They didn't last long.”

[Eric mimes opening the cracker box and passing it around. Hamada laughs and gives a small bow. Otherwise, Hamada bows, then pats the chair again. Both branches continue.]

[The player can sit or inspect the projector trolley.]

If sitting first:

[Hamada starts moving his dinner toward Eric.]

Eric [apologetic]: “No, that's yours. I've eaten.”

[Eric touches his stomach and shakes his head. Hamada withdraws the food, relieved, and opens it properly. Eric stands when the player next moves. The projector remains available.]

Inspecting the trolley:

Eric [curious]: “Can I borrow this?”

[He points at the projector, then down the corridor. Hamada follows the gesture and nods. He takes the clipboard off the trolley and offers its pen. The entries are room numbers beside signatures; Eric's room number appears on his key tag.]

Hamada [polite, overheard: true]: 「部屋の番号、ここにお願いします。」

[He points from the key tag to the blank space on the sheet. Writer gloss: “Your room number here, please.”]

Eric [uncertain]: “You're using it, though.”

[Hamada points at the television, then holds up his film's sleeve. Its cover shows a man carrying an enormous pane of glass. Hamada steps sideways, protecting an imaginary pane from Eric.]

Eric [amused]: “I think I've seen that one.”

[Hamada's face brightens. He turns over the sleeve. The still on the back shows the same man standing in an empty frame, surrounded by broken glass.]

Eric [dry]: “Well, I've seen it now.”

[The borrowing choice stays open. The player can send Mio a message from the phone or move the trolley first. Sending the message is optional; returning to it after moving the trolley plays the same exchange.]

Eric's text: “is the projector in the dorm free to borrow?”

Mio's text: “yeah write your room number down”

Mio's text: “why do you need a projector already”

If Eric has entered his room:

Eric's text: “My window faces a wall.”

Otherwise:

Eric's text: “The room photo has a concrete wall outside the window.”

Mio's text: “ah you got that side”

Eric's text: “I thought a picture might help.”

Mio's text: “there are some old scenery tapes in the cupboard”

Mio's text: “i'm eating so please don't ask me which one”

[End text exchange. The cupboard can be inspected without texting; its covers show their subjects. There are two scenery tapes: an aquarium and a seaside promenade. The projector currently plays the promenade. The player can preview either through ordinary controls.]

### 2. A little further back

[Interacting with the trolley gives one control prompt: “Walk to move the trolley. Interact again to let go.” Hamada points to a clear patch of wall. Movement is constrained to clear floor, with generous cable slack; no furniture precision puzzle.]

[Push toward the wall. The projected beach shrinks. Hamada watches it, then gestures toward its former size.]

Hamada [gentle]: 「戻って。」

> He draws his hands back toward himself, bringing you and the trolley with them.

Hamada [slow]: 「戻って……」

[Show 戻って (modotte, go back), with the full English meaning. Offer type or voice, addressed to Hamada.]

Eric [trying]: 「戻って。」

[Hamada nods and repeats the pulling gesture. The trolley remains under the player's control; people are not compelled by the command.]

Eric [understanding]: “Back a bit. Right.”

[Pull back. The picture grows. At its broadest useful position it covers the wall above the chairs. Interacting releases the trolley. Hamada rolls its cable neatly out of the walkway, then carries his dinner to the kitchenette counter behind the room divider.]

[The player can inspect the wall from a chair. The promenade tape is reaching a shot of a sunburned couple waving into the camera. An enormous waving hand crosses the room.]

Eric [to himself, low]: “I preferred the sea.”

[The video deck is the marked target. The player can use its rewind button or say 戻って (modotte, go back). Both choices are visible.]

Using the rewind button:

[Static bars cross the picture. The couple jump backward, then the beach returns. Releasing the button resumes normal playback.]

Eric [quiet]: “There.”

[The command remains available. The player may continue directly to the evening choice without trying it on the deck.]

Saying 戻って to the deck:

[Accept type or voice. Hamada is behind the divider, facing the sink. At Eric's word the deck gives the established low kotodama note. Its display still reads PLAY. On the wall, the sea draws itself backward from the shore, clean and sharp. A gull flies tail first across the entire room. The usual rewind static never appears.]

Eric [whisper]: “Oh. You can do that.”

[Playback stays reversed while the player examines it. No failure comes from waiting. The ordinary play button returns it to forward playback; 動いて (ugoite, move) does the same with another low pulse. 待って (matte, wait) freezes the projected frame while the tape mechanism continues audibly. Pressing play releases the hold.]

[A third optional modotte use reverses the aquarium preview. Fish swim backward between the chairs. It counts toward the shortcut unlock without adding a separate practice screen. Repeated uses remain toys and earn no repeated relationship reward.]

Eric [first reversed-fish use only, whisper]: “Sorry. That's probably confusing.”

[Hamada returns holding his dinner and a damp cloth. He wipes the low table. Normal playback resumes as Eric touches the play button, before Hamada faces the picture. If the player did not use magic, Hamada returns after the first preview instead.]

### 3. What to do with the evening

[Hamada puts his own film beside the deck. He starts to take its cassette out, remembers Eric and stops. He gestures to the clipboard and the corridor, then to the chairs. He leaves his film beside the deck.]

[Choose one. The labels state the practical consequence:]

- “I'd like to take it to my room.” Borrow the trolley tonight.
- “I'll stay for your film.” Leave the trolley here and sit with Hamada.
- “Give me a moment.” Keep previewing before deciding.

“Give me a moment”:

Eric [apologetic]: “Give me a moment.”

[Hamada nods and starts eating. Return to free movement and the previews. Interacting with the clipboard or empty chair reopens the same choice. The evening never advances without a decision.]

### 4A. Eric's room

Eric [apologetic]: “I'd like to take it to my room.”

[Eric writes his room number on the clipboard. Hamada checks that the projector's fan has finished cooling, unplugs the trolley and puts the cable on its hook. He takes his film to the television and inserts it in the television's separate deck.]

[The television works immediately. Hamada turns its volume down before sitting.]

Eric [warm]: “Thanks. Enjoy your film.”

Hamada [warm, overheard: true]: 「どうぞ。あ、コードも。」

[He points to the extension lead on the cupboard hook. Eric picks it up.]

[Choose the aquarium or promenade tape by its pictured cover. This choice controls the room's lasting view. Walk the trolley down the corridor and through Eric's door; the camera follows the trip. Hamada stays watching his film.]

[Inside, Eric positions the trolley beside his desk. Two viewing spots are marked by ordinary interaction outlines: wall beside the bed, or curtain over the real window. The player tries either by rotating the trolley with the displayed control.]

If aiming beside the bed:

[The picture fills the blank wall. Eric lies back on the bed, tests the view and puts a pillow behind his head.]

Eric [pleased]: “Yeah, that's better.”

If aiming at the curtain:

[Pulling the curtain closed provides the projection surface. The concrete wall disappears behind it; its window frame remains visible around the fish or beach. Eric sits on the edge of the bed.]

Eric [dry]: “They'll charge extra if they see this.”

[The player can choose ordinary playback, or use 戻って and 待って to keep a favourite shot. The sound of waves or water plays softly. Projection controls remain accessible after the scene.]

[Eric's phone shows a new message only if the earlier Mio exchange was sent.]

Mio's text: “did you get your window”

Choose “Send a photo” or “I'll answer tomorrow.”

Sending a photo:

[Eric's phone camera frames the chosen view.]

If aquarium:

Mio's text: “oh you got the underwater rooms”

If promenade:

Mio's text: “those two are going to wave at you every twenty minutes”

Eric's text, either tape: “I've found a way round that.”

Mio's text: “of course you have”

[Eric sets the phone face down.]

Answer tomorrow:

[Eric sets the phone face down without replying. No additional message or relationship penalty.]

[If no text exchange began, Eric simply puts his phone on charge. The room's normal save interaction becomes available. The chosen projection remains on; the player decides when to sleep.]

### 4B. The common room

Eric [warm]: “I'll stay for your film.”

[He pushes the clipboard away and taps the empty chair. Hamada gets halfway through a bow before remembering his open dinner. He saves it from tilting and laughs.]

Hamada [flustered, overheard: true]: 「あっ、{sumimasen}。危ない、危ない。」

[Hamada fills the small whistling kettle in the kitchenette and switches on its hotplate. He puts his film in the deck and moves his cushion to one end of the sofa. The player chooses a chair or the other end of the sofa. Either is comfortable and earns the same single shared-scene memory.]

[The film starts. A man in overalls carries the huge glass pane along a pavement. He cannot see a ladder ahead. No film dialogue or subtitles are needed.]

[If Eric chose the chair, its back falls one notch as he sits.]

Eric [startled]: “That's lower than I expected.”

[Hamada reaches out, sees the chair is stable, then settles back.]

[If Eric chose the sofa, Hamada briefly tries to divide his cushion between both seats.]

Eric [warm]: “Keep it. I'm fine.”

[Eric pats the sofa. Hamada takes his cushion back. Both branches continue.]

[The glass carrier approaches the ladder. Hamada leans forward. The player has two optional interactions: lean out of the beam, or raise a hand into it.]

Lean out:

[The actor ducks under the ladder. Hamada exhales; Eric looks at the sleeve, then at him.]

Eric [amused]: “You know what happens.”

[Hamada ignores the sleeve and points insistently at the film. A second ladder enters from the other side.]

Raise a hand:

[Eric's shadow covers the oncoming ladder. Hamada ducks sideways to see around it. Eric lowers his hand.]

Eric [apologetic]: “Sorry.”

[Hamada puts his dinner down, raises his own hand into the beam and catches the actor's head between finger and thumb. His timing is poor. The actor walks out of his grip.]

Eric [laughing]: “You missed him.”

[Hamada tries again. Eric can move his chair to make his own shadow larger, or sit and watch. Neither option adds spoken dialogue. Hamada returns to the film when the player sits.]

[A kettle whistles in the kitchenette. Hamada reaches for his mug, realises it is empty and stands. Before leaving he points to his eyes, then to the film.]

Hamada [apologetic, overheard: true]: 「{sumimasen}、ちょっと{matte}ください。」

[The command is addressed to Eric. Hamada goes behind the divider. The player can press pause, or quietly say 待って to the deck. The latter freezes the image on the unbroken glass with the kotodama pulse; Hamada cannot see the deck. Both actions wait indefinitely.]

[Hamada switches off the hotplate, returns with two mugs, puts one on a coaster beside Eric and sits. Interact with play to resume. If the film ran ahead while Eric explored, use the ordinary rewind button to get back to the ladder. Hamada waits while Eric finds the scene; there is no lost payoff.]

[The actor clears both ladders. A small dog trots past. The actor bows courteously to let it through, taps the pavement with the pane and loses all the glass at once.]

[Hamada laughs hard enough to put his mug down. Eric's response is a small laugh, voiced without a written line. The camera stays at the ordinary play angle, with both men and the film visible.]

[After the film, the deck clicks off. Hamada stretches, picks up both mugs and leaves Eric's empty seat in place while he cleans the table. The player can help wipe the table or wave and leave; Hamada returns the wave in either case.]

Eric [warm]: “Good night.”

[The corridor remains playable. Eric's room has its original view tonight. The trolley is available to borrow on a later visit; this evening choice does not also grant the private projection ending.]

## Continuity and consequences

The train-door mime establishes recognition on every day-1 route. The cracker line is conditional on the actual gift. Hamada's opening farewell fits after his call with his daughter in subplot 2 without explaining that subplot to a player who skipped it. No part depends on the revised day-1 closing conversation.

The private ending saves the tape and projection surface in Eric's room. The shared ending records that he watched Hamada's film and leaves their viewing arrangement in the common room, with a mug coaster beside Eric's chosen seat. Neither ending consumes money, creates a work obligation or labels the other choice a failure. Returning later can develop the other activity; this script covers the chosen evening completely.
