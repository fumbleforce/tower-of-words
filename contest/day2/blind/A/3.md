# 3. Seven Seats

## Pitch

Mori has organised Eric's welcome party at Jun's bar the way a manager would, with a paper circular, a seating plan and a table booked for seven, although B2 has five people and one of them hasn't been to a party in five years. Eric can help fill the table, and at the end of the night he has to stand up and introduce himself with the handful of Japanese words he owns.

## Why it's fun to play

- A thread the player follows with his eyes before his hands. All day Mori walks past Mio's desk, looks at the circular under her coffee can, straightens it and walks back. The player can leave it or do something about it.
- One conversation with Mio decides what kind of evening it is. She can stay home, come as Eric's translator, or come on a promise he then has to keep at the table with a bottle in his hand.
- The party is built from small real rituals the player does himself: where to sit, who pours for whom, the toast, a speech made from the few words he has, and the single clap that ends it.
- The seven chairs keep score without anyone saying so. Who fills them depends on what Eric did in the lobby that morning and whether he talked to Mio. A full table is rare, and Mori counting it is the payoff.

## Cast and places

- **Mr. Mori** (day 1). 58, formal, kind, no English. Tonight he's the organiser, and he does it properly.
- **Mio** (day 1). Doesn't go to drinking parties, for reasons she gives sideways.
- **Jun** (new; approved design). 38, payroll by day, runs the company bar in the evenings. Neat, glasses, green apron. Quiet, precise, notices numbers. No English.
- **Kenji** and **Emi** (day 1), in smaller parts.
- **Mr. Ishibashi**, **Mr. Hamada** and **Tama**, if sub-plot 1 sends them.
- Places: B2 (morning and 17:30), the walk out through the lobby, Jun's bar on the arcade street (new): a counter, a till from 1991, one long table at the back.

## Words

- **お疲れさまです** (otsukaresama desu, the everyday hello and goodbye at work). From Mori at 17:30, when he goes round the desks. Said to the team as they leave, and at the bar door at the end.
- **乾杯** (kanpai, cheers). From Mori at the bar, glass raised.
- Already known and used: よろしくおねがいします, おはようございます, すみません and 外人 (in the speech), 猫 (in the guard's line), 動いて (optional, on the till). From sub-plot 2, if learned: おかわり and ごちそうさまでした, both to Jun.

## How to read the script

`Name:` is a line in English. `Name (overheard):` is Japanese Eric can't follow; the italic line under it is for the reader. `>` is narration. Square brackets are staging. `{kanpai}` shows the word with reading and English. `[type: ...]` is the typing prompt.

## The script

### A. B2, morning: the circular

[The first time Eric sits at his desk on day 2, Mori comes over with a clipboard held in both hands, and bows.]

**Mori (overheard):** エリックさん。今晩、エリックさんの歓迎会を開かせていただきます。
*Means: Eric-san. Tonight we will be holding your welcome party. Clear: エリック.*

[He turns the clipboard round and holds it out with both hands.]

> A printed sheet with a hand-drawn map, 18:00, and a column of names, each with an empty box beside it.

**Kenji:** (from his desk, too loud) Party! Eric-san, party for you! Welcome party, tonight, at Jun-san's bar. And the guest don't pay. Best!

**Eric:** For me?

**Kenji:** For you! Is rule.

[Mori takes a small round seal from his breast pocket, breathes on it, and stamps the box beside his own name. He walks the sheet over to Kenji, who stamps his so hard the desk shakes, then reads the top of the page.]

**Kenji (overheard):** …七名？
*Means: …seven people?*

**Mori (overheard):** 七名です。
*Means: seven.*

[Kenji counts B2 on his fingers, gets to five, looks at his hand, and shrugs at Eric. Mio is in the machine room, so Mori lays the circular on her empty desk, squares it to the edge, and goes back to his own.]

### B. During the day

[Each time the period changes, Mori gets up, walks past Mio's desk, looks down at the circular, straightens it by a millimetre and walks back. No lines. From lunch on, Mio's black coffee can is sitting on top of it.]

[Talk to the circular: Mori's box and Kenji's are stamped, Eric's says 主賓 (guest of honour) with no box to fill, and Mio's and Emi's are empty.]

### C. Mio

[Trigger: talk to Mio at her desk after lunch, while the circular is under her can.]

**Eric:** Mori-san's circular is under your coffee.

**Mio:** Mm, I know. It's a good coaster, honestly.

**Mio:** I don't really do the nomikai thing. The drinking parties.

- **"Why not?"** → [below]
- **"Okay."** → **Mio:** Mm. [She goes back to her screen.] (Nothing changes. Mio doesn't come, and at 17:30 the circular is still under the can.)

**Mio:** When I was new I was upstairs, in Planning, and every Friday there's a nomikai. The new girl sits by the door, because that's the lowest seat, and she pours for everybody, the kachō, the buchō, all of them. And you have to watch every glass the whole night, so if somebody's is half empty it's your fault, basically.

**Mio:** And then they all go home and you're like, wait, did I eat anything?

[She turns the can a quarter turn on the paper.]

**Mio:** So when I came down here I stopped going. Mori-san knows, but he puts it on my desk anyway, every time, for like five years now.

**Mio:** When Kenji came I didn't go either, so now he thinks I hate him, which... I don't. He's just loud.

- **"Nobody pours at my party. I'll make sure of it."** →
  - **Mio:** Ha. It's not your party, it's Mori-san's party about you. You can't even read the menu.
  - [She looks at the can for a while.]
  - **Mio:** ...Okay. One drink. But if anybody gives me a bottle, I'm going home.
  - [She lifts the can and writes her name in her box, very small.] (Mio comes on the promise.)
- **"Come and translate for me. I won't understand a word."** →
  - **Mio:** Ahh. So I'm working tonight.
  - **Mio:** ...Fine. But I sit next to you, not by the door.
  - [She writes her name in her box.] (Mio comes as translator.)
- **"Then don't come. That's fine."** →
  - [Mio looks up at him for the first time in this conversation.]
  - **Mio:** ...Mm. Thanks.
  - [She lifts the can, writes 欠席 (absent) in her box in neat small kanji, and puts the circular back on her desk, squared to the edge.] (Mio stays home, but for the first time in five years she has answered the circular.)

### D. 17:25, Emi passes through

[The lift opens. Emi comes in fast, grabs a folder from her desk, spots the circular and stamps it standing up.]

**Emi:** Oh, is this tonight? Lovely. Head office want me on a call at six, so I'll be late, but tell him I'm coming. I am coming.

[She's gone before anyone can answer.]

### E. 17:30: お疲れさまです

[At 17:30 exactly, Mori stands, straightens his jacket, and goes round the desks one at a time with a small bow at each.]

**Mori (overheard):** お疲れさまです。
*Means: good work today. What you say to everyone at the end of the day.*

**Kenji (overheard):** お疲れさまでーす！

[Mori reaches Eric's desk, gives the small bow, and waits.]

**Mori:** {otsukare}.

**Mori:** (slow) {otsukare}...

[type: otsukare, prompt: "> He's still waiting, half bowed."]

[Eric says it. Mori bows again, satisfied, and moves on. The player can say it to the others now:]

- **Kenji:** [a fist bump] おつかれ！ (The short version. Clear: おつかれ, otsukare, casual.)
- **Mio, if she's coming:** Mm. You can just say otsukare to me. It's shorter.
- **Mio, if she isn't:** Otsukare. ...Have fun, okay?

[Mori picks up the circular and reads the boxes. If Mio wrote 欠席, he looks across at her and gives her a small nod, and she nods back. If the can is still on it, he slides the sheet out from under the can without moving the can.]

**Mori (overheard):** では、参りましょうか。
*Means: well then, shall we go?*

### F. The walk to Jun's

[Out through the lobby and along the arcade street. What Eric sees in the lobby depends on sub-plot 1: the guard putting a fish in the bowl outside the doors, the visitor badge on the bowl, the guard looking up at the lift, or Tama back beside the desk. Walking lines:]

**Kenji:** Jun-san's bar is best bar on the island.

**Mio (if she's coming):** It's the only bar on this street.

**Kenji:** So, best!

### G. Jun's bar: seats

[A narrow room, a counter, and one long table at the back set for seven, with seven glasses and seven small dishes. Jun, in a green apron, is drying a glass.]

**Jun (overheard):** いらっしゃいませ。森さん、七名様ですね。
*Means: welcome. Mori-san, a party of seven, isn't it.*

[Jun looks at the people coming in behind Mori and counts them without moving his lips.]

**Jun (overheard):** …はい。どうぞ、奥へ。
*Means: …right. Please, go on through.*

[Goal: "Sit down." The player picks a chair.]

- **A chair by the door:** [Mori stops dead.] **Mori (overheard):** あっ、いえいえ、エリックさんはこちらです。今日の主役ですから。 *Means: oh, no, no, you're over here. You're the guest of honour tonight.* [He steers Eric by the elbow to the far end.] **Kenji:** Door seat is for the youngest. Is my seat, always.
- **The chair at the far end:** [Mori nods, very pleased.] **Kenji:** Eh! You know the rule?

[Kenji takes the door seat. If Mio came on the promise, she sits next to Kenji at the door end without thinking about it. If she came as translator, she sits next to Eric.]

### H. Pouring

[Jun puts three big bottles of beer on the table. Mori picks one up and, out of thirty years of habit, holds it out toward the door end. Kenji takes it and starts pouring for Mori with both hands.]

**If Mio came on the promise:** [Her hand goes to the second bottle before she's thought about it, and she's already half out of her chair to pour for the guest of honour.]

- **Take the bottle and pour for Mio** →
  - [Eric takes the bottle out of her hand. She stops. Then she picks up her glass and holds it out with both hands, tilted at exactly the right angle. Eric pours, with far too much foam.]
  - **Mio:** You're doing it wrong. Label up.
  - [He turns the bottle so the label faces up. She lets him finish.]
  - [Mori has watched the guest of honour pour for Mio. There's a pause. Then he laughs, once, and holds his own glass out to Eric.]
  - **Mori (overheard):** では、私にもお願いします。 *Means: in that case, me too, please.*
  - [After that, nobody keeps track of who pours for whom.]
- **Let her pour** →
  - [Mio pours for Eric, for Mori and for Kenji, label up, not a drop spilled, and sits back down.]
  - (She'll leave after the toast; see I.)

**If Mio came as translator:** [She doesn't touch the bottles. Kenji, very carefully, fills her glass.] **Kenji:** Mio-san, please. **Mio:** ...Thanks, Kenji. [Kenji doesn't say anything for quite a while.]

**If Mio isn't there:** [Kenji pours for everyone. Her chair is empty.]

### I. The toast

[Mori stands with his glass.]

**Mori (overheard):** えー、本日はお忙しいところ、お集まりいただきまして、ありがとうございます。エリックさんの歓迎会ということで、一言…
*Means: well, thank you all for coming on such a busy day. As this is Eric-san's welcome party, just a few words… Clear: エリック.*

[He keeps going.]

- **Mio at the table:** He says welcome. [Mori keeps going.] That's, like, the whole thing. It's just longer.
- **No Mio:** **Kenji:** He say welcome. And more welcome. Very polite welcome.

[At last Mori lifts his glass toward Eric and holds it there.]

**Mori:** {kanpai}.

**Mori:** (slow) {kanpai}...

[type: kanpai, prompt: "> Every glass at the table is up."]

**Everyone (overheard):** 乾杯！
*Reads: 乾杯 sharp.*

[Glasses touch all down the table. Kenji goes red after half a glass.]

**Kenji:** I am strong. Is only my face.

[If Mio came on the promise and Eric let her pour: she drinks half her glass, puts it down and stands.]

**Mio:** I have a build running. ...Sorry, Mori-san.

[She bows to Mori and goes. Her chair stays pulled out.]

### J. The chairs fill

While the table talks, the player can:

- **Say おかわり to Jun** (if learned at lunch): **Jun (overheard):** はい、おかわりですね。 *Means: yes, another one. Reads: おかわり sharp.* [A fresh bottle.]
- **Talk to Kenji,** if he went first at lunch: he stands up and tells the table, in Japanese, with the penguin waddle. Mori laughs until he has to take his glasses off.
- **Talk to Kenji,** if Eric went first at lunch: **Kenji:** You go first, I pour. Is fair. [He tops up Eric's glass every time it drops below half, all evening.]

Then the door opens. Who comes in depends on sub-plot 1.

**Hamada** (the record ending, or the apology ending): [He comes in alone for his Friday beer, still wearing the yellow armband. He sees Eric and bows. Mori is on his feet at once, waving him to an empty chair as if he'd been expected.]

**Mori (overheard):** 浜田さん、どうぞ、こちらへ。
*Means: Hamada-san, please, over here.*

**Hamada (overheard):** え、いいんですか、{sumimasen}、{sumimasen}…
*Means: oh, are you sure? Sorry, sorry…*

- *Record ending:* **Hamada (overheard):** 猫が、来客になりました。 *Means: the cat became a visitor. Reads: 猫 sharp.* **Kenji:** The cat is... a visitor? What cat? **Mio (if there):** The guard's cat. The one that doesn't exist.
- *Apology ending:* [Jun sets a beer in front of Hamada that nobody ordered.] **Jun (overheard):** 浜田さん、守衛さんからです。 *Means: Hamada-san, this is from the guard.* [Hamada looks at the glass, picks it up in both hands and bows toward the door. Then he tells the table, pink to the ears:] **Hamada (overheard):** 今日、生まれて初めて、書類に嘘を書きました… *Means: today, for the first time in my life, I wrote a lie on a form…* **Kenji:** Hamada-san say today he write a lie on paper. First time in his life. [Hamada laughs and drinks.] **Kenji:** He look very happy about it.

**The guard** (the truth ending): [At half past six the door opens. It's the guard, out of uniform, in a grey jacket and no cap. Behind the counter, Jun stops drying the glass.]

**Mori (overheard):** 石橋さん！お珍しいですね。どうぞ、どうぞ。
*Means: Ishibashi-san! Well, this is a rare sight. Please, please.*

[Mori and the guard bow to each other, both very deep. The guard sits in an empty chair with his back straight, takes the bottle, and pours for Eric with two hands, label up. Then he bows over the table.]

**Guard (overheard):** 猫の件では、お世話になりました。
*Means: thank you for your help, in the matter of the cat. Reads: 猫 sharp, the rest blurred.*

**Kenji:** Eh? Neko no ken? What cat?

**Mio (if there):** Don't.

[Tama, who has followed the guard in, walks the length of the bar and jumps up onto an empty chair.]

**Jun (overheard):** …猫、ですね。
*Means: …that's a cat.*

**Guard (overheard):** たまです。
*Means: this is Tama.*

**Emi** (always; she stamped the circular): [About seven, lanyard still on.]

**Emi:** Sorry, sorry. Head office rang at six and just kept ringing.

**Emi:** Good news, I'm here. I've only got till eight, but I'm here.

[She sits in the nearest empty chair and touches her glass to everyone's.]

**Emi:** {kanpai}! Eric, we never had our proper chat. This one's going to be a slightly drunk chat, I'm afraid. We'll do a sober one on Monday.

### K. Seven

[Jun brings a plate of karaage and looks at the chairs.]

**Jun (overheard):** 森さんは、いつも七名様ですね。
*Means: it's always seven with you, isn't it, Mori-san.*

**Mori (overheard):** ええ。
*Means: yes.*

- **Mio at the table:**
  - **Mio:** He always books seven. There were seven people in B2 when he came down, in the nineties. He says booking less is bad luck.
  - [She drinks.]
  - **Mio:** I think he made that up.
- **No Mio:**
  - **Kenji:** He always book seven. Before, B2 is seven people.
  - [He counts round the table with his chopsticks, and puts them down.]

**If every chair is taken** (Mio still at the table after the toast, the guard and Tama, Emi): [Mori looks down the table and counts under his breath, one finger moving.]

**Mori (overheard):** …七。
*Means: …seven.*

[And he laughs, properly, with his head back. Kenji stares at him, then starts laughing too.]

**If any chair is empty:** [Mori fills the glasses at the empty places himself, label up, and leaves them there.]

### L. The speech

**Mori (overheard):** では、エリックさん。一言、お願いします。
*Means: well then, Eric-san. A few words, please.*

[Everyone turns to Eric.]

- **Mio:** You have to say something. Like, introduce yourself. Short is fine. Short is better, actually.
- **No Mio:** **Kenji:** Speech! Small speech. Your name, and... anything!

[The choice. The Say menu offers short phrases built from the words he has:]

- **おはようございます**
  - [A pause. Kenji laughs first, then the whole table.]
  - **Mori (overheard, laughing):** こんばんは、ですね。 *Means: that would be "good evening".*
  - [He bows anyway.]
  - **Mio (if there):** It's the evening, Eric.
- **"エリックです。よろしくおねがいします。"** and a bow
  - [Mori stands up to bow back, which makes everyone else stand up to bow, and for a moment the whole table is on its feet.]
  - **Mori (overheard):** こちらこそ、よろしくお願いします。 *Means: the pleasure is ours.*
  - [Small applause. Behind the counter, Jun claps twice.]
- **"外人です。よろしくおねがいします。"**
  - [If Mio is there, she chokes on her beer.]
  - **Mori (overheard, gently):** 外国の方、ですよ。 *Means: "person from abroad", please. Clear: 外国の方 (gaikoku no kata), as on day 1, when he told Mio the same.*
  - [He thinks about it.]
  - **Mori (overheard):** …うちの、外国の方です。 *Means: …our person from abroad.*
  - **Mio (if there):** He says you're our foreigner now. [She looks at her glass.]
  - **Kenji (if not):** He say you are our gaijin now. Is good. Is very good.
- **すみません**
  - [Everyone bows back without thinking, and Hamada, if he's there, says すみません back.]
  - **Mio (if there):** Why are you apologising?
  - **Kenji (if not):** Why sorry? ...Sorry!
- **Say it in English** (only if Mio is there)
  - **Eric:** I'm Eric. I'm from Norway. I fix old machines.
  - **Mio (overheard):** エリックです。ノルウェーから来ました。古い機械を直してます。…あと、森さんがちょっと怖いそうです。 *Means: I'm Eric, from Norway. I fix old machines. …And apparently he's a bit scared of Mori-san. Clear: エリック, ノルウェー (Noruwē, Norway).*
  - [The table laughs. Mori laughs hardest.]
  - **Eric:** What did you tell them?
  - **Mio:** Nothing. Just, you know. Norway.

### M. One clap

**Mori (overheard):** それでは最後に、一本締めで締めたいと思います。
*Means: and now, to finish, let's close with a single clap.*

- **Mio:** When he says "iyo", everybody claps once, together. Just once, okay? Three times is a different thing.
- **No Mio:** **Kenji:** One clap! Pan! When Mori-san say "yo".

**Mori (overheard):** お手を拝借。
*Means: may I borrow your hands.*

[Every pair of hands at the table comes up.]

- **Clap** → [One clap, alone, in a silent bar. Everyone looks at Eric. Mori doesn't miss a beat.] **Mori (overheard):** …いよーっ。 [The whole table claps once, together, a second after Eric, and falls about laughing.] **Kenji:** Two clap! Norway style!
- **Wait** → **Mori (overheard):** いよーっ。 [Clap] → [One sharp clap, everyone together.]

**Mori (overheard):** ありがとうございました！
*Means: thank you very much!*

[Everyone bows. Behind the counter, Jun bows too.]

### N. Going home

[At the till, the 1991 till prints half of Mori's receipt and stops. Jun presses a key. Nothing.]

- **Say 動いて to the till** → [The kotodama effect. It prints the rest. Mori looks at the till, then at Eric, and puts one finger to his lips, as he did at the copier on day 1.]
- **Leave it** → [Jun takes the pen from behind his ear and writes the receipt out by hand, very neatly.]

[At the door the player can say お疲れさまです to each person (Kenji: "Otsukare! Monday!"; Emi, already on her phone: "Otsukare, everyone! I have to ring them back."; Hamada, a very deep bow). If learned, ごちそうさまでした to Jun gets a small nod and ありがとうございました.]

[Outside, Mori turns back and bows to the bar's door. Through the window, Jun is clearing the table.]

**If Mio chose to stay home** (the "Then don't come" answer): [At the dorm entrance, Mio is standing by the vending machine with a can of coffee.]

**Mio:** I was buying coffee.

**Mio:** ...Was Mori-san happy?

- **"He was."** → **Mio:** Mm. Good. [She goes in.]
- **"He poured a glass for your chair."** → [Mio looks at the can for a while.] **Mio:** ...Next time, maybe. [She goes in.]

**If Mio left early** (Eric let her pour): [Eric's phone, on the walk back.]

**Mio (text):** sorry i left

**Mio (text):** my hand just does it, i can't stop it

## How it ties in

- **Day 1.** Mori's seven cups on the kitchenette tray (three of them dusty) if Eric had lunch with him; the circular's 七名 plants the same number for everyone else. Mio's "Upstairs you have to bow to everybody all day" at lunch is where her story about pouring comes from. Mori's 外国の方 correction comes back and turns into 「うちの外国の方」. His finger to the lips at the copier comes back at the till. Emi's "Tomorrow you and I should have a proper chat" becomes a slightly drunk one.
- **1, There Is No Cat.** Decides who fills the empty chairs: the guard and Tama after the truth, Hamada after the record, Hamada and a beer from the guard after the apology, nobody after the lift.
- **2, Nobody Goes First.** おかわり gets a beer from Jun, and ごちそうさまでした closes the night. Kenji tells his penguin story again, or pours for Eric all night because Eric went first.
- **Later.** Mio's "Next time, maybe", and a promise at the table that Eric either kept or didn't, which she will remember.

## Staging needs (for the builder)

Jun's bar as a place: a counter with Jun and the till, a long table for seven at the back with a door end and a far end. Jun's body (approved portrait exists). Bottles as props people hand to each other; a pour animation; everyone standing to bow; the one clap (hands up, then one clap together). The guard out of uniform is a small costume change.
