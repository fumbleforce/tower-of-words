# 1. There Is No Cat

## Pitch

The lobby gets its monthly safety patrol on Friday, and this month Accounts has the rota, so the man with the armband and the checklist is Mr. Hamada, who is never on time for anything. Line seven on his list is a cat with a red line through it, and the guard, who has spent years telling everyone 猫はいません (there is no cat), needs Eric to make that true for twenty minutes without either of them saying a word about it.

## Why it's fun to play

- It's a small stealth farce in a room the player already knows. Hamada works his way across the lobby one stop at a time, pointing at each thing and calling out ヨシ (the Japanese workplace "checked!"). He only moves on when the player does something, so the player watches him come and never has to beat a clock.
- The cat follows the bowl, not you. Tama ignores every word Eric has, but the machines around her don't: 待って holds the barrier so it doesn't flash when she walks under it, and the new command 来て brings the lift at once.
- 来て works on people too, as a word. Said to Hamada it brings him over, which is how Eric can walk him to the one piece of evidence in the lobby that helps the guard.
- Learning 猫 changes a line the player has heard since day 1. Before, 猫はいません is gibberish. After, it reads 猫 plus a blur, said by a man sitting next to a cat.
- The ending is chosen with the player's hands: say 猫 to Hamada, bring him to the visitor book, say すみません, or (if you're in the lift) press the close button. Four different mornings for three men and a cat.

## Cast and places

- **Mr. Ishibashi**, the guard (day 1). Strict, fair, polite. Feeds a cat he says isn't there.
- **Mr. Hamada**, Accounts, 12th floor (day 1, the sleeping man). On patrol duty for the first time, with a yellow armband and a clipboard. Terrified of doing it wrong, and he owes the guard for every late morning.
- **Tama**, the calico. Rides things. Goes where she likes.
- **Kuro**, the receptionist (day 1, visitor counter). Sees everything, says little. Teaches 来て.
- **Mio**, by text, and in person in one ending.
- Places: the lobby (gate, guard desk, visitor counter, lift bank), one lift, B2 for a moment.

## Words

- **猫** (neko, cat). A word, from Hamada. It stays sharp in the guard's line from then on, and saying it is one way to end the scene.
- **来て** (kite, come). A command, from Kuro. On the lift it's kotodama (the car arrives at once). On Hamada it's just a word, and he comes. On Tama it does nothing.
- Already known and used: おはようございます, すみません, 待って, and 開けて if he learned it at the gate on day 1.

## How to read the script

`Name:` is a line in English. `Name (overheard):` is Japanese Eric can't follow (blurred, only known words sharp); the italic line under it is for the reader, not the player. `>` is narration. Square brackets are staging. `{kite}` shows the word with reading and English, as in day 1. `[type: kite]` is the typing prompt that teaches a word.

## The script

### A. The lobby, the first time Eric comes in on day 2

[The morning rush is on. The guard isn't at his desk: he's at Kuro's visitor counter, bent over the visitor book, writing slowly with a fountain pen. He caps the pen, puts it in his breast pocket, and walks back to his desk. There he crouches, one hand held out low, palm down, fingers flapping toward himself. Tama sits by the bench, washing a paw. Her blue bowl is on the floor beside the guard's desk.]

**Guard (overheard, whispering):** おいで…おいで。
*Means: here, puss… here.*

[Tama carries on washing. The guard sees Eric. He stands, straightens his cap, and sits down at his screen.]

**Guard (overheard):** 猫はいません。
*Means: there is no cat. Eric hears it as a blur.*

### B. Hamada arrives

[The entrance doors bang open behind Eric. Hamada hurries in with a clipboard, a yellow armband on his sleeve. The guard looks at the lobby clock (8:50), then at Hamada, then at the clock again.]

**Guard (overheard):** …早いですね。
*Means: you're early.*

**Hamada (overheard, out of breath):** パトロールですから！
*Means: it's the patrol! Clear: パトロール (patorōru, patrol).*

[Hamada bows to the guard. The guard stands and bows back, exactly as deep. Then Hamada sees Eric.]

**Hamada (overheard):** あっ、昨日の…！{sumimasen}、昨日は本当に…
*Means: oh, from yesterday! Sorry, yesterday, really…*

[A deep bow. Then he can't help himself: he holds the clipboard out to Eric the way you'd show someone your new phone.]

> A checklist with little pictures down the side, and a sheet of red stickers clipped to the back. Number seven is a cat with a red line through it.

[Hamada runs his finger down the list, rehearsing under his breath, pointing at the fire extinguisher by the door and then at the exit sign.]

**Hamada (overheard):** 消火器…ヨシ。非常口…ヨシ。七番、動物…
*Means: fire extinguisher, check. Emergency exit, check. Number seven, animals…*

[At number seven his eyes go to the bowl beside the guard's desk, and he lowers his voice. He draws whiskers on his own cheeks with his fingertips.]

**Hamada:** {neko}...

**Hamada:** (slow, very quietly) {neko}...

[type: neko, prompt: "> He taps the little cat on the list."]

[Eric says it. Not quietly enough. Across the lobby, the guard's typing stops.]

**Guard (overheard):** 猫はいません。
*Now the player sees 猫 sharp, then the blur.*

[Tama, three metres from him, yawns.]

[Eric's phone.]

**Mio (text):** safety patrol in the lobby this morning, it's in the chat

**Mio (text):** good luck to the guard

[Hamada takes a deep breath, turns to the entrance, and begins. He points at the extinguisher.]

**Hamada (overheard):** 消火器、ヨシ！

[The guard looks at the bowl. Then at Eric. Then at the lifts. Then he goes back to typing.]

[Goal: "Hamada is checking the lobby. The guard's desk is number seven." Markers on the bowl, on Kuro and on Hamada.]

### C. The patrol, one stop at a time

Hamada has five stops before the desk: the exit sign, the benches, the vending machine, the gate, and the notice board. Each time Eric does something (talks to someone, says a word, picks something up, goes through the gate), Hamada moves on one stop and calls it out with a point, and the guard glances up at him. The first time it happens, the camera goes to Hamada so the player sees the rule.

- 非常口、ヨシ！ (exit, check)
- ベンチ、ヨシ！ (benches, check. Clear: ベンチ, benchi, bench.)
- 自販機、ヨシ！ (vending machine, check)
- ゲート、ヨシ！ (gate, check. Clear: ゲート, gēto, gate.)
- 掲示板、ヨシ！ (notice board, check)

Then the guard's desk (seven), then the lift (eight). If Eric stands still, so does Hamada, fussing with his pen.

Small things along the way:

- **Talk to the guard:** he types faster.
- **Say 猫 to the guard:** 「猫はいません。」 He doesn't look up.
- **Say おはようございます to Hamada:** a flustered おはようございます back and a bow; he loses his place and goes back to the extinguisher. (This buys a step.)

### D. Kuro and the visitor book

[Trigger: Eric comes within a few steps of the counter, or talks to Kuro. Kuro glances at Hamada's back. Then she catches Eric's eye, holds her hand out low, palm down, and flaps her fingers toward herself.]

[Eric takes a step back.]

[Kuro shakes her head, very slightly, and does it again, slower.]

**Eric:** (quietly) Oh. You mean come here.

[He steps toward her. She nods.]

**Kuro:** (whispering) {kite}.

**Kuro:** (slow) {kite}...

[type: kite, prompt: "> She beckons once more, small, below the counter."]

[Eric says it. Kuro turns the visitor book round to face him and puts a finger beside today's first line.]

> Today's date, and TAMA in careful capitals. The ink is still wet.

[Kuro touches her own breast pocket, where a pen would be, and tilts her head a centimetre toward the guard. Then she turns the book back and goes on with her work, her face perfectly straight.]

[If Eric says 来て to Tama at any point: she looks at him and goes back to her paw.]

### E. Way one: take the bowl

[Talk to the bowl. There's a little fish left in it.]

- **Take the bowl** → [Eric picks it up. The guard doesn't look at him. He types very fast. Tama stops washing and follows the bowl.] Goal: "Take Tama somewhere the patrol isn't."
- **Leave it** → [The guard sighs, reaches down, and slides the bowl behind the plant with his foot. Half of it sticks out.] Go to F.

**With the bowl.** Tama trots a metre behind Eric wherever he goes, stopping to sniff things.

- **The entrance:** she sits down on the mat and won't go further. She has come in, and she isn't going back out.
- **The visitor counter:** Kuro looks at the cat, at Eric, and slowly shakes her head.
- **The benches:** Hamada is about to point at the benches. Tama lies down under one anyway.
- **The gate:** Eric taps his card and walks through, and Tama goes under the flaps behind him.
  - If he says **待って** to the gate first: [the kotodama effect; the flaps freeze open, the reader stays dark, and Tama strolls through in silence. The guard sees it. He touches the brim of his cap.]
  - If he says **開けて** (only if known): [the gate slams open, as on day 1. Hamada looks round.] 「ゲ、ゲート…ヨシ…？」 *(gate… check…?)* [He writes something, then crosses it out.]
  - If he does nothing: [the gate flashes red.] 「共連れを検知しました！」 *(tailgating detected!)* [Hamada looks round, sees only Eric, and the guard says loudly to nobody, 「失礼しました」 *(sorry about that)*.]
- **The lifts:** press the button, and the lift is on the twelfth floor; it takes two of Hamada's stops to come. Or say **来て** to the lift: [the kotodama effect; it arrives at once.] Tama walks in after the bowl. Eric presses B2.

**The ride.** [Down: 1, B1, B2. The doors open on B2. Tama sits in the middle of the floor and looks up at the numbers.]

[Talk to Tama: Eric bends to pick her up.]

[She goes heavy, like a bag of rice, and slides out of his hands.]

[The doors close. The counter goes B1, 1. Upstairs, Hamada has checked an empty space beside the guard's desk (the guard's shoulders come down an inch) and moved on to number eight, the lift, and pressed the button to test it.]

[The doors open on the lobby. Hamada is standing in front of them with his arm out, pointing into the car.]

**Hamada (overheard):** エレベーター、ヨ…
*Means: lift, che… Clear: エレベーター (erebētā, lift).*

[He lowers his finger. He looks at the cat between Eric's feet, and at the bowl in Eric's hands. Across the lobby, the guard has stood up.]

**Hamada (overheard, faint):** …猫、ですよね？
*Means: …that's a cat, isn't it? Reads: …猫 plus a blur.*

- **Say 猫 to Hamada** → The truth.
- **Say すみません to Hamada** → The apology.
- **Step out, go to the visitor book, and say 来て to Hamada** → The record. (Tama stays in the lift, looking at the numbers.)
- **Press the close button** → The lift.

### F. Way two: the desk

[If Eric left the bowl. Hamada reaches the guard's desk. The bowl pokes out from behind the plant, and Tama is sitting next to it, which doesn't help.]

**Hamada (overheard):** あの…石橋さん。これは…
*Means: um, Mr. Ishibashi. This is…*

**Guard (overheard, eyes front):** 猫はいません。

[Hamada looks at the cat. The cat looks at Hamada. Hamada, who can't look at the guard, turns to the only other person there.]

**Hamada (overheard):** …猫、ですよね？

- **Say 猫 to Hamada** → The truth.
- **Go to the visitor book and say 来て to Hamada** → The record. (If Eric just taps the book, Kuro calls Hamada over herself: 「浜田さん、ちょっと。」 *Hamada-san, a moment.*)
- **Say すみません to Hamada** → The apology.
- **Walk off through the gate** → The apology, without Eric in it: Hamada decides on his own, and Eric sees the end from the far side of the barrier.

### G. The endings

#### The truth (Eric says 猫)

[Hamada lets his breath out. Somebody said it.]

**Hamada (overheard):** …ですよね。
*Means: …right.*

[He writes. Then he peels one red sticker off the sheet on the back of the clipboard and sticks it on the bowl. He bows to the guard.]

**Hamada (overheard):** {sumimasen}、規則なので…
*Means: sorry, it's the rules…*

[Hamada moves on down his list. The guard looks at the red sticker for a long time. Then he comes round the desk and picks up the bowl (or takes it from Eric with both hands, the way you'd accept a business card), and looks down at the cat.]

**Guard (overheard):** …猫、ですね。
*Means: …it's a cat, isn't it. Reads: …猫 plus a blur.*

[He turns to Eric and holds an open hand toward her, the way you'd introduce a colleague.]

**Guard (overheard):** たまです。
*Means: this is Tama. Clear: たま (Tama).*

[He carries the bowl to the entrance and sets it down outside the glass doors, square to the mat. Tama follows it out. He comes back, sits, and says おはようございます to the next person through the doors.]

(The guard's bond goes up. From now on the bowl lives outside the doors, and the guard says her name out loud every morning. In the evening the guard comes to the party.)

#### The record (Eric brings Hamada to the visitor book)

[Being called over like that makes Hamada jump.] **Hamada (overheard):** は、はい？ *Means: y-yes?* [He trots over to the counter.] Kuro turns the book round for him. He leans over it.]

**Hamada (overheard):** 来客…たま様…
*Means: visitor… Ms. Tama…*

[He looks up, and his whole face changes.]

**Hamada (overheard):** 来客なら…問題ありません！
*Means: if she's a visitor… then there's no problem!*

[He writes, turns, points at the cat, and calls it out with enormous relief.]

**Hamada (overheard):** 来客一名、ヨシ！
*Means: one visitor, check!*

**Kuro (overheard):** 入館証、お作りしますね。
*Means: I'll print her a visitor badge, then.*

[Kuro prints a badge and clips it to the rim of the bowl without a flicker on her face. The guard looks at the book, at his own handwriting. He looks at Eric. It takes a while. Then he gives Eric the smallest bow of the day.]

(Tama is officially a visitor. From tomorrow the guard signs her in, Kuro clips a fresh badge to the bowl, and the guard knows that everybody knows. His bond goes up by less, and Hamada's goes up. In the evening Hamada comes to the party.)

#### The apology (Eric says すみません, or walks off)

**Hamada (overheard):** あ、いえ、こちらこそ{sumimasen}…
*Means: oh, no, I'm the one who's sorry…*

[They bow to each other, twice. Then Hamada looks down at his list for a long moment, and writes.]

**Hamada (overheard):** …動物、なし。ヨシ。
*Means: …animals, none. Check.*

[He finishes the lift without looking at anyone and goes up in it. The guard waits until the doors have closed. Then he stands and bows to the closed doors, deeper than he bows to anyone.]

(Hamada has lied on a form for the guard, and now the guard owes him, which turns their morning arrangement round. In the evening Hamada comes to the party, and the guard buys his drink without coming in.)

#### The lift (Eric presses close)

[The doors close on Hamada's face. Down: 1, B1, B2. The doors open. Tama, who has had her ride, gets up and walks out, straight down the corridor to the machine room door, and sits in front of it.]

[Upstairs, Hamada looks at the closed doors, at his list, and at the doors.]

**Hamada (overheard):** …エレベーター、ヨシ。
*Means: …lift, check.*

[In B2 the machine room door opens. Mio looks down at the cat, then at Eric with a cat bowl in his hands.]

**Mio:** ...Why is the lobby cat at my door?

**Eric:** She wanted a ride.

**Mio:** Mm.

[She holds the door open, and Tama walks in as if she works there.]

[Tama spends the day asleep on Kenji's broken chair, so Kenji works standing up. At 17:20 the lift opens and the guard steps out onto B2 with the bowl held in both hands. He bows to the room, collects the cat without a word, and goes.]

**Mio:** Huh. I've never seen him standing up before.

(The problem is only put off, but B2 has now met the guard. Nobody from the lobby comes to the party.)

### H. The lobby later in the day

One beat, the next time Eric crosses it:

- **Truth:** [the bowl outside the doors, and the guard's voice through the glass] 「たま、おはよう。」 *(morning, Tama)*
- **Record:** [the badge on the bowl says VISITOR, and under it, in Kuro's handwriting, たま様.]
- **Apology:** [whenever the lift doors open, the guard looks up to see who's in it.]
- **Lift:** [the bowl is back beside the desk, and the guard doesn't meet Eric's eye.]

## How it ties in

- **Day 1.** The guard's 猫はいません at the gate, the TAMA line in the visitor book (now we see who writes it), Hamada from the train and the gate, and the 待って at the train doors. If the guard saw the gate burst open on day 1, the touch to his cap at the barrier is the first sign he noticed.
- **2, Nobody Goes First.** Hamada eats alone at the Accounts table at lunch, still in his armband. After the apology he pushes his curry round the plate and doesn't eat it.
- **3, Seven Seats.** The truth ending brings the guard (and Tama) to the bar, the record ending brings Hamada, and the apology brings Hamada plus a beer from the guard. They're the people who can fill Mori's empty chairs.

## Staging needs (for the builder)

Hamada walking a route of stops with a point and a caption at each; the bowl as an item Eric carries; Tama following the carried bowl at a distance; a lift ride inside the lobby scene (down to B2 and back up) with the cat in the car; the red sticker and the visitor badge as small props on the bowl; the guard writing at the visitor counter at the start.
