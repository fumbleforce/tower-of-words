# The person at the desk

Kenji wants to cover B2's afternoon walk-in desk, but Mori keeps answering questions before he has finished asking them. Eric helps him rehearse, then chooses whether to support him from the desk or give him the room when a real visitor arrives.

## Playing it

The player sits on the customer side of a worktable, operates a mouse while Kenji tries to follow what they mean, and deliberately reproduces a small, funny problem on a screen. In the afternoon, a choice puts Eric either beside Kenji, handling demonstrations, or across the room with Mori, leaving Kenji to find his own way through a visitor's request. Both routes solve the visitor's problem; the choice changes how he learns, who Eric spends the scene with, and the working arrangement Kenji keeps.

This takes place entirely on B2. The cast is Eric, Kenji and Mori, with Mio translating a few necessary exchanges and Hamada visiting in the afternoon. The worktable becomes the temporary walk-in desk; the office, copy-room entrance and kitchenette remain freely connected. Nobody needs to enter the machine room.

The only new phrase is 見せて (misete, show me). It is a request to a willing person, with no magical effect. Eric uses familiar すみません (sumimasen, excuse me) if he wants Mori's attention. Japanese stays accompanied by English meaning, and later uses keep supported typing or voice until the existing practice requirement is met. All unlearned Japanese below is marked `overheard: true`; bracketed translations are for the writer and performer, never player-visible subtitles. No translations require a click. Stage directions are for staging, not narration cards.

## Full script

### Late morning: the other side

*[Available after Eric returns to B2. Kenji has put a spare keyboard and mouse on the worktable. Its monitor shows a photograph of a cat. A visitor chair faces it. Mori is standing at the corner with a pad. Nobody calls Eric over until the player approaches.]*

Kenji, bright: “Eric-san! Customer. Please?”

*[He pulls out the visitor chair, then notices that this leaves nowhere for Eric's knees. He moves the keyboard to his own side.]*

Eric: “Am I buying something?”

Kenji: “No buying. Practice customer. I am support.”

Eric: “I thought you already were.”

Kenji: “Yes! Yes. But customer comes, Mori-san is...”

*[He mimes an enormous welcoming bow. Mori returns an ordinary, courteous one.]*

Mori, `overheard: true`, polite: 「よろしくお願いします。」

*[The familiar greeting is clear. Kenji makes a small, defeated noise. Mio passes the doorway carrying her lunch box.]*

Mio: “He wants to take the walk-ins this afternoon. Mori-san said okay, but now he keeps helping.”

Kenji, to Mori, `overheard: true`: 「森さん、まだ練習始まってないです。」

*[For performance: “Mori-san, we haven't started practising yet.”]*

Mori, `overheard: true`, apologetic: 「ああ、すみません。どうぞ。」

*[For performance: “Ah, sorry. Go ahead.” He steps back half a pace.]*

Eric: “Does it have to be me?”

Mio: “If he can understand your problem, he should be okay with anyone.”

Kenji: “English also. Good practice.”

Mio: “I'm eating in the kitchen today, by the way. The floor is full of cables. Come get me if the practice customer becomes angry.”

*[She crosses to the kitchenette and sits. The conversation ends. Sitting in the visitor chair starts the rehearsal; walking away leaves it available. Kenji taps the seat once if spoken to before Eric sits.]*

Kenji, repeat interaction: “Customer chair. This one.”

*[Eric sits. Kenji straightens the keyboard, then his lanyard. The monitor and both men share the frame.]*

Kenji, careful: “Hello. What is problem?”

Eric: “This cat is too small.”

*[The player now controls the desktop photograph. One large button reads “Try the mouse wheel.” It enlarges the cat until only one eye fits on screen. There is no timing requirement.]*

Eric: “Now it's too big.”

Kenji: “Ah. Wheel. Other way.”

*[The player rolls it back with “Make the picture smaller.” The cat returns to its original size.]*

Eric: “Good. Can you make the buttons bigger too?”

*[Kenji reaches for the mouse. Mori reaches at the same time. Their hands stop short of it.]*

Mori, `overheard: true`: 「表示の設定ですね。ここから...」

*[For performance: “The display settings. From here...”]*

Kenji, `overheard: true`, a little louder: 「僕がやります。」

*[For performance: “I'll do it.”]*

*[Mori withdraws. Kenji opens a menu, changes his mind and closes it. He knows Eric is watching. He points at the photograph.]*

Kenji: “Buttons. Cat buttons?”

Eric: “The little ones across the top.”

Kenji: “Top... ah.”

*[He points to the cat's ears.]*

Eric: “We may need the angry-customer service.”

*[Kenji laughs despite himself, rises and calls through the open doorway.]*

Kenji, `overheard: true`: 「ミオさん、ちょっといいですか。」

*[For performance: “Mio-san, have you got a moment?” Mio brings her lunch box to the doorway.]*

Mio: “Already?”

Eric: “I want bigger buttons. He thinks I want a bigger cat.”

Mio: “Ah. You should show him.”

*[She addresses Kenji in Japanese, then Eric in English.]*

Mio, `overheard: true`: 「わかんないなら、見せてもらえば？」

*[For performance: “If you don't understand, why not ask him to show you?”]*

Kenji: “Show me. Ah, yes. 見せて (misete, show me).”

*[He slides the mouse back to Eric.]*

Mio: “That means ‘show me’. You can use that also, when we talk too much.”

Kenji, slowly: “見せて (misete, show me).”

*[The supported typing or voice prompt opens for **misete**, with the English meaning present. Eric addresses Kenji.]*

Eric: “見せて (misete, show me).”

*[Kenji immediately slides the mouse back to his own side. Both men look down at it.]*

Eric: “I think it's mine again.”

Mio: “Okay, you two can work that out.”

*[She returns to her lunch. The mouse remains between them. The player clicks the tiny toolbar above the cat, using a large accessible target over the same part of the screen. Kenji nods, changes the display size and makes the toolbar larger.]*

Kenji: “This?”

Eric: “That's it.”

*[Mori leans forward to look, then checks himself and stays where he is. Kenji notices.]*

Kenji, `overheard: true`, casual: 「森さん、見ていいですよ。」

*[For performance: “Mori-san, you can look.”]*

Mori, `overheard: true`, warm: 「ありがとうございます。」

*[For performance: “Thank you.” Mori comes around the table. He looks at the enlarged controls, then points at the cat.]*

Mori, `overheard: true`: 「これは、健司さんの猫ですか。」

*[For performance: “Is this your cat, Kenji?”]*

Kenji, `overheard: true`, pleased: 「いえ、動画の。森で拾われた猫で、最初はこんなに...」

*[For performance: “No, it's from a video. It was found in a forest, and at first it was only this...” He measures a tiny kitten between his palms.]*

*[The photograph changes to a video thumbnail. Kenji catches himself with the mouse poised over Play.]*

Kenji, to Eric: “Customer finished?”

Eric: “The customer is very happy with his buttons.”

*[Kenji turns the screen towards Mori and starts the video. Eric can stand and leave. This ends the morning scene without advancing the day on its own.]*

### Afternoon: someone has actually come

*[Available during the afternoon. Kenji sits at the worktable. The cat window is closed. Mori has the visitor pad at his own desk. Hamada steps out of the lift carrying a laptop in a padded sleeve. He pauses at the office doorway until the player approaches the worktable or speaks to him.]*

Hamada, `overheard: true`, tentative: 「すみません。ちょっと、教えていただきたいんですが。」

*[For performance: “Excuse me. Could someone show me something?”]*

*[Mori stands. Kenji also stands, catches his knee on the underside of the table and sits back down. The laptop stays safely in Hamada's hands.]*

Mori, `overheard: true`: 「浜田さん、どうされました？」

*[For performance: “Mr. Hamada, what can we do for you?”]*

Kenji, `overheard: true`: 「こっち、空いてます。」

*[For performance: “There's a seat here.” He holds the visitor chair away from the table. Hamada looks from Mori to Kenji. Mio looks up from her desk.]*

Mio: “Kenji, let him sit down first, okay?”

*[Hamada sits. Mori brings the pad over and starts to put it on the worktable. Kenji looks at Eric.]*

Kenji, low: “Together? You and me?”

*[Mio comes close enough to hear.]*

Mio: “You can sit with him, if you like. Or I can tell Mori-san to give him some room. He keeps saying he'll let him do it, then he sees someone needs help and...”

*[Mori has already uncapped his pen.]*

#### Player choice

- “I'll sit with Kenji. He can do the talking.” Follow A.
- “I'll take Mori-san for tea. Let Kenji try.” Follow B.

The choice stays open indefinitely. Both routes complete the visit. There is no approval score for choosing one over the other.

### A: two people behind the table

Eric: “I'll sit with Kenji. He can do the talking.”

Mio, `overheard: true`: 「健司が聞いて、エリックが画面を見るそうです。森さん、今日はこの二人に任せませんか。」

*[For performance: “Kenji will hear him out, and Eric will look at the screen. Mori-san, shall we leave it to these two today?”]*

*[Mori hands Kenji the pad, puts the pen on top and returns to his own desk. Mio stays beside the table for the first explanation. Eric pulls a spare chair alongside Kenji.]*

Kenji, to Hamada, `overheard: true`, polite: 「どうしました？」

Hamada, `overheard: true`: 「今夜、娘と話すんですが、この顔を消したくて。声だけでいいんです。」

*[For performance: “I'm talking to my daughter tonight, and I'd like to get rid of this face. Just the voice is enough.” He opens the laptop. His face fills its camera preview.]*

Mio: “He's calling his daughter tonight. He wants the camera off.”

Eric: “Does he know we can see him here too?”

Mio: “Mm, she lives abroad. I think he just wants to talk without checking his face all the time.”

*[Kenji points to the camera button, then to Eric, inviting him to demonstrate. Mio returns to her desk. The player clicks the camera button. Hamada's preview becomes his initials.]*

Kenji: “Camera off. Voice, okay.”

*[Hamada leans towards the initials and clears his throat. The microphone meter jumps. He laughs, points at the camera icon and asks Kenji something.]*

Hamada, `overheard: true`: 「ここですね。もう一度、お願いします。」

*[For performance: “It's here, then. Once more, please.”]*

Kenji, to Eric: “Again, please.”

*[The player switches the camera on and off again. Hamada follows Eric's finger, but leaves his own hands folded. Kenji notices, then turns the whole laptop to face Hamada squarely.]*

Kenji, `overheard: true`, gentle: 「今度は浜田さんが。僕、触りませんから。」

*[For performance: “Your turn now, Mr. Hamada. I won't touch it.”]*

*[Kenji puts both hands under his thighs. Eric follows his example automatically. Hamada looks at the pair of them, amused, and operates the camera himself. On, then off.]*

Hamada, `overheard: true`, relieved: 「ああ、できました。ありがとうございます。」

*[For performance: “Ah, I've done it. Thank you.”]*

*[He shuts the laptop, stands and bows to Kenji first, then Eric. Kenji stands late, bumps the table with his knees again, and steadies the visitor pad. Hamada leaves through the corridor towards the lift.]*

Kenji: “You are fast. I am slow English, but I see his hands.”

Eric: “I would have kept pressing it for him.”

Kenji: “Yes. You like buttons.”

*[He grins, then points to Eric's chair.]*

Kenji: “Next customer, this chair okay? When you are here.”

Eric: “When I'm here.”

*[Kenji leaves the chair beside his. Continue to the closing scene.]*

### B: the kitchenette

Eric: “I'll take Mori-san for tea. Let Kenji try.”

Mio, `overheard: true`: 「森さん、エリックがお茶をご一緒したいそうです。浜田さんは健司に任せて。」

*[For performance: “Mori-san, Eric would like to have tea with you. Leave Mr. Hamada to Kenji.”]*

*[Mori looks at Eric. Eric gestures to the kitchenette. Mori considers the uncapped pen, caps it and gives the pad to Kenji.]*

Mori, `overheard: true`, formal: 「お願いします。困ったら、呼んでください。」

*[For performance: “Please take care of him. Call if you need me.”]*

*[The player walks with Mori to the kitchenette. The camera follows Eric throughout. Kenji and Hamada remain visible across the corridor. These lines are heard on the way, with the usual blurred Japanese captions.]*

Kenji, `overheard: true`, polite: 「どうしました？」

*[For performance: “What seems to be the problem?”]*

Hamada, `overheard: true`: 「今夜、娘と話すんですが、この顔を消したくて。声だけでいいんです。」

*[For performance: “I'm talking to my daughter tonight, and I'd like to get rid of this face. Just the voice is enough.” He opens his laptop and gestures at the camera preview.]*

Kenji, `overheard: true`: 「ミオさん、カメラを切っても、声は聞こえますよね？」

*[For performance: “Mio-san, if we turn the camera off, they can still hear him, right?”]*

Mio, `overheard: true`: 「うん。マイクのボタンはそのままで。」

*[For performance: “Yes. Leave the microphone button alone.” She points once from her desk. Kenji nods and lets Hamada try. The camera stays with Eric.]*

*[Mori takes two cups from the rack. One has a tea bag already waiting in it. He offers that one, thinks again and takes a fresh cup for Eric.]*

Eric: “Was that yours?”

*[Mori taps his own chest, points towards the office and spreads his palms. He has not managed to pour it yet. He finally fills both cups.]*

Eric: “Right. Sit down before somebody else comes.”

*[The player takes a seat. Mori sits opposite. In the office, Hamada's camera preview disappears. He laughs. Mori half turns in his chair. Eric can get his attention with familiar すみません (sumimasen, excuse me), or tap Mori's cup and point at his own. Both actions simply bring him back to the tea.]*

*[Mori lifts his cup and drinks. A quiet playable pause: Eric can drink, look towards the doorway or stand. No timer advances the scene. Interacting with the teapot lets Mori offer a refill; accepting or declining is a nod or raised palm, with no extra dialogue. The next beat starts when Eric chooses to stand.]*

*[Hamada appears in the doorway with his laptop open. Kenji comes beside him, keeping clear of the screen.]*

Kenji: “Eric-san. Customer test.”

Eric: “Me again?”

Kenji: “No, no. Hamada-san.”

*[Kenji gestures for Eric to ask. The available phrase remains fully glossed.]*

Eric: “見せて (misete, show me).”

*[Hamada demonstrates. His camera preview is on. He clicks its button, and the preview becomes his initials. He speaks, and the microphone meter still moves. He does it once more, smiling at Mori.]*

Kenji: “Tonight, daughter. Camera no. Talking yes.”

Eric: “He just wants the voice?”

Kenji: “Yes. Camera is...”

*[Kenji pats his hair, checks his collar and sucks in his cheeks, imitating Hamada fussing over his own preview. Hamada recognises himself and laughs. Kenji stops the imitation before it becomes unkind.]*

Mori, `overheard: true`, warm: 「なるほど。ゆっくりお話しできますね。」

*[For performance: “I see. Now you can have a relaxed conversation.”]*

*[Hamada bows to Kenji, closes the laptop and walks to the lift. Mori carries his own cup to the sink. He leaves the second half of the pot for Kenji.]*

Kenji, low, to Eric: “One time, I don't know. I ask Mio-san. Then okay.”

Eric: “Did he mind?”

Kenji: “No. He is talking about daughter. Very much talking.”

*[He opens the visitor pad to show a single line of notes, then shuts it without expecting Eric to read the Japanese.]*

Kenji: “My next customer can be quiet.”

*[Continue to the closing scene.]*

## Connections

Kenji's enthusiasm, limited English and forest-cat distraction continue from day 1, as does Mori's habit of helping through small practical acts. The subplot starts only after Eric's station visit, but does not assume how that repair went or which colleague he ate with yesterday. It leaves the afternoon and evening free for the other two threads; its payoff is completed here, without requiring a future quest.
