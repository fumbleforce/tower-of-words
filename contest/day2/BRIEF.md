# Writing contest: day 2 sub-plots

Jørgen is comparing Claude and Codex as creative writers. The winner leads creative writing for the game from now on. Both get this brief and nothing else from the organiser.

## Task

Write three sub-plots for day 2, each with its full dialogue. A sub-plot is a thread the player can follow through the day: a person, a problem, a want, at least one real choice, and a payoff (on day 2 or clearly set up for later). The three should differ from each other in cast, tone and what the player does.

For each sub-plot:

1. Title and a two-sentence pitch.
2. Why it's fun to play: what the player does with their hands (walk, talk, choose, use a Japanese command), not only what they read.
3. Cast and places. Use the day-1 cast and places where it helps; new people and places on the island are allowed (GUIDE's setting).
4. The words or commands it teaches or uses. At most two new ones per sub-plot, taught the way day 1 teaches (GUIDE, Learning rules; game3d/story/FORMAT.md).
5. The full script: every line of dialogue, choices and their branches, stage directions and gestures. Overheard Japanese is marked as in day 1.
6. How it ties to day 1 and to the other two sub-plots, if it does.

Length: whatever the story needs; roughly 1,500 to 3,000 words per sub-plot.

## Rules

- Write in plain English text in markdown, one file per sub-plot: `contest/day2/<you>/1.md`, `2.md`, `3.md`, plus `notes.md` for anything else (skills used, what you'd do next). Don't write game code.
- Don't open the other contestant's folder, before or after you finish. Don't read each other's inbox messages about the contest.
- You may find and install any creative-writing or game-design skills you think will help, and must name them in notes.md.
- Nothing is committed to git until Jørgen has judged.
- When done: Claude's entry reports to the organiser; Codex appends a `done` entry to collab/to-claude.md.

## Judging

Jørgen judges blind in the bible's Review section: the entries appear as Writer A and Writer B, and who is who is revealed after he sends his verdict.
