# 2. Nobody Goes First

## Pitch

Every Friday Kaori makes too much curry, rings a bell at 12:40 for free seconds, and watches a canteen full of polite people each wait for somebody else to go up first, until the pot goes in the bin. Kenji is broke until payday and would rather go hungry than be the first one out of his chair, and the only person in the hall who doesn't know he's supposed to wait is Eric.

## Why it's fun to play

- A new place with its own rules: a 1989 meal ticket machine, a serving counter, long tables that each belong to a department, and a hatch where you hand your tray back.
- The main choice is made with the player's feet. When the bell rings, nobody moves, and the player can walk up to the counter, talk Kenji into going, or take his tray back like everyone else. There's no menu until he has already moved.
- The first time Eric says おかわり, he says it to Kaori with his plate held out, so learning the word and using it are the same act. ごちそうさまでした goes to her at the tray hatch, where she keeps track of who says it.
- 外人 from day 1 comes back with an edge. In one ending a man at the Sales table mutters about Eric, and the only word the player can read is 外人.

## Cast and places

- **Kenji** (day 1). 21, two months in, keen, broke until the 25th. Loud in English, which he can barely speak, and shy in Japanese, which he can. He has eaten in this canteen every day for two months and never said a word to the cook.
- **Kaori** (new; approved design). 44, runs the canteen kitchen. Tall, short grey-streaked hair, chef jacket, a towel over her shoulder, an old faded tattoo on one forearm that nobody mentions. What she cares about is waste: she writes the kilos of leftovers in a small notebook every day, and Fridays are always the worst. Casual, loud, short Japanese. No English, except one number.
- **The hall.** The Sales table by the window (a section chief and his juniors), the old men from building maintenance, the young people's corner, and Mr. Hamada eating alone at the Accounts table.
- Places: the canteen (new), the corridor behind the kitchen, B2 in the afternoon.

## Words

- **おかわり** (okawari, seconds, another helping). Kenji whispers it when Kaori calls it across the hall; Eric types it at the counter, to her. It also works at Jun's bar in the evening (sub-plot 3), where it gets another beer.
- **ごちそうさまでした** (gochisōsama deshita, thank you for the meal). From Kenji at the tray hatch. Said to Kaori, and to Jun when leaving the bar.
- Already known and used: 外人 (read, not said), 動いて (optional, on the ticket machine).

## How to read the script

`Name:` is a line in English. `Name (overheard):` is Japanese Eric can't follow; the italic line under it is for the reader, not the player. `>` is narration. Square brackets are staging. `{okawari}` shows the word with reading and English. `[type: ...]` is the typing prompt.

## The script

### A. B2, 12:00

[Kenji rolls over to Eric's desk on his chair. It squeaks: it's still the broken one.]

**Kenji:** Eric-san! Lunch? Today is Friday, so canteen is curry. Every Friday is curry. Is like a rule.

**Kenji:** And rice is free, many times. This is very important for me, because payday is twenty-five, and today is two. Only two. Pshh.

**Kenji:** In September I buy a new chair, for gaming. It is on a boat now, I think.

[If Eric had lunch with Mio on day 1, she said he could eat in the machine room again, and that offer stands.]

- **"Sure, let's go."** → B.
- **"I said I'd eat with Mio."** (only if he ate with her on day 1) → **Kenji:** Ah, machine room. Very quiet there. Okay, okay! [He rolls away.] (Kenji goes alone; see J.)
- **"Maybe next week."** → **Kenji:** Next week also curry! Every week curry. [He goes alone; see J.]

### B. The walk

[From B2 up to the plaza and across to the canteen. If Eric learned 来て in sub-plot 1 he can use it on the lift, and Kenji is delighted: "Eh, fast! Lucky lift." These lines play while walking:]

**Kenji:** Canteen is very big. Everybody on the island eat here, two hundred people maybe.

**Kenji:** Every department has a table. Sales by the window, because Sales. Old guys from maintenance in the middle. Young people in the corner, because there nobody is kachō. Kachō is... small boss.

### C. The ticket machine

[Just inside the doors: a big grey machine with rows of buttons, each with a handwritten paper strip in a little window.]

**Kenji:** First, ticket. You buy ticket, you give ticket to Kaori-san, you get food. Easy!

[The menu as the machine shows it. Japanese with its reading, English under everything:]

- カレー (karē), curry. ¥380
- カツカレー (katsu karē), curry with a pork cutlet. ¥500
- 日替わり定食 (higawari teishoku), today's set, grilled mackerel. ¥550
- きつねうどん (kitsune udon), udon with fried tofu. ¥330
- ライス (raisu), a plate of rice. ¥100

**Kenji:** I always take curry. Katsu curry is for people with payday.

[Eric picks. Then Kenji feeds in his coins one at a time, counting under his breath. He presses カレー. The button doesn't light.]

**Kenji:** Ah. No, no, no...

[He slaps the side of the machine, on a dent exactly the size of a fist. Nothing.]

**Kenji:** It eat coins sometimes. It like some coins, I don't know which. Pshh.

- **Say 動いて to the ticket machine** → [The kotodama effect. The カレー button lights, and a ticket drops into the tray.] **Kenji:** Eh?! It never work when I hit it! ...Eric-san, you have lucky hands.
- **Give Kenji a hundred yen** → [¥100 from Eric's wallet. Kenji feeds it in and the button lights.] **Kenji:** No, no, you are the new one, I should... Okay. I pay you back on the twenty-fifth. With interest. One melon soda.
- **Leave it** → [Kenji looks at the machine for a while, then counts what he has left and presses ライス.] **Kenji:** Rice. One hundred yen. Is okay! Rice is the main thing. Curry is only... decoration.

### D. The counter

[Kaori takes the tickets at a long steel counter, tears each one in half and hands over a plate. She looks at Eric for a second longer than at anyone else. She puts his plate down and fans her mouth with her hand.]

**Kaori (overheard):** はい、カレー。辛いよ。
*Means: here, curry. It's spicy. Clear: カレー (karē, curry).*

[Kenji holds his ticket out without looking up. Kaori takes it and hands him his plate without a word. He says nothing either.]

### E. The tables

[Kenji heads for the far corner. On the way:]

- **The Accounts table:** Mr. Hamada, alone. If sub-plot 1 happened, he's still wearing the yellow armband. He half stands and bows, sits, and bows again sitting down. (If he lied on his form, he's pushing his curry round the plate and hasn't eaten any.)
- **The Sales table:** full. Nobody looks up.
- **The corner:** Kenji puts his tray down. "Here! Young people table."

[They eat. If Eric bought the katsu curry, Kenji keeps looking at the cutlet.]

- **Give Kenji a piece of katsu** → **Kenji:** Eh? Really? You are very good man. [He eats it in one bite.]
- **Eat it** → [Kenji watches every piece go.]

**Kenji:** In Norway, you eat curry?

**Eric:** Not like this. We eat a lot of fish.

**Kenji:** Fish! Salmon! Norway salmon is very famous here, in sushi. Before, Japanese people don't eat salmon sushi, and then Norway come and say, please, try, try... and now everybody eat it. Is true, I see on YouTube.

**Eric:** I didn't know we did that.

**Kenji:** So is your fault. Thank you.

[Kenji has finished in about three minutes. He glances at the big pot on the counter, and away again.]

**Kenji:** After twelve-forty, the curry is free also. Kaori-san ring a bell.

### F. 12:40, the bell

[Kaori comes round the counter with a small brass hand bell and rings it.]

**Kaori (overheard, loud):** カレー、まだあるよー！おかわり、どうぞー！
*Means: there's still curry! Seconds, help yourselves! Clear: カレー.*

[She holds up an empty plate and taps the rim with her ladle. Then she goes back behind the pot and waits.]

[Nobody gets up. People go on eating, a little more slowly, eyes on their plates. At the Sales table a young man glances at the section chief beside him. The chief looks into his tea.]

**Kenji:** (whispering) Okawari. It means more, another one. You go up and say, okawari.

**Kenji:** (slow, still whispering) {okawari}...

**Kenji:** But look. Nobody go first. If you go first, you look greedy, so everybody wait for somebody else. And then Kaori-san throw it away. Every Friday, same.

[Kaori takes a small notebook out of her apron pocket and looks at it.]

[Goal: "Kaori is offering seconds." Markers on Kaori, on Kenji, and on the tray hatch.]

### G. The choice

What the player does next decides it:

- **Walk up to Kaori with your plate** → **Eric goes first**.
- **Talk to Kenji** →
  - **"You've been coming here two months. You go first."** → **Kenji goes first**.
  - **"Come up with me."** → **Eric goes first**, with Kenji right behind him.
  - **"Let's just leave it."** → **Nobody goes**.
- **Take your tray to the hatch** → **Nobody goes**.

#### Eric goes first

[Eric stands. His chair scrapes, and the tables around go quiet. He walks to the counter with his plate held out. Kaori looks at it, and at him, ladle up, waiting.]

[type: okawari, prompt: "> Kaori is waiting, ladle up."]

[Eric says it. Kaori grins, for the first time today, and fills the plate higher than it was the first time.]

**Kaori (overheard):** いいね、あんた。
*Means: you're alright, you.*

[Behind Eric a chair scrapes. Kenji is on his feet with his plate in both hands, second in line. Then the maintenance men, then a young man from Sales, then another. When Eric turns round there's a queue.]

[If Eric said "Come up with me": as she ladles, Kaori looks past Eric at Kenji, the first time she has looked straight at him. Kenji stares at his plate.]

[On the way back to the corner, Eric passes the Sales table. The section chief says, not quietly:]

**Sales chief (overheard):** 外人は遠慮しないねえ。
*Means: foreigners don't hold back, do they. Reads: 外人 sharp, the rest blurred.*

**Young Sales man (overheard):** でも、ほら、今のうちですよ。
*Means: well, but, now's our chance.*

[The young man gets up and joins the queue. After a moment, so does the chief.]

[Back at the table, Kenji sits down behind a mountain of curry.]

- **"What did the man at the Sales table say?"** →
  - **Kenji:** Ah... he say...
  - [Kenji looks at his curry.]
  - **Kenji:** He say, gaijin is very brave. Something like this.
  - **Eric:** That's what I thought.
- **Eat** → [Kenji eats as if someone might take it back.]

(Kaori's bond goes up, Kenji ate, and at the Sales table Eric is now the foreigner who doesn't hold back.)

#### Kenji goes first

**Eric:** You've been coming here two months. You go first.

**Kenji:** Me? No, no. Eric-san, I am new!

**Eric:** Not any more. I'm new.

[Kenji looks at his empty plate. At the pot. At Eric.]

**Kenji:** ...Okay.

[He stands. His chair scrapes, and the tables around look up. He walks to the counter holding his plate out in both hands.]

**Kenji (overheard, very small):** お、おかわり…お願いします。
*Means: s-seconds… please. All blurred, since Eric hasn't typed おかわり yet, but the player has just heard Kenji whisper it and can follow.*

[Kaori looks at him for a long moment.]

**Kaori (overheard):** …あんた、毎日来てる子だよね。
*Means: …you're the kid who comes in every day, aren't you.*

**Kenji (overheard):** は、はい！
*Means: y-yes!*

**Kaori (overheard):** 名前は？
*Means: what's your name?*

**Kenji (overheard):** ケ、ケンジです！
*Means: K-Kenji! Clear: ケンジ.*

**Kaori (overheard):** ケンジね。
*Means: Kenji. Right.*

[She fills his plate to the edge. Behind him a chair scrapes, then another, and a queue forms behind Kenji. He walks back past it with his plate held up in front of him.]

**Kenji:** Eric-san. I am first penguin.

**Eric:** First what?

**Kenji:** First penguin! In Japanese we say this. All the penguins stand on the ice and nobody want to jump, because maybe there is a shark. Then one jump, and everybody jump. The first one is very brave, or eaten by the shark.

**Kenji:** Today, shark is not here. Lucky!

[Eric can join the queue now, as one more person in it. At the counter: [type: okawari, prompt: "> Kaori has the ladle ready."] and a plain refill.]

(Kenji's bond goes up a lot, and Kaori knows his name. Kaori barely notices Eric.)

#### Nobody goes

[If Eric said "Let's just leave it":]

**Kenji:** Mm. Yes. Is normal.

[They pick up their trays. Nobody moves. At 13:00 Kaori puts the bell down, lifts the pot in both arms and carries it into the kitchen. The scrape of a ladle on the bottom of a pot, then a bin lid. She takes out the notebook and writes.]

[If Eric took his tray to the hatch instead, Kenji sighs, picks up his own tray and follows him; the pot goes in the bin behind them.]

(Eric doesn't type おかわり today; he heard it but never used it.)

### H. The tray hatch (every ending)

[A window into the kitchen with a steel shelf. Kaori is at the sink behind it. Kenji puts his tray down, puts his palms together and bows his head toward the kitchen.]

**Kenji (overheard):** ごちそうさまでした！
*Means: thank you for the meal!*

[In "Kenji goes first" he says it loud enough for half the hall. In the others it's a mumble.]

**Kenji:** When you give back the tray, you say this. Gochisōsama deshita. Is like "thank you for the food". Kaori-san hear everybody. She know who say it and who don't.

**Kenji:** (slow) {gochiso}...

[type: gochiso, prompt: "> Kenji puts his palms together again, and waits for you to do the same."]

[Eric says it at the hatch. Kaori's answer depends on the ending:]

- **Eric went first:** [Kaori dries her hands, takes the notebook out and holds it up to the hatch: 11 kg, crossed out, and under it, 2. She taps the 2.] **Kaori:** Two. **Kaori (overheard):** また来週ね。 *Means: see you next week.*
- **Kenji went first:** [She's still looking at Kenji. She lifts a soapy hand an inch at Eric.] **Kaori (overheard):** はいよ。 *Means: yep.*
- **Nobody went:** [She doesn't look up from the sink.] **Kaori (overheard):** はいよー。

### I. Coda, if nobody went

[Eric and Kenji leave by the corridor behind the kitchen, the short way to the lifts. A door opens. Kaori steps out with a small foil package and pushes it into Kenji's hands.]

**Kaori (overheard):** ほら、持ってきな。
*Means: here, take this.*

**Kaori (overheard):** あんた、毎週あの席から鍋見てるでしょ。
*Means: you sit in that seat and stare at the pot every week, don't you.*

[She goes back in. Kenji looks at the package: two rice balls, still warm.]

**Kenji:** ...She know my seat.

(The custom holds and the curry went in the bin, but Kaori has noticed Kenji, and Eric saw it.)

### J. B2, the afternoon

[One beat, the first time Eric talks to Kenji after lunch:]

- **Kenji went first:** [Kenji is telling Mori all about it, standing up, with a penguin waddle between the desks.] **Kenji (overheard):** 森さん！今日、ファーストペンギンになりました！ *Means: Mori-san! Today I became the first penguin! Clear: ファーストペンギン (fāsuto pengin, first penguin).* [Mori laughs and claps three small claps.] If Mio is at her desk: **Mio:** He's going to say this all week. Maybe all year.
- **Eric went first:** **Kenji:** Next Friday, I go first. Maybe. ...Maybe the Friday after.
- **Nobody went:** [Kenji eats one rice ball at his desk. Without looking up, he puts the other beside Eric's keyboard.]
- **Eric ate with Mio, or skipped lunch:** **Kenji:** Today also nobody take okawari. Pshh. (Eric doesn't learn either word today.)

## How it ties in

- **Day 1.** Kenji's broken chair and his "So now I am not the newest", which Eric hands back to him ("Not any more. I'm new."). Kenji's forest cats on YouTube become Norwegian salmon on YouTube. 外人, which Mio said was "like saying the new guy", comes back from someone who doesn't mean it that way. The vending machine that stuck on day 1 has an older cousin in the ticket machine.
- **1, There Is No Cat.** Hamada at the Accounts table, still wearing his armband, and in one ending unable to eat. If Eric has 来て, he can call the lift on the way.
- **3, Seven Seats.** おかわり gets another beer from Jun at the bar, and ごちそうさまでした is what Eric says to him on the way out. Kenji's penguin story gets told again at the party, in Japanese, with the waddle. If Eric went first, Kenji pours for him all evening.

## Staging needs (for the builder)

The canteen as a place: ticket machine by the door, the steel counter with Kaori and a big pot, long tables by department (about thirty people reads as a full hall), a tray hatch into the kitchen, a back corridor. Kaori's body (approved portrait exists). At the bell, people keep eating but slower, eyes down; then, after whoever goes first, people stand one after another to form a queue.
