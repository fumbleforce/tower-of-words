Images
Character portraits, expressions and backgrounds were made with AI image tools and then edited by the team.

Image control patches: Anima-LLLite by kohya-ss. Upscaling: Real-ESRGAN (BSD-3-Clause). Cut-outs: anime-segmentation by SkyTNT (Apache-2.0).
[CHECK] Scenery and the Photos collectibles: One Obsession by maxfeifei8 (Civitai).
One travel print was made with OpenAI image generation.

3D characters and animation
Character models, rigs and animation clips were made with Meshy.
[CHECK] Model created with Meshy. (https://www.meshy.ai)
World pieces, rooms, props, extra characters and many gestures are built in code for this game.

Voices
Every voice is synthetic. No real person was recorded or imitated.
Voices were designed from written descriptions and produced with Qwen3-TTS (Alibaba Cloud Qwen team, Apache-2.0) and Irodori-TTS by Aratako (MIT).
[CHECK] A few station and building announcement voices were first made with MiniMax Speech.

Music and sound
Background music made with Google Lyria. Audio carries a SynthID watermark. [CHECK]
Ambience beds and some effects made with Stable Audio 3 by Stability AI (Stability AI Community License). [CHECK]
[CHECK] Opening theme and the evening track made with YuE2 (CC BY-NC 4.0).
Other effects are synthesised for this game.
