# The island beyond B2

Design doc from the relationships agent, 2026-09-28, revised the same day after Jørgen's note on the frame. Nothing here is approved or built. Day 1 comes first (GUIDE, Scope). This doc covers the machines Eric keeps coming back to, the places, how they open, and what a day, a week and the half-year feel like. The people are in notes/RELATIONSHIPS.md.

## The idea

Eric is a support engineer, hired to keep the island's ancient systems running because the company won't replace anything that still works. It's the chairman's old rule, and half the vendors went out of business years ago. The old machines answer to his voice. Nobody explains why, and nobody investigates. IT tickets send him everywhere: a ticket is a job and a day pass, and it puts him in front of a machine and the people who depend on it. He comes back to the same machines all game, and every new word, command or bond gives him more to do with them. The oldest districts have the most machines. In the newer, more polite ones, Japanese does more of the work than commands, which also follows GUIDE's order of casual first, polite and keigo later.

### How a machine grows over the game

1. **Every command and word works on every machine**, and each gives a different result. Trying one is an experiment, and the machine's card in the phone records what happened. The copier across the game:

| When | What he can do at the copier |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | 動いて wakes it |
| Week 1 | 出して prints; 止まって clears a jam |
| Week 3 | 全部 + 出して empties the paper tray across the floor. ゆっくり + 出して gives clean copies of Kanae's fragile negatives. |
| Week 5 | 動かないで holds a sheet mid-feed so Sumi can line up a certificate. 休んで at night means no jam in the morning. |
| Week 8 | Mori's binder: open the side panel first, and 止まって then 動いて clears in one go (a two-word sequence) |
| Later | 回って on its cooling fan in summer; もう一回 for the council's last-minute reprints |

2. **The keeper changes the result.** With Mori standing there, the copier does things it won't do for Eric alone. With Kenji there, the vending machine gets generous.
3. **Jobs bring people.** Machines do work for people, so a visit to a machine is also a visit to someone's story: the copier is printing Emi's council case, the fax is carrying Rei's month-end.
4. **Keepers open the casing** at bond step 3: the quirk, the manual, a spare part.
5. **The logs become readable.** The log inside each machine is gibberish at first, like overheard talk, and gets clearer as he learns words. The entries hold quirks, old engineers' jokes, and names of people still on the island.
6. **Seasons and load change them.** The boiler in winter, the fax at month-end, the PA before the festival.
7. **Machines remember.** A well-kept machine does small favours (lift 3 waits for him, the vending machine drops a can on Mondays). A neglected one gets tetchy and its tickets escalate.

## Stakes over a run of days

- **Escalation ladders.** A fault shows a symptom, then threatens someone's plan, then reaches a dated event. The plaza PA hums in the first week of October, drops out in the third, shows yesterday's feed the week before the festival, and on festival day goes live in whatever state Eric left it.
- **The weekly due item.** Monday's newsletter prints the week's events, and one of them always needs a machine by Friday: the council papers, the sports-day PA, Kanae's print run, the ekiden trial. Whose it is decides who he spends the week near.
- **Dated events happen anyway.** Bonds decide Eric's part in them, not whether they happen (RELATIONSHIPS.md).
- **The audit** in December (Emi's arc): an auditor shadows him for a week and wants every fix explained, so what he learned the slow way (binders, logs, keepers) is what carries him.
- **The big nights** need many machines and people at once: festival day, Mori's yearly check in January, and the February storm. In the storm the pump station and the rooftop fans have to hold together, Goro's hives are on the roof, Mio's night build is running in the machine room, and the dorm boiler is all that keeps Kaori's last bath warm.
- **When he misses something**, the event goes ahead with less, the person reacts, and the machine is there to try again tomorrow.

The staff council (社員会) runs the island's small budgets (canteen, dorms, events, spare parts) and votes once a month. It has three groups. The old guard is Kiyoko's faction, which wants seniority respected and nothing changed without reason. The reformers are head office's people, who want everything measured, flexible hours and English in meetings. The basement is Kenji, whose single promise is free drinks from the B2 vending machine.

## How places open up

| Key | How it works |
|---|---|
| Tickets | A ticket is a day pass to where the job is. |
| Badge lines | After his first ticket in a district, plus one person there at bond step 2, Nanami adds the district to his card. |
| People | Keys, invitations and machine casings from people at step 3. |
| Commands | Old doors, lifts and gates answer to him. The service tunnels under every district open to 開けて and are the fast way around. |
| Standing | Machines kept running and people talking about it lead to a staff offer (正社員 as 主任) at New Year. Legal and the executive floors need it. |

No gate asks him to recall a word (GUIDE). What he knows decides how much a place gives him: the production floor is noise until he has some words, and the bar's talk and the machines' logs give up more each month.

## Kinds of tickets

The B2 whiteboard has three to five each morning. More turn up in the world: someone flags him down, a phone rings in the canteen, a note is left on his tray.

| Kind | The choice inside it |
|---|---|
| Fault | By hand (slow, safe) or by command (fast, someone might see) |
| Escalation | Fix it now, or deal with the bigger version before the event |
| Round (weekly) | Short. Where favours, quirks and keepers turn up. |
| Parts hunt | A part nobody makes, found through people across the island |
| Event | Dated and public, with a crowd watching |
| Night call | Night places and night people |

## A day and a week

A day is 10 to 15 minutes, one train ride, using the periods in game3d/js/sim.js: the commute (one overheard exchange; Mio on Thursdays), the morning (greetings, the board, a ticket or two), lunch (the choice of company), the afternoon (tickets across the island, people at their open times), the evening (one social place, or home), and the night (dorm and save, sometimes a night call). Pacing is in RELATIONSHIPS.md.

| Day | What's different |
|---|---|
| Monday | Biggest board. The newsletter prints the week's due item and hooks. |
| Tuesday | Council day when there's a vote |
| Wednesday | Round day. Mio leaves for her mother's after work. |
| Thursday | Mio on the morning train with pickles |
| Friday | The team drinks (飲み会); the due item is due |
| Saturday | No tickets unless there's a night call. Beach, arcade, sports ground, weekend classes. |
| Sunday | Quiet. Laundry, one person at a time; later, trips off the island. |

## The half-year

Eric's contract runs from 1 October to 31 March, the second half of the Japanese fiscal year.

| Month | On the island |
|---|---|
| October | Day 1 on the 1st, also the 内定式 for next year's hires. The plaza PA starts to hum. Sports day. The first council vote. General Affairs and the sports ground open. |
| November | The anniversary festival on the plaza, live on island TV. The dorm boiler starts sulking. |
| December | Nanami's hearing, the audit, year-end parties (忘年会). The production halls open. Trips off the island begin. |
| January | The New Year ekiden on the 1st. The staff offer, which opens Legal. Mori's yearly check. Goro shows him the pumps and tunnels. |
| February | The storm night. The executive floors open through Kiyoko. The romance beats land by the end of the month. |
| March | Kanae's anniversary issue, Mio's claim, transfers and retirements. Each story ends its own way. |

## What this asks of the build, later

Only once day 1 is stable. The places are flat-shaded rooms like day 1's, joined by continuous transitions (GUIDE), and the tunnels and the monorail do much of that. Schedules, open times, gifts, bonds and taught commands already exist in sim.js. The new pieces are machine state (health, results per word, favours), readable logs, the ticket board with escalation ladders, badge lines, the calendar and the council vote. The parked room generator in TODO.md could help with repeated spaces.
