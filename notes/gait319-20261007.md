# Gait diagnostic cadence, 7 October 2026

Issue #319 remains open. This change corrects a reproduced free-walking measurement error; it does not change animation clips, actor scheduling, movement speeds or collision behavior.

The original strict day-one Eric failure is retained at `game3d/shots/fast/2026-10-07T14-07-52-899Z-3073196`. It reported an office slide for 4.8 seconds. A repeated full route passing did not explain it.

## Reproduction and cause

`game3d/tools/gait319-check.mjs` records the actual native player's root, feet, active mixer actions and free-walker state after simulation updates, alongside drawn-frame samples. Both clocks are kept in the raw JSON. A normal baseline completed without the long office warning; Chromium CPU throttling at four times slower reproduced it repeatedly.

In the stressed baseline, during game time 357–362 seconds, 105 free-walk simulation samples yield a planted-root/foot distance ratio of 1.08443. Eleven drawn samples of the same motion yield 11.8162. Their median intervals are 0.05 and 0.4004 game seconds. The sparse samples skip whole stride cycles. The walk action remains enabled, unpaused and at full weight. The old warning's `gait off` text describes stale scripted-gait bookkeeping; free movement uses the walker's gait state.

The first attempted correction sampled every actor after every main simulation step. Its retained stress run exposed incompatible clocks: scripted `walkRig` movement runs on animation frames, and wrapped Meshy cast poses update when rendered. Measuring their old pose between renders is also misleading. That attempt was rejected.

The final correction samples only the free player after a completed main step. Scripted players, named cast, crowd and ambient actors keep the existing drawn-frame sampling. Each actor records its own timestamp and cadence; changing cadence, parent or place starts a new measurement window. Swimming and seated exclusions remain, with dry sliding and submerged pair collisions still checked. All numeric failure thresholds are unchanged. The existing coarse-sampling tolerance applies only to drawn samples; floating-point roundoff at a 0.05-second main step cannot select it.

The dual-cadence stress run has no long free-player slide episode. Its drawn samples still alias: in the same 357–362 interval, the simulation ratio is 1.03218 and the drawn ratio is 25.2798. Other stressed cast/crowd episodes remain recorded. This is not a frame-rate improvement or proof that those actors' warnings are harmless.
