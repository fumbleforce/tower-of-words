## Routine and clock

Wake in room 203, optionally check the PC, walk to work or the board, choose how to spend the day, then return and choose Sleep. Use four existing periods: morning at work ("Morning" on weekends), lunch, afternoon, after work ("Evening"). Evening starts at 18:00. Entering home never ends a free day automatically.

| Action | Clock cost |
|---|---|
| Walk, browse tickets/posters, join clubs, inspect finds, brief conversation | None |
| Station sign-off or any of these six small tickets, including confirmation | None; each is a short encounter |
| A substantial daytime relationship visit or other offered activity | One period; its start choice names the period it spends |
| Room 203 chair: "Spend the rest of the morning/lunch/afternoon here" | Advances to the next period; always available |
| Room 203 chair: "Rest until evening" | Skips remaining daytime periods, even with no work done |
| Evening club scene or Kotodama run | Uses that evening; returns to evening exploration, with Sleep available at home |
| Room 203 bed: Sleep, offered in evening | Next day's morning |

Day 3 teaches time at the first "Rest until evening" choice. Earlier jobs need no time tutorial. Later days also allow smaller period advances.

Each day offers one major scene and two tickets. Deferred scenes stay at their places and times; walking there offers them. There is no event-choice screen or cap on scenes the player deliberately visits. Station confirmation recurs every morning; tennis on Sunday evenings; B2 on weekday evenings. Tickets follow [systems.md](../docs/game/systems.md#tickets), including payment once and no deadlines.

Eric's first supported command sends melon soda from the machine to Kenji; frame its impossible movement and the established sound/light effect. Kenji reaches for the selection button, then stops with the can already in his other hand. Mori looks from the machine to Eric. Mio asks them to keep this within the room; both agree before further play. Save that they witnessed it. Later entrances recall this and skip the reveal.

Use [Kotodama's guided first delivery](../game3d/minigames/kotodama.html), with melon soda as Kenji's introductory request. Pause after delivery for those reactions, then offer Leave or Continue. This introduction is the reveal scene. Keep its polite command form, using the existing `dashite` verb; gloss `kudasai` without an extra lesson.

Day 5 adds recipient versus object (`ni` / `o`) one tap at a time. Names, さん and メロンソーダ keep readings and English. Particles can wait another evening. `to`, `mo` and further commands stay optional extended play.

Exit returns to the same B2 evening. Scores, hearts and replay drinks stay inside the minigame; no ticket, wallet or bond rewards. First-delivery exit gets Kenji's response; full-run exit uses the last recipient actually served. Integration needs melon-soda data/art, the delivery pause and safe returns.
