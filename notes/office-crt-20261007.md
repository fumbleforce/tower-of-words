# Office CRT correction

The office's existing [period rule](../docs/game/art-and-sound.md) specifies beige CRTs. The shared detailed and plain monitor builders instead produced thin panels. This correction adds a default-off `crt` option and enables it only for the six island desks, chief desk, machine-room cart and Emi's existing monitor. Other places and unopted builder output retain their previous geometry.

The housing reuses the original screen object/material and local transform, so live status handles and screen dimensions stay fixed. The chief desk alone also needed its inherited orientation corrected: it had placed the screen back toward Mori and the keyboard beyond the monitor. Rotating that desk from −π/2 to +π/2 puts the screen toward the seated worker and the keyboard between him and the screen; chair, actor, desk center and paper contacts stay fixed. Native before/after surface-normal and keyboard-order evidence accompanies this explicit transform correction. Every other monitor transform is unchanged. The tube casing tapers to fit the existing opposed-desk gap, has a broad pedestal, beige bezel and exposed top, side and rear ventilation. Its .205 canonical-unit depth is checked against the actual .44 spacing between the opposed monitor origins. Three existing dust covers follow the deeper housing; no furniture or actor moves.

Four unit checks cover default parity, original live-screen identity/transform, substantial housing depth with unobstructed screen and nonintersecting opposed backs. The native `office-crt-check.mjs` checks all nine actual monitors and captures chief/cart side and oblique views inside their real rooms. These are labelled diagnostic views, not story cameras. The earlier independent review accepted the isolated CRT housing and orientation at 8/10 with no runtime findings. Its one checker finding is fixed: the helper installs `scopedRoute({ publicOnly: true })` before navigation and sets public mode itself. Research story acceptance is separate.

CRT changes are independent of the uncommitted Research story and its held Claude voice gate. The exact CRT-only patch includes the housing helper, opt-in builders/call sites, three lowered byte budgets, this note, the existing art-rule source link and the scoped tests/tool. No story or audio is included. [Showcase](../showcase/office-crt-20261007/entry.json) retains the CRT housing and orientation attempts and the isolated desktop/phone captures.
