I bought corn soup (V-7 "Got: Hot corn soup"), walked to Mori and pressed E: nothing (V-10, V-11). Clicking him does nothing either (V-13). Items in the bag can't be clicked (V-12). The goal chip stays on "The vending machine is stuck" after the machine has already paid out (V-8).

   - I only got in by guessing a click on the floor inside the room (176-walkin). Then the chair beat fired without me touching the chair (178-tab).
4. **Models walk through and stand inside other models and furniture.** Screenshots:

## Design notes (not bugs)

- Scrambled speech (Aoi, guard, Mori, Sales) takes up a big share of the lobby and B2. Blurred random kana with no explanation read to me as broken text at first. Kenji's bottom-bar bark isn't even blurred (V-9, "あむね、ケごほお…"), so a learner tries to read it. One line of framing the first time would fix this, or making it clearly look like "can't follow".
- Too many markers at once: nine on the train (02) and about 20 in B2 (156). The teal one is the goal, but I had to hunt for it.
- Eric's portrait (a grown man with a ponytail and glasses) doesn't match his 3D model (a spiky-haired kid).
- Portraits cover the action. On the platform Mio's portrait sits exactly over Aoi and the doors (66), and on phone she covers half the car (P-09).
- Clicking the target of the current goal gives an English fallback ("Hi. Um… good morning?") instead of pointing to Q. It's fine as a joke once, but it loops.
- The phone shows keyboard hints ("1 2 3" key numbers on choices, "Hold V" in the typing box on desktop). The phone says "Hold the mic", which is correct.
- I couldn't check whether a phone keyboard covers the typing box, because headless has no on-screen keyboard. Worth a real-phone check.

Yes. The magic-word hook (the doors stop for 待って, the copier works for 動いて, Mio's recap) made me want day 2. But I nearly quit at the machine room door and when I couldn't give Mori his soup, because nothing told me what I was doing wrong.
