# Plaza noticeboard and bakery flyer

Final writing for C0212/C0215, #117/#118, implemented unchanged in dc9a3eb4 at [game3d/story/finds.js](../game3d/story/finds.js). Residents write in Japanese; the interface gives a glossed heading and English gist. The drafts below record how the copy was selected.

## Proposed merged copy

### ありがとう (arigatou, thank you)

To whoever folded my washing at the coin laundry: thank you. The shirts were still damp, so I've hung them up again. You fold much better than I do.

Small reply in another hand: Sorry. The towels were dry.

### カラオケ (karaoke)

Does anyone know the song with the woman on the ferry in the video? It starts quietly, then she gets angry. Karaoke box, room 3.

Small reply in another hand: Room 3 shows the ferry video for every song.

### ポンプ (ponpu, pump)

If you borrowed my bicycle pump, please bring it back. My front tyre is flat.

Small reply in another hand: Returned. I pumped up your tyre while I was at it.

### ラジオ体操 (rajio taisou, radio exercises)

The bakery staff leave after the first stretch to start the ovens. They are not skipping. Please stop crossing their names off the sheet.

## Bakery flyer

## Staging limits

The collected flyer retains its full text when reopened. No pickup quest for the objects mentioned in notices. The laundry, karaoke and bakery are established places; these messages do not require new interiors in day 1. Merged from independent root and helper drafts, then revised from Claude's cold read in notes/plaza-notices-coldread.md (C0215). The headings are glossed rather than learned. No coupon, price, opening hour or timed event. The noticeboard reading surface is explicitly requested in #117.
