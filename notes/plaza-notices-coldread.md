# Plaza noticeboard and bakery flyer: cold read

Read as a first-time player of the draft in wt/codex-train-discoveries notes/plaza-notices-draft.md (X-0332, C0212, #117/#118). Checked against GUIDE (Writing, Learning rules), docs/game/setting.md, words.md, places.md and Jørgen's in-game feedback (2026-09-30_212453: "noticeboard should have some mundane slightly funny or interesting posts"; _212743: "am I supposed to get something? Just mail saying Bakery, but i am not able to pick it up. It should say someting / do something").

## Across the board: the language

setting.md says everyone on the island speaks Japanese ("Nobody speaks English. Even at the supermarket."), so a resident's note is written in Japanese. The world's written text so far is either bilingual (the fountain's コインを入れないで PLEASE DON'T THROW COINS, the flyer's パン BAKERY) or described (the train poster "in katakana"). Handwritten notes in plain English would be the only English-speaking residents on the island.

Fix: each notice gets a short Japanese heading shown the way words.md shows a word (Japanese, reading, English), with the English body underneath as the gist, the way "English always carries the text". Pick headings Eric can sound out, loanwords where possible, since that is the words-in-context charm Jørgen likes and katakana is his weak spot:

| Notice | Heading |
|---|---|
| Washing | ありがとう (arigatou, thank you) |
| Karaoke | カラオケ (karaoke) |
| Pump | ポンプ (ponpu, pump) |
| Exercises | ラジオ体操 (rajio taisou, radio exercises) |

None of these become known; they're glossed, not taught. Keep the English titles out, so each post shows one heading, not two.

## Per post

### Washing: fix (small)

Good: ordinary, grateful and slightly passive-aggressive at once, and the coin laundry by the dorms is canon. Two problems. A reader doesn't know where the washing was, and "Sorry, I only checked the towels." needs a second read to see that the towels were dry and the shirts weren't.

Body: "To whoever folded my washing at the coin laundry: thank you. The shirts were still damp, so I've hung them up again. You fold much better than I do."
Reply: "Sorry. The towels were dry."

### Karaoke: fix (the reply)

The body is good and hooks the island's ferry. "I was in karaoke room 3." reads as a stray fact. The reply "Do you remember any of the words?" is a straight answer and has no joke, so the post ends flat.

Body: "Does anyone know the song with the woman on the ferry in the video? It starts quietly, then she gets angry. Karaoke box, room 3."
Reply: "Room 3 shows the ferry video for every song."

(Karaoke machines play the same stock clips over any song; the reply makes room 3 the point.)

### Bicycle pump: fix

Mundane but the only one with no turn: "Returned. Sorry." closes it off with nothing. "by the dorm entrance" also points at a pump the player could go looking for in the dorm court, where none is built (GUIDE: story and screen must match), so drop the place.

Body: "If you borrowed my bicycle pump, please bring it back. My front tyre is flat."
Reply: "Returned. I pumped up your tyre while I was at it."

### Morning exercises: fix (clarity)

The best idea on the board (company culture, an attendance sheet for radio exercises, and it sets up the bakery the flyer pays off), but it asks the reader to infer the joke across three sentences. "The bakery staff can leave..." reads like a rule, not a person who is fed up. Radio exercises on the sports field are canon.

Body: "The bakery staff leave after the first stretch to start the ovens. They are not skipping. Please stop crossing their names off the sheet."

## Bakery flyer: fix

As drafted it is accurate (slicing to order is a real Japanese bakery detail, worth keeping) but it is shop information, and it doesn't answer Jørgen's "am I supposed to get something?": nothing in it is for Eric, so there's no reason he took it. Make it the welcome flyer every new dorm resident finds in their box. It's his first post on his first day in the worst room in the dorm, which is reason enough, and the half-loaf line lands as a small joke about living alone. Don't add a coupon or offer: the bakery can't be visited on day 1 (scope).

パン (pan, bread) BAKERY, as on the model
New to the dorms? Welcome!
Fresh bread every morning, straight after radio exercises.
Half-loaves for one. We slice to order.
Covered shopping street

It ties to the exercises notice without a clock time (a bakery opening hour would be a new fact with no home). The "do something" half is the builder's: taking it into Finds (C0212).

## Phone and setting

With these fixes every post is a heading, three short sentences or fewer and a one-line reply, which reads on a phone without scrolling if the board shows one post at a time. Nothing mentions cars, a commute or the mainland. No quest, reward or new place is implied. The staging notes (reply attached to its own notice, the flyer keeps its text when reopened) are right.
