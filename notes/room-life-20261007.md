## Beats

The canteen worker wipes the counter and pauses when approached. Pointing to the dispenser or return trolley gets a Japanese direction, supported by the physical object and her gesture. A separate question concerns the outside chairs she closes each evening. At lunch she is busy; asking later still offers the full reply. She thanks a player who actually helped on the terrace. Returning gets a shorter response.

Kenji watches a silent sports replay on the common-room television. Talk can concern the result he already knows or his continued exclusion from Mio's machine room. If the player has already discussed the arcade with him, the work conversation can recall that conversation. Leaving does not consume either topic. On Sunday his existing discovery of the remote under him plays once, then he sits back down.
