## 2026-10-10: proud to show it off

Jørgen: "I think we have made good progress on the process. ... I think its fun. Especially when the intro landed, that has been a major highlight. I want to commend you and the other claudes for getting things to this point where I would be proud to show it off. ... When it hits well, it's really good."

What had landed by then:
- The anime opening, a 70 s film to the opening theme, with the station rebuilt and the island's best views (the other Claude session's lane).
- The anime look as the game's default: toon light, outlines, painted stone, and the fountain back to one continuous waterfall.
- The cast with written bios, voice sheets and one voice agent per character; Kenji's first meeting in one consistent voice; Kuro's day-one reception that warms only when you greet her in Japanese.
- A new-game screen with Eric or Carina, chosen on portrait cards.
- Outdoor places rebuilt on one system: forecourt trees everywhere, the sports courts with floodlights, the north campus, the station with its cross deck.
- The shared metal and glass look, one lighting and time-of-day system, and every place within its performance budget.
- A process that ships: a landing queue, day tests that wait for the GPU, a stale check that wants commits rather than notes, and agents that clean up after themselves.
- The local-only side reached the game itself, playable in a normal run rather than only in a viewer.
