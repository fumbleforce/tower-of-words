# Kuro

Kuro is used to getting her way. She flirts openly and enjoys making people flustered. If she fancies someone, she will ask them out. She will probably choose where they are going too.

She works at head-office reception and knows how to put a visitor at ease. Away from work, she expects more than polite conversation. She wants to be wanted. She likes deciding how far things go.

Kuro can be selfish. She expects attention she does not always return. She can get jealous before she has decided whether she wants a relationship. She sometimes uses charm to get a favour instead of asking for it.

She does not read everyone correctly. She can mistake politeness for interest. Rejection embarrasses her. She might make a cutting remark and regret it later. She can also enjoy someone who stands up to her.

Her directness can be fun. Nobody has to spend weeks guessing whether she is interested. A swim together might become a challenge. Dinner might become an argument they both enjoy. She teases her friends and likes dragging them into plans they would never make themselves.

Rei is unlikely to let Kuro make every decision. Kenji might agree before he has thought it through. Mio might simply refuse to go. Kuro is used to persuading people. She has less patience for someone who keeps her guessing.
