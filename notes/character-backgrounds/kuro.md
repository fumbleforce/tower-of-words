# Kuro

Kuro is thirty-eight and flirts openly with people she finds attractive. She speaks in a low, unhurried voice and enjoys asking personal questions, especially when she can see that she is making someone flustered. She has no difficulty asking for a date and usually assumes she will choose the restaurant. Once there, she may order another drink for her guest without asking whether they want one.

Kuro wears her black hair in a high bun, with a blunt fringe and long locks beside her face. She has dark eyes and wears black trouser suits that she chooses carefully for their fit. She likes it when someone notices the effort she has made with her clothes. At reception, she remembers visitors' names and faces and pays attention to their moods. Her limited English makes it difficult to say much to visitors from abroad.

Kuro spent most of her twenties at a mainland hotel's reception desk. Shared flats and changing shifts made it difficult to have anyone over. She accepted the job on the island because it came with her own place and regular hours. Although she had little interest in the company at first, she enjoyed knowing who was arriving and who was being kept waiting. If a visitor snaps their fingers at her, she sometimes makes them wait even when she could help them straight away.

Kuro lives in a small staff apartment overlooking the east side of the island. Her bedroom is crowded with clothes, but she keeps the dining table clear so she can have people over. She finds cooking boring, so she prefers to order takeaway and serve it on proper plates with a bottle of wine she has chosen. She swims regularly, even when she has nobody to go with, and likes a long bath afterwards. At home she watches old romantic dramas and complains about the decisions the heroines make.

Kuro wants one of the island's expensive seafront apartments by the time she is forty-five, with a balcony large enough for dinner guests. She is saving for it and would be pleased to have a wealthy partner, but would resent being told what to do because they paid for things. She used to live with a boyfriend, but they separated after repeatedly arguing about money. She still describes him as stingy and usually leaves out how often she hid purchases from him.

Kuro checks her phone repeatedly after sending an invitation. If no answer comes, she may send something cruel and regret it in the morning. She gets jealous even when she has promised nothing herself. She is attracted to people who stand up to her, but she still wants to decide what they do together.
