# Jun

Proposed in play: Jun makes a room welcoming without demanding conversation. Payroll and bar work reward his preparation, but he can be more irritated by a wrong stock estimate than by a difficult customer.

## Established

The `jun` entry in [the bible](../../bible/facts.yaml) establishes age 38, payroll work by day and running the company bar in the evenings. The former-jazz-drummer alternative was rejected and stays rejected. His exact rota and the bar's staffing are not yet established; the proposal does not require him to work two complete shifts every day.

## Proposed history

Jun entered payroll work through routine office administration and found he liked the combination of a repeatable process and small changes that mattered to people. He learned to explain a confusing payslip without assuming its owner was bad at arithmetic. He can enjoy a neatly reconciled total and still understand why the person asking is upset before the explanation is finished.

He first helped behind the company bar when an existing worker needed cover. He liked setting up a room where people could spend time without having to justify being there. Over time he took responsibility for ordering and arranging evening cover as well as serving. The eventual schedule needs to show other staff and real time off; running the place is not proof that he personally pours every drink.

His family entertained in an informal way. People brought something, somebody had forgotten a glass and a guest might end up washing up because they preferred that to the main conversation. Jun learned to offer a practical task without making its acceptance the price of welcome. He is comfortable with a quiet customer and does not assume they need to be drawn out.

An old friend from payroll started visiting the bar and once began a salary conversation within earshot of other customers. Jun changed the subject and spoke to the friend later. They remained friends. The incident made him more deliberate about keeping his two jobs separate.

He is in a steady relationship with someone who sometimes comes to the bar with their own friends and sometimes has no wish to spend an evening at his workplace. Their shared time depends on a rota they both see. They enjoy shopping for an ordinary home meal together because it gives them a reason to argue mildly over something unimportant. The partner's identity remains open.

## What this gives play

Jun remembers what somebody chose without deciding that they must always choose it again. He can calm a busy counter through preparation and become disproportionately annoyed when his careful stock estimate is wrong. He offers practical hospitality and sometimes completely misreads the reason for a customer's good mood.

He wants a workable evening rota and one well-attended quiet event where people can actually hear each other. Saki's proposed game evening is one possibility if both characters want it, not an established weekly club. He also wants to cook at home more often without turning it into another stock-management exercise.

Incidental tells include setting water where it is easy to reach, writing an order down even when he thinks he will remember it, and genuinely failing to recognise a familiar customer's coat when they leave it behind.

## Discoveries across play

1. His eventual introduction can offer a drink or a place to sit. Known `nomitai` lets the player make a preference; buying alcohol is never the condition for conversation.
2. A later visit reveals that another adult is running the counter while Jun sits with his own meal. A `yasumi` question can lead to how he began covering shifts.
3. If the player knows Saki, a public invitation she agreed to can lead between them. `yoyaku` supports asking whether the evening is booked, with the invitation retained for a later opportunity if missed.
4. With familiarity, he can describe how he and his partner make time together, or the payroll friend's badly placed question. He does not repeat the salary or name the employee discussed.

No amount of friendship unlocks payroll information or another customer's private account. His bar can hold laughter, boring evenings and conversations he does not fully understand.
