# Kenji

Kenji is twenty-one and started his first job on B2 two months ago. He is eager to help and often agrees to do something before he understands what he has been asked. When he realises he has misunderstood, he is embarrassed to ask again. Sometimes he guesses what he should do and ends up taking the wrong equipment to the wrong floor.

Kenji has an open smile, untidy black hair and a tie he rarely gets straight on the first try. He is pudgy and rather clumsy, often bumping into chairs as he squeezes past them. He drinks melon soda throughout the day and has been banned from the machine room since he spilled some there.

Before moving to the island, Kenji lived with his parents above their small bicycle shop on the mainland and helped out in the shop. His father was patient when he needed a repair explained several times, but customers sometimes became irritated with him. He preferred working in the back, where he could finish one thing before someone asked for another. He took a vocational computing course before applying for the island job. Leaving home was his idea, although his mother still phones to ask whether he has washed his shirts.

Kenji spends much of his time in his dorm room building plastic spacecraft from old science-fiction television programmes. There are several unfinished models among the carefully sorted parts, and a pile of laundry beside the bed. He takes his time over the models and finds the printed instructions easier to follow than several people talking at once. He also knows the programmes well enough to talk about them without worrying that he has got something wrong.

Kenji wants to become an engineer who can be sent out on a job alone. His first large purchase would be a proper workbench for his room. For now, he is so worried about making mistakes that he sometimes says everything is fine when a job has gone wrong. Colleagues have had to stay late finishing jobs he said were done. He is quick to apologise but worries that they will stop trusting him to do the work.

Kenji is grateful to anyone who takes the time to explain things without making him feel stupid. He listens patiently and tries to be just as patient when someone needs his help. He finds it difficult to refuse a favour, especially when the other person sounds disappointed in him.
