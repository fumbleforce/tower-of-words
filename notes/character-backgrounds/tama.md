# Tama

Proposed in play: Tama has several households' worth of acquaintances and no interest in making their accounts agree. She visits where she expects comfort, surprises people by changing her route and occasionally leaves two admirers each certain she has chosen the other.

## Established

[Cast](../../docs/game/cast.md#tama-tama) and [story](../../docs/game/stories/tama.md): a calico cat who travels on the monorail, appears near the guard and sleeps on the B2 chair. Her [familiarity route](../../docs/game/cast.md#tama-a-place-she-chooses-to-return-to) concerns choosing to return while retaining a way out. Her origins are not established.

## Proposed observed history

Nobody in the current cast knows Tama's whole history. Ishibashi remembers her appearing at the station before she began waiting near his lunch. Mori remembers her first visit to a warm B2 chair and assumes she already knew the building. Neither account establishes where she was born or whether she belonged to somebody else.

Ishibashi initially put water near the shelter after finding her there on several warm days. He moved it when the passage became busy. She returned to the first spot for a while, then used the new one. The arrangement became familiar enough that he was irritated when someone put a bag in front of it. He does not consider this an interesting story about himself.

Mori once offered her a folded cloth on an unused seat. She ignored it until he moved it onto the chair she had chosen. He remembers having found a solution. Mio remembers losing a perfectly usable cloth to the cat. Kenji remembers that she came to inspect his empty bag while everybody was discussing where she ought to sit.

A shop worker recognises her through the window. Tama sometimes pauses there and sometimes keeps walking. The worker believes she likes the morning sun on that side of the street. Later the player may see her there when it is cloudy. A resident's fond explanation need not be the final explanation.

## What she does now

Her days can include food, warmth, irritation and curiosity without a hidden mission. A familiar person makes an encounter more predictable, but does not guarantee that she will accept touch. She may approach somebody who has barely noticed her and leave when they become interested. A bag moved yesterday can deserve another inspection today.

For continuing play, a small change in the environment can alter her route. A noisy repair by the shelter sends her to another dry place for a while. Ishibashi notices the absence. The player sees her elsewhere before he does, and can later tell him where. When the work ends she may return, making the world feel shared without inventing danger or a rescue plot.

The change must be visible, temporary and compatible with the existing Tama route. Her absence cannot silently remove a promised meeting indefinitely. A later return should use a place the player has actually seen her choose, and she must retain an unobstructed exit.

## Learning who knows her

Her background emerges from different people recognising the same animal. A word such as `koko` helps ask where she was seen, while a later time or place word can clarify why two apparently contradictory accounts were both true. The player is collecting observations rather than decoding a secret the cat carries.

An encounter can end with her settling down, departing or taking the seat somebody else wanted. The person present may find it funny, inconvenient or mildly disappointing. No speech, command compliance or reward for feeding is added by this history. The established familiarity milestones still govern when she chooses to approach the player without food.
