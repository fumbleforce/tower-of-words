# Tama

Proposed in play: Different residents have earned different kinds of familiarity with Tama. Repeated encounters show a cat remembering useful places and people while remaining free to leave.

## Established

[Cast](../../docs/game/cast.md#tama-tama) and [story](../../docs/game/stories/tama.md): a calico cat who travels on the monorail, appears near the guard and sleeps on the B2 chair. Her [familiarity route](../../docs/game/cast.md#tama-a-place-she-chooses-to-return-to) concerns choosing to return while retaining a way out. Her origins are not established.

## Proposed history

The usable history is a record of associations that different residents have seen. Tama began visiting the station before the player arrived. Ishibashi first noticed her waiting away from the busiest part of the entrance, then appearing beside the sheltered area when he unpacked lunch. He put water down. The bowl became routine before anybody agreed on who was responsible for it. Her earlier life remains unknown.

Mori encountered her later on a day when a warm chair had just been vacated. His first attempt to offer a folded cloth elsewhere was ignored. He put it on that chair, and she used it. He remembers this as a successful arrangement. Mio remembers having to move the cloth whenever someone actually needed to work. Both can be right without turning the cat into a dispute that the player must solve.

A bakery worker recognises her through the window and keeps the entrance clear when she is passing. Kenji has tried to attract her attention with an object she then preferred to ignore. She later investigated the bag it came from. These are ordinary observed encounters, with room for residents to overstate how well they know her.

Her movement choices can suggest memory: a dry edge of a bench after rain, an open route between furniture, a particular person who remains still. She leaves when someone becomes noisy and may return when the disturbance has passed.

## What this gives play

Tama's present wants change with warmth, food, shelter, disturbance and curiosity. Familiar people make some places predictable, but familiarity does not guarantee touch or obedience. The player learns to notice her pause before approaching, move a bag and keep an exit open. Sometimes she chooses a different seat for reasons nobody identifies.

Incidental tells include inspecting a moved cloth before settling, briefly attending to an unfamiliar bag, and returning after a room quiets down. The guard is sometimes surprised by where she appears.

## Discoveries across play

1. After the player has actually seen the bowl, Ishibashi may identify it as the same one he has used for some time. His account adds a small piece of the relationship without spending his later route moment of directly acknowledging the cat in the rain.
2. Mori can tell the cloth story only when the chair is visible and not during urgent work. A later appearance on a different seat keeps his story from becoming a rule.
3. At the bakery, a passing interaction can show that another person recognises her. The player learns that their own encounters are only part of her day.
4. Once familiar, she may approach without food and leave again. That remains governed by her existing route; the background adds possible callbacks, not earlier completion or bonus obedience.

Words such as `koko` can help ask where someone saw her. Answers remain the residents' observations; Tama keeps choosing her own company.
