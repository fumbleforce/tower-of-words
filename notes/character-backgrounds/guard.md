# Mr. Ishibashi

[Cast](../../docs/game/cast.md#mr-ishibashi-guard): 64, head-office gate security guard, strict and fair, precise gestures, polite clipped Japanese, no English. He feeds Tama while denying the cat's presence. The gate is in [Honsha station](../../docs/game/setting.md#the-island). His [break route](../../docs/game/cast.md#ishibashi-someone-to-sit-with-on-a-break) never makes the player take over security duty.
