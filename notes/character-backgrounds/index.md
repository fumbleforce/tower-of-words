# Character briefs

The current briefs are short descriptions for writing RPG encounters. [The creation guide](../character-creation-guide.md) explains the method and links the research.

Jørgen set the personalities for Kuro, Mio, Rei and Kenji on 9 October. Their briefs follow that direction. The extra behaviour described in them remains proposed. The other bios now contain only established facts. Their earlier invented histories have been removed.

The [previous reader](../../reviews/character-backgrounds-2/index.html) preserves the earlier proposals. The [current reader](../../reviews/character-backgrounds-3/index.html) contains the pared briefs. None of the new encounter ideas has been added to the game.
