# Cold read: day-2 outline (7bb9f81a)

Claude's story-reader pass on notes/day2-outline.md, for Codex (X-0432 to X-0434, work #186). Read as a first-time player who has just finished day 1, then checked against GUIDE (Dialogue quality, Story craft, Learning rules), docs/game/ and Jørgen's recorded taste. The outline itself is unchanged.

Verdict: the shape is right. One work problem, one evening with the team, four people at the party, one new pattern, an optional nook. Jørgen asked for exactly that. Scene writing can start once findings 1 to 6 are settled in the outline. Findings 7 onward can be handled while writing.

## Must fix before writing

1. **The order of the day fights the day-1 ending, and it doubles the walking.** (Outline, scenes 1 and 2.) Day 1 ends with Mio saying, on both branches, "Tomorrow morning you go down to the station" / "It's the station, tomorrow morning ... Don't sleep through it" (office.js `end_ticket`). The outline starts at B2 with Emi and then sends Eric back west to the platform. The route becomes room 203 → dorm court → plaza → forecourt → lift → B2 → lift → forecourt → station → platform → (back to B2?) → forecourt → plaza → arcade → promenade → dorms. On a phone in a 10 to 15 minute session that is mostly walking, and nobody says what happens between the station and "after work". Suggested fix: put the station first, on the way to work, as Mio promised. Then Eric arrives at B2 with a result in hand, and Emi's "what can you actually maintain" lands on something he has just done. Kenji's invitation can come in that same B2 scene. A desk sit (as on day 1) moves the clock to after work. Emi's need for a diagnosis before she orders still works: she hears it from him instead of sending him.

2. **開けて isn't known on every path.** (Scene 2, "Prompted 開けて".) Eric learns `akete` only if he took Hamada's word at the gate (sleeping-man, `word_type`). On the guard's way he hears Hamada say it but never types it, so it isn't known (words.md, "When a word is known"). For those players the platform teaches a new word, and the day then has two new things, not "one new pattern". Suggested fix: restart the doors with 動いて, which every player knows from the copier. It's also a nice small surprise, since a motor is a machine that "works". Keep 開けて as the line for players who know it, or drop it.

3. **The ticket choice has no stakes, and it skips past what day 1 set up.** (Scene 2, the two wordings.) "Couldn't reproduce it with the controls" and "Works normally; keep an eye on it" are nearly the same sentence. Both are honest, both save the money, and nothing reads them, so the choice is a click. Day 1 already set up a real dilemma: Mio told the station it was the sensor and told Eric "if anybody asks, it was the sensor" (cold branch of `end_ticket`), and Emi now has new money and wants a diagnosis before she orders a sensor (emi-budget). Covering for Mio means Emi spends her hard-won budget on a part that fixes nothing. Not covering means the ticket contradicts Mio. Suggested fix: make one option Mio's story ("Sensor's worn, replace it") and one the honest non-answer. Give the choice one reader: Mio's reaction at the party, or Emi's line when the order does or doesn't go in. Still one choice and no new scene.

4. **The station scene repeats day 1's best moment without a turn.** (Scene 2.) The player has seen 待って stop these doors, heard Mio talk about it at lunch, and had it listed back to them in the evening. Making them say it on the same doors again to "learn" that the sensor is fine tells them nothing new. Also, as written the required steps are: doors cycle, sensor test, 待って, 開けて, ticket choice, Mio or phone. That is six beats for "check a door", which is the pickle-jar problem Jørgen named. Suggested fix: the required job is two beats, run the check (doors cycle and the sensor light passes, staged as one action) and write the ticket. Saying 待って becomes the player's own option ("Try it again"), not a prompt. The turn is that someone is watching. If Mio came, this pays off her day-1 line "I want to see how you, um... fix a sensor": she watches him do it, or she watches him decide not to. If she didn't come, give the scene a reason to be careful (station staff waiting on the ticket). The outline's "motor still trying" detail is good and worth keeping for players who choose to try.

5. **Mori can't be in two places, and the optional encounter breaks the party's setup.** (Exploration, the `shotengai_back_alley` nook.) "Mori and Mio already have the food at the promenade benches ... the party is ready when he arrives", but in the nook "Mori is resting beside his shopping bag on the way to the benches". If the food is already there, why is he still carrying shopping? Also, Kenji is meant to be walking Eric to the promenade, so who is with Eric when he stops in the alley, and what does Kenji do? Suggested fix: move the quiet Mori moment to the walk home after the party (Mori stopping to rest on the way back, same nook, same "neither needs to fill the silence"). That gives the evening a coda, and nothing about the party's setup changes. If it stays before the party, then Mori is late and the party isn't ready yet; pick one.

6. **Where did the food come from? Every shop is shut.** (Scene 3.) places.md says every named shop in the arcade has 準備中 CLOSED on the door, and "The shops stay shut". A shopping bag and "a mixture" of food need an open konbini or bakery, or another source (the canteen, the izakaya's takeaway). Decide which, and record it in places.md before writing, since it is a place fact. A small payoff is available: Eric's mailbox on day 1 holds a bakery flyer (evening-walk, beat 5).

## The evening: room beyond teaching

7. **The party has a situation but no want, obstacle or turn yet.** (Scene 3.) GUIDE, Story craft: "Scenes must want something". At the moment the outline's party is choosing food, one exchange and a topic choice. The outline already has the seed: Mori bought a mixture because he doesn't know what Eric eats. That's Mori's small worry, and Eric's choice can be the answer to it. Real, specific material is close by: Mori went to Lillehammer in 1994 (cast.md), and Norway is the reason Japan eats salmon sushi. If one of Mori's guesses is a pack of salmon sushi bought "for the Norwegian", Eric picking it, or politely not, is the turn, and it's Mori's moment, not a lesson. Others can have their own small things without new steps: Kenji's melon soda, Mio's mother's pickles (she was given a bag on day 1 because "island has no food"). These are props and lines, not beats.

8. **Mio teaches again, at the one scene meant to get her off duty.** (Japanese section: "Mori offers it; Mio supplies the Japanese".) She has taught most of day 1's words. Suggested fix: let Kenji model 食べたい. He speaks casual Japanese, he's the one "eating before everyone has chosen", and grabbing something with 食べたい! is exactly in voice. Eric copies him. Mio can comment dryly or not at all. That gives her the room the outline promises her ("finally away from the server room").

9. **Each person needs a reason to be there, and Emi's absence needs one line.** (Scene 3, cast.) Mio "doesn't love strangers", goes home at 18:05, and eats alone because "nobody talks to me there" (cast.md, lunch.md). Why does she come? Most likely because Mori asked her and she can't say no to Mori. One line from her covers it. Emi is the team lead and isn't at her own team's welcome. A line from Kenji or a text from Emi ("upstairs again, eat something on my budget") makes four people read as chosen, not as a gap. And a reader at a welcome party by an izakaya will ask why they're on benches. One line covers that too (Mio vetoes the noise, or Mori prefers the sea). Japanese welcome parties (歓迎会) are usually on a Friday, and day 2 is Friday 2 October. That's a detail worth using.

10. **Day-1 choices the party should notice.** (Scene 3.) The outline reads only Mio's offer. One line each, no new branches: `lunch_mori` (Mori and Eric already have Lillehammer between them), `lunch_mio` (Mio is less guarded), `gate_magic` (Kenji's 朝、ゲートが勝手に開いたって gossip had a follow-up), `hamada_friend` (the crackers). Not all of them are needed. Choose one or two that change how someone greets Eric.

11. **Mori's Japanese needs to reach the player.** ("Mori speaks Japanese, with English carrying the player through it.") On day 1 the cold playtest said "Everything Mori says back is scrambled". At a party where he is one of three people, overheard, muffled Japanese walls him off, and Mio translating puts her back to work (finding 8). Use the subtitled mode in systems.md (Overheard Japanese: voice clear, English shown) for Mori's party lines, and say so in the outline.

12. **The Kenji meeting point.** (Scene 1 and 3: "a clear meeting place at the arcade's east mouth".) They leave the same office at the same time, so the player will wonder why they meet across the island and don't walk out together. Either Kenji has a reason to go ahead (an errand, the food), or he leaves with Eric. Also, "east mouth" is design talk. Kenji's English is nouns, so it needs a landmark he can say ("Izakaya! Blue... curtain. Here.") and the goal pin does the rest. If Kenji walks Eric to the promenade, make it him walking ahead and Eric free to follow, not an escort that waits on the player.

## Learning load

13. **～たい as a chunk is gentle enough, but the activity is four steps for picking a snack.** (Japanese section.) Choose in English → Mori offers → Mio supplies the Japanese → Eric types tabetai while pointing. That runs a simple action through four parts. Also, once Mori has offered the thing, saying "I want to eat" back is odd: you'd just take it. Suggested fix: two steps. Someone holds the food out with "which one?" (in English or subtitled), Eric taps one, then types tabetai once as the way he takes it, modelled first by Kenji (finding 8). Optional extra turns stay optional. The one short explanation of the ending comes from a person in a line (GUIDE: no exposition panels), for example Kenji's school English: "Taberu, eat. Tabe-tai, eat... want! Want eat!"

14. **Once 食べたい is in the Say menu, players will try it on everything.** On day 1 the cold player tried new words on whatever was near (開けて on the guard). 食べたい needs a few honest answers for people and machines (a machine "doesn't want anything" is already a joke in the Favours prototype). There's also a systems question: words.md has `phrase`, `command` and `word`, and 食べたい is none of them exactly. Settle that with Claude (engine) before scene writing.

## Hidden staging and access work (for Claude's build side)

15. **There is no way back to the platform.** places.md "Getting between places" has `train → gate`, `gate → forecourt` and `forecourt → plaza`, but no `forecourt → gate` or `gate → train`. No story node trips to `gate` or `train` either. The `train` place only exists as the arriving car with passengers and the near wall cut away. 17. **The door test needs things to see.** "Show stopped doors, a clear sensor and a motor still trying": the train model has no visible sensor, beam or test light, and no motor sound. That's new art and sound, and Jørgen approves every asset.

19. **"The player ends the evening when ready".** Name the one action that does it (talk to someone, or walk away and someone says goodnight) so there's no hunting, then the trip home. Day 1 ends on a summary screen. Say whether day 2 has one.
