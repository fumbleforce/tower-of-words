# Walkthrough: Mr. Mori

Mr. Mori (58), the oldest on the B2 team. Friendship route. Design only, nothing approved. Calendar, commands and machines: 00-overview.md.

## Who he is

- He joined Amakawa in 1990, the same year as Kiyoko. He came up through facilities IT, installed most of B2's machines in the nineties, and ran the section for eleven years. At 55 he lost the title under the company's age rule for managers (役職定年) and stayed on as ordinary staff. He still sits at the section chief's desk at the head of the island, because nobody has moved it and nobody wants to ask him to.
- He speaks only polite Japanese. He bows to guests, makes tea at three, and corrects Mio's 外人 to 外国の方 every time, quietly.
- He talks to machines politely, and always has. On day 1 he says 動いて to the copier, and it's Eric's voice that works. Mori sees that and doesn't ask about it. He bows very slightly to the copier.
- **Want:** to hand over thirty years of knowing these machines while he still can.
- **Problem:** it's all in fifteen binders, handwritten from 1996 on, mostly diagrams with Japanese notes. He has no English, and Eric has almost no Japanese.
- **Pressure:** head office has opened an early retirement offer (早期退職) to everyone over 55, closing on Friday 29 January. His wife Setsuko wants him to take it. He has filled in the form and not signed it.

Sample lines (Japanese, with English shown):
- 「どうぞ、お先に。」 (After you, please.) At the lift, every time.
- 「これは……私の字ですね。」 (That's... my handwriting.) Opening an old binder.
- 「エリックさん、お茶、入れましょうか。」 (Shall I make tea?)

## Point pace

The steps need 6, 14 and 24 points, at most 3 a day. Mori's sources: day 1 (3), the first three copier rounds (3), the locker (2), Monday tea (1 a week), one binder-guided fix on a B2 machine a week (2), and his needs (3 each). That gives step 2 in the first week, 14 by early November, and 24 by mid-December if you keep going to tea and using the binders. If you're short in January, the tea week (need 3) makes up the gap.

## Walkthrough

### Step 0 to 1: the lift lobby (Day 1)

**What counts for him:**
- よろしくおねがいします, said properly with a bow: +1 (the greeting).
- The copier: Eric's 動いて works where Mori's didn't. Flag `mori_saw_copier`, always set.

**Result:** step 1. He's in People as "Mr. Mori, B2".

### Day 1 lunch: the kitchenette

**Needs:** lunch on day 1, choosing him over Mio.

**The scene:** almost no shared language, and tea. The thermos is empty. He shows Eric by pointing how he fills it, and teaches 入れて. His Japanese is soft gibberish, with the words Eric knows standing out clear.

**Result:** +2 (3 for the day with the greeting), the command 入れて, flag `lunch_mori`.

**If you ate with Mio:** he teaches 入れて at the first three o'clock tea you go to, with no points.

### Beat 1: the copier round (step 1; weekday mornings in October)

**Needs:** step 1, 8:15 on a weekday, the copy room.

**What you do:** turn up at 8:15 with the side panel open, and stand next to him.

**The scene:** two minutes with no words. Panel open, paper checked, drum wiped, panel shut, a bow, 動いて. If Eric says 動いて instead, it starts faster and hums, and Mori looks at the copier, then at him.

**Result:** +1 on each of the first three mornings, then no more points. The round stays his. From the fourth morning he hands Eric the cloth for the drum, and that's Eric's part from then on. Flag `mori_round`.

**Tip:** the side panel is the copier's quirk: open it first and it jams far less. The round shows it before any binder does.

### Step 2: Friendly (6 points)

**Typical timing:** Tuesday 6 October with the day-1 lunch and three rounds, a few days later otherwise.

**What opens:** tea on Mondays as an open time, his likes in People, and **his personal ticket, locker 7**.

### Personal ticket: locker 7 (step 2)

**Needs:** step 2, a weekday afternoon, the lockers by the B2 stairwell.

**What you do:** the ticket is written in pencil and scanned in: locker 7 won't open. His key turns and nothing happens.

**The scene:** a 1990 steel locker with a cam lock.
- 開けて: it opens with a bang, and fifteen binders slide out onto the floor.
- By hand, with a screwdriver: it opens slowly, and Mori catches the binders.
- ゆっくり + 開けて (once you have it): the door swings open, and nothing falls.

Inside are the binders, labelled by machine and year in his handwriting. He picks up コピー機 1996 and holds it for a moment. Then he gives it to Eric without explaining, and puts the rest back.

**Result:** +2 and **Binder 1**. Its main diagram: side panel, then 止まって, then 動いて clears any jam in one go.

**If you don't come:** he opens it himself in week 4 and gives you Binder 1 at tea. There are no points, but nothing is lost.

### The turn: the council copies (step 2 with 14 points; Monday 9 November, or the next second Monday)

**Needs:**
- Step 2, 14 points, and Binder 1.
- Mori's check-up morning: Monday 9 November. If you're not ready, the next chances are Monday 14 December and Monday 11 January.
- Words taught: ありがとうございます, お茶.

**What you do:** at 9:05 Emi needs eighty copies of her council case (the vote is tomorrow) by 10:00. Mori is at the clinic. The copier jams on sheet three.

**The scene, beat by beat:**
1. The jam is a bad one: paper in the fuser, the kind Mori always clears himself.
2. Binder 1 is on your desk. The diagram shows the side panel, then 止まって, then 動いて. Following it clears the jam, and the copies are done by 9:50. Plain 動いて without the panel jams it again on sheet twenty.
3. Mori comes back at 11:00 and finds Binder 1 open on top of the copier at the right page, and the copy count on the display.
4. At tea he puts two more binders on Eric's desk, 空調 and 九四. At 17:25 he shows him the thermos and says 休んで, as he does every evening, and teaches it.

**Choices at tea:**

| Choice | What happens | Leads to |
|---|---|---|
| Point at the 1990 new-hires photo on the kitchenette wall | He points at himself, then at a young woman with a sharp bob: Kiyoko. 「同期です。」 (We joined the same year.) 同期 is taught. | `mori_doki`, which opens a line in Kiyoko's keeper story |
| Ask for the thickest binder on the shelf, the pump log | 「まだです。」 (Not yet.) | It comes at step 5 |
| Just drink the tea | He refills it | +1 |

**Result:**
- **Step 3**, and the command **休んで**.
- **The weekly binder ladder** starts the next Monday.
- **His address:** his first casual sentence to Eric, at the thermos: 「いいでしょう、これ。」 (Nice, isn't it.)
- **Access:** the key to the kitchenette cupboard, where the good tea is.
- Emi: +1, for the copies.

**Tips:**
- 休んで said at night keeps Kyūyon's winter relapse from coming back (mio.md), and stops the copier's morning jams.
- The Kiyoko photo is one of only two ways to warm Kiyoko up before February.

### The early retirement notice (from Tuesday 1 December)

HR's notice goes up on the B2 corridor noticeboard. Kenji reads it and says something to Mio (overheard, with 退職 clear once Mio's margin notes have used it). Mio's next note: "Mori-san got the letter, everyone over 55 got it."

Nothing else happens until Monday 18 January, when Mori leaves the form on the kitchenette table himself (need 3). He wants someone to see it, and he'd never say so.

### The payoff: the yearly check (Saturday 23 January, fixed)

**Needs:** Saturday 23 January, 8:00, the B2 machine room. It happens whatever your step is. Your step decides your part:
- Step 3 with 24 points: the full scene, and step 4.
- Step 3 with fewer points: the full scene with no step change. The key choice still happens.
- Below step 3: Mori runs it with Kenji. Eric carries things, and it's over by 15:00.

**Who's there:** Mori, Mio (all day), Kenji (until 13:00), Emi (brings lunch at 12:00), and Aoi if she's on her B2 rotation.

**What you do:** the yearly check (年次点検) powers down every B2 machine in the order on Mori's 1996 checklist, checks each one by hand, and powers them back up in reverse. Mori reads the checklist aloud, a line at a time.

**The scene, beat by beat:**
1. 8:00. The checklist is on a clipboard: forty-one lines in pencil, initialled every year since 1996.
2. **Powering down.** For each machine you choose: by hand, Mori's way, slow, with Mori walking you through the diagram, or 休んで, fast and gentle. Everyone on the team has seen machines behave oddly around Eric since October, and nobody makes anything of it on a Saturday.
3. **Checks.** Filters, fans, the copier's drum, the thermos. Each binder you hold makes one check faster and turns up something: a 1996 coin inside the copier, Mio's 2022 note taped inside rack 3.
4. **Powering up**, in reverse. Everything comes back until the last line, Kyūyon.
5. **Kyūyon doesn't start.** Mio tries, and so does Eric's plain 動いて. The last page of Binder 3 has a note in Mori's 1996 handwriting, and with the words he has now Eric can read the one that matters: ゆっくり.
6. Mori stands in front of rack 3, bows, and says 動いて, as he has every year for thirty years. Nothing happens.

**The key choice:**

| Choice | What happens | Flag |
|---|---|---|
| Say ゆっくり + 動いて quietly, under his voice | Kyūyon starts as Mori finishes speaking. He believes it answered him. He says nothing, and he stands there a while. | `mori_believes` |
| Say ゆっくり + 動いて yourself, out loud, after him | Kyūyon starts. Mori looks at Eric, then bows to him, the same small bow he gives the copier. | `mori_knows` |
| Fix it by hand from Binder 3 | It takes until 19:00. Everyone stays, and Emi orders pizza. Mori does the last line himself. | `mori_by_hand` |
| You don't have ゆっくり | Only the by-hand choice is open | `mori_by_hand` |

7. Mori signs the checklist for 2027 and gives Eric the machine room key on its old brass tag.

**Result:**
- **Step 4** (with 24 points).
- **Perk:** once a week at tea he explains one quirk by diagram, and that machine's card fills in.
- **The machine room key:** you can open rack casings without a ticket.
- Mio +1, Kenji +1 for being there.

**Tips:**
- Learn ゆっくり from Sumi before January. Without it the key choice has one option.
- All three choices end well, in different ways.

### Step 5: the pump station (step 4; Saturday 30 January, 6 or 13 February)

**Needs:** step 4, and one of those Saturdays at 7:00 on the sea wall by the pump station.

**What you do:** find Mori and Setsuko on their morning walk.

**The scene, beat by beat:**
1. Setsuko speaks good English. She taught it at the island's school for thirty years. She translates Mori's stories about the sea wall, and adds her own opinions.
2. Mori stops at the pump station gate. It's older than his time at Amakawa: 1979, before the island was finished. Under his arm is the thickest binder on his shelf, which isn't his. It's the original engineers' log, handed to him in 1990.
3. He gives it to Eric. Setsuko translates what he says, then tells Eric what he meant.
4. **Choice:** walk back with them for breakfast at family tower C, or go on to the arcade street alone.
   - Breakfast: Setsuko's rolled omelette, and the 1990 photo album. Flag `mori_breakfast`.
   - Going on alone: he waves, and nothing is lost.

**Result:**
- **Step 5 (friendship).**
- **The pump log (1979)**: the key item for the storm.
- **His address:** he pours Eric's tea first from now on.

**Missable:** if you miss all three Saturdays, the pump log stays on his shelf, and the storm is harder (below).

## Tips

- Take lunch with Mio on day 1 (the stronger start) and pick up 入れて at Mori's first tea. You lose only 2 points with him.
- Go to the copier round on the first three mornings. It's the fastest start on the island.
- Fill the thermos at 17:00 in October for need 1.
- Be at B2 on Monday 9 November with Binder 1 in hand.
- Get ゆっくり from Sumi before the yearly check.
- Keep one February Saturday free for the pump station.
