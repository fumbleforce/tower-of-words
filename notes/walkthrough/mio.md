# Walkthrough: Mio

Mio (25), B2's programmer, and the only person on the team with English. Romanceable. Design only, nothing approved. Calendar, commands and machines: 00-overview.md.

## Who she is

- Dry and low on energy in the morning. Keeps strangers at arm's length with headphones half on. She calls Eric 外人 the way you'd say "the new guy", and Mori quietly corrects her every time.
- Her English comes from work and the internet: loose, with "so", "like" and "basically", and an article dropped now and then (game3d/story/VOICE.md).
- She joined in April 2022 and has kept the B2 machine room running since then, mostly on her own. A foreign legacy engineer turning up now looks to her like the company planning her replacement. She doesn't say so.
- On the evening of day 1 she lists what she's seen him do and asks how he does it (STORY.md). After that she treats his voice like an undocumented feature: she uses it, doesn't report it, and doesn't try to explain it.
- **Want:** to stay on the island without asking anyone for anything.
- **Secret:** she's on a fixed-term contract, renewed every six months. The April renewal would take her past five years, and at that point Japanese law (無期転換) lets her ask for a permanent contract. Companies often avoid that by not renewing. HR decides fifth-year renewals by 15 March. Without a written request on file (asking for renewal and saying she intends to convert), their default is a one-year renewal "while reviewing", which leaves her exactly where she is now. The request has to be in keigo. She hates keigo more than anything at work.

Sample lines, for the voice:
- "HR sends paper for everything, it's like... their hobby."
- "You fixed it by talking to it. Okay. I'm not writing that in the ticket."
- 「……なんで敬語？」 (said when he's polite to her)

## Point pace

The steps need 6, 14 and 24 points, at most 3 a day. Typical sources for Mio: day 1 (3), the Kyūyon ladder in October (up to 6), her dryer ticket (1 or 2), two talks a week (2), needs (3 each), the winter relapse (up to 4). Step 2 comes in week 1 or 2. The 14 for her turn comes by mid-November if you keep an eye on Kyūyon, or early December if you don't. The 24 for her payoff comes in January at a steady pace.

## Walkthrough

### Step 0 to 1: the train (Day 1, Thursday 1 October)

**Needs:** nothing. This is day 1 (STORY.md).

**What counts for her:**
- Trying おはようございます on her first: +1.
- The doors with 待って: flag `mio_saw_doors`. It's always set.

**Result:** step 1. She's in People as "Mio, B2, programmer".

### Day 1 lunch: the machine room

**Needs:** Day 1, lunch, choosing her over Mori.

**The scene:** the only real conversation of day 1. The rack alarm goes off mid-onigiri and she teaches 止まって.

**Result:** +2 (3 for the day with the greeting, which is the cap), the command 止まって, flag `lunch_mio`. She uses his name once, when it's just the two of them.

**If you ate with Mori:** she teaches 止まって on Monday 5 October when the rack alarm goes off, with no points. That day's black coffee (+1) is how you catch up.

### Beat 1: the account (step 1; Fri 2 to Fri 9 October)

**Needs:** step 1, a weekday morning, B2.

**What you do:** log in at your desk. The 1994 directory cuts every name to eight characters and doesn't like his, so the account locks. Tap the error, and Mio is already behind you.

**The scene:** she fixes it without asking, typing too fast to follow. As she sits down again, a white envelope with 人事部 (HR, with a reading) printed in the corner slides out from under her keyboard. It's sealed. She pushes it back under.

**Choices:**
- Ask about it: "HR sends paper for everything, it's like... their hobby." Sets `mio_envelope_seen`, which lets her turn start in November instead of December. +1.
- Say thanks: +1.
- Say thanks in keigo: 「……なんで敬語？」, and no point.

### The Kyūyon ladder (Monday 5 to Friday 30 October)

| Rung | When | Symptom | Fix | Result |

### Step 2: Friendly (6 points)

### Personal ticket: dryer 14 (step 2)

**Needs:** step 2, a Tuesday or Friday between 1:30 and 2:30, dorm A laundry.

**The scene:** she's sitting on dryer 14 with her hoodie inside, wet. The coin slot has swallowed her coins again. Each command does something different:

| You say | What happens | Her reaction |
|---|---|---|
| 動いて | A normal sixty-minute cycle | "Okay, cool." She goes to bed. +1 |
| 温めて | Very hot. The hoodie shrinks one size. | She wears it anyway for a week, and you can see it on her model. +1 |
| 回って | Spins with no heat | "...you're doing this on purpose." +0 |
| 出して | Pops the door and spits out her hoodie and eleven coins other people lost over the years | She splits the coins with him. +2 |
| もう一回 + 出して (later) | Spits out every sock lost in dryer 14 since 1990 | She sits in the pile. "This is the best ticket I've ever had." +2 |
| ゆっくり + 温めて (later) | A perfect gentle dry | She stays and talks while it runs. +2 |

**Result:** the 2:00 laundry becomes the one open time where nobody else is around.

**If you miss it:** after three weeks she kicks the dryer herself and closes the ticket. The laundry stays an open time. Only the scene is gone.

### Beat 2: margins (step 2, from week 3)

**Needs:** step 2, and at least two tickets filed from B2.

**What happens:** your ticket forms come back with her edits. She fixes the Japanese before HR's clerk sees it and leaves a note in English in the margin. Every third ticket you file on a B2 machine comes back with a note, and about half of them are tips:
- "copier jams less if you open the side panel first, Mori's trick"
- "Kyūyon hates Mondays, don't restart before 10"
- "lift 3 skips 4, everyone knows, don't try"

It's a steady hint system that grows with your tickets. The lift note sets up her payoff.

### The turn: the request (step 2 with 14 points; from 1 November, or 1 December without the envelope)

**Needs:**
- Step 2 and 14 points.
- A Monday, Tuesday or Friday between 19:00 and 21:00 during her night build, at her desk.
- The date: from 1 November with `mio_envelope_seen`, otherwise from 1 December, when she brings it up herself.

**What you do:** walk past her desk during the night build. She has a document open, and she minimises it. Talk to her.

**The scene, beat by beat:**
1. She doesn't look up. She needs someone to read something "who won't have opinions about it". It's her request to HR, in keigo, three paragraphs. She explains the renewal and the 15 March date in two sentences, without looking at him.
2. She asks him to proofread it. He can't read keigo, and she knows. She wants to read it aloud once to someone, and he's the one person on the island who can't judge her grammar.
3. **Proofreading.** Her draft is shown line by line, Japanese over English. On each line, tap one word you recognise, such as a greeting, a polite ending you've heard from Ishibashi, or お願いします. She corrects each tap ("no, that's right, that's... okay it's the right one, I was just checking"). By the fifth line she's correcting her own draft and reading faster. There's no fail state.
4. She saves it as 「最終」, then as 「最終2」.

**Choices at the end:**

| Choice | What happens | Leads to |
|---|---|---|
| Offer to go with her to HR | "You'll bow wrong. ...Maybe come to the lift." | `mio_lift`, which gives the full payoff scene |
| Say you'll tell Emi so she can back it | "Don't. Emi will make it a project." If you tell Emi anyway, Emi backs it in writing. | `mio_told_emi`: the request is certain, and Mio stays guarded (see Result and Endings) |
| Say nothing and share her Tuesday melon bread | She lets you | Step 3 with no flag |

**Result:**
- **Step 3.**
- **Access:** B2 at any hour on his card, and Kyūyon's admin notes. They're her English notes on the 1994 manuals and add results in the machine room (see Machines).
- **Her address:** エリック in front of the team too, unless `mio_told_emi`, in which case only when they're alone.
- Margin notes now come on every ticket.

**If you never get there:** if the turn hasn't played by 15 February, she writes the request alone. See the "one more year" ending.

### The payoff: the fourth floor (step 3 with 24 points; 5 January to 12 March)

**Needs:**
- Step 3 and 24 points.
- A weekday morning between 9:30 and 11:00, HQ lift 3.
- 止まって (you've had it since day 1 or 5 October).
- `mio_lift` for the full scene.

**What you do:** she texts "going up. lift." Meet her at lift 3 on B2.

**The scene, beat by beat:**
1. HR is on the fourth floor. Lift 3 has skipped the fourth floor since the 1998 renovation (her margin note, and Mori's lift 3 binder if you have it). Everyone rides to five and walks down. Today she's holding the envelope with her request inside, and she doesn't press anything.
2. The lift climbs. At the point where it should pass 4:
   - **止まって:** it stops at the fourth floor for the first time in years, with the shimmer and the hum. The doors open on HR. She looks at him, then gets out.
   - **待って:** it stops between 3 and 4 and waits. She laughs, and they ride on to five and walk down the stairs together.
   - **Nothing:** they ride to five and she walks down alone.
3. A few minutes later she comes back out of HR, very red. "They said thank you. In keigo. Like, a lot of keigo."

**Result:**
- **Step 4.**
- **Perk:** she takes one tech ticket off your board each week. The lowest-priority B2 ticket disappears on Monday. The first one comes with a margin note: "took this one. you don't get all of them". That's the moment she stops treating him as her replacement.
- **Reward picture** (private mode only): "mio-lift", concept to be made. Not described here (public repo).
- **Lift 3:** after 止まって, it stops at the fourth floor for everyone from now on. It's lift 3's first new trick. Nanami, its keeper, asks who fixed it (+1 with her).

**Without `mio_lift`:** she walks past your desk with the envelope and says, "Okay. Going." She comes back with two coffees, and you get step 4 without the lift scene. The reward picture moves to step 5.

### Step 5: the Wednesday train (step 4; Wednesdays from 6 January to 24 February)

**Needs:**
- Step 4.
- A Wednesday between 17:00 and 17:30 in B2, as she's leaving.
- Words taught: よろしくおねがいします (day 1) and two food words from anywhere (the canteen's menu board is the easy place).

**What you do:** she stops at your desk with her coat on and asks if you want to carry the pickles back. That means going with her tonight to her mother's town, forty minutes by monorail and a local train across the bay, and taking the first train back on Thursday: the 8:30, the train from day 1. Saying "not tonight" moves the offer to the next Wednesday.

**The scene, beat by beat:**
1. The monorail at dusk, going the other way from day 1. She's asleep by the second stop.
2. Her mother's house. The front room is a small pickle business: crocks along the wall, an order book by the phone. Her mother talks fast, all in Japanese. Words Eric knows come out clear, and the rest is soft gibberish. Mio translates about a third of it and makes up the rest.
3. Dinner. Her mother asks Mio something with 外人 in it. Mio answers with something short, and her mother laughs.
4. **The key choice.** After dinner, her mother turns to Eric, bows the formal way, and says よろしくおねがいします, the phrase Mio taught him on the train on day 1. From a mother across her own table, it's how you'd greet someone joining the family. Mio goes very still. It's the only line her mother says all evening that Eric understands without help.
   - **Bow and say it back, properly:** Mio says nothing on the walk to the station. On the platform she puts her hand in his coat pocket, because hers are full of hand warmers. Flag `mio_partner`.
   - **Say "we work together" in English and smile:** Mio translates it word for word, for once, and her mother nods and writes him into the order book as a customer: "エリック, Thursdays, the sour ones." Flag `mio_friend`.
5. The first train back, Thursday 8:30. She sits next to him on the far bench. On the partner path her headphones stay in her bag the whole way.

**Result:**
- **Step 5**, friend or partner.
- **Reward picture** (private mode, partner path): "mio-crocks", concept to be made.
- **Pickles:** her mother's pickles are in your bag every Thursday on both paths. Everyone on the team likes them except Kenji.

**Missable:** the window closes on 24 February. After that, Mio stays at step 4 and the Thursday train is still friendlier.

## Tips

- Take the day-1 lunch with her: it's a command and 3 points.
- The four Kyūyon rungs in October do most of step 2 for you.
- Ask about the envelope in week 1. It moves her turn a month earlier.
- Keep 全部 + 送って for the winter relapse. It's the funniest thing in B2, and it's worth waiting for.
- Get ゆっくり from Sumi before the dryer ticket if you can. The gentle dry is the best version of that scene.
