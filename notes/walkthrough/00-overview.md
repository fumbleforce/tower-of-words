# Walkthrough: overview

A player's guide to the half-year on the island, written before the game exists so it can be built from. Design only, nothing approved. It follows GUIDE.md, notes/RELATIONSHIPS.md (the bond rules) and notes/ISLAND.md (machines, places, tickets). If this guide and those docs disagree, those docs win until Jørgen decides.

Files:
- 00-overview.md: this file. It has the calendar, commands and words, the machines, and how to read the character files.
- One file per full arc: mio.md, mori.md, emi.md, rei.md, kaori.md, yuzuki.md, nanami.md, kuro.md.

## The basics

- Eric is a support engineer on a six-month contract (1 October 2026 to 31 March 2027), hired to keep the island's ancient systems running because the company won't replace anything that still works. The old machines answer to his voice. Nobody explains why, and nobody investigates.
- Day 1 is Thursday 1 October (game3d/story/STORY.md). Emi is at head office in Tokyo that day, arguing for a parts budget for the old machines (Jørgen, 2026-09-28), and comes back on Monday 5 October.
- A day has six periods: commute, morning, lunch, afternoon, evening, night. A day of play is 10 to 15 minutes.
- Bond steps are 0 Stranger, 1 Known, 2 Friendly (6 points), 3 Trusted (14 points plus the turn scene), 4 Close (24 points plus the payoff scene), 5 Partner or friend (the romance beat, before the end of February). A person gains at most 3 points a day. Point sources are in RELATIONSHIPS.md.
- Dated events happen on their dates whatever your bonds are. Your bond decides your part in them.
- English is always shown. Nothing asks you to recall a word. "Words taught" in a requirement means you've been taught them in play, from anyone.

## How to read a character file

Each step or beat is an entry with:
- **Needs**: bond step and points, words or commands, the date window, the place, the period, and flags from earlier scenes.
- **What you do**: the actions, in order.
- **The scene**: who is there, what happens, the choices and where each one leads. Only a few sample lines, to set the voice.
- **Result**: commands, access, items, reward pictures (private mode only, GUIDE), and changes to other people.
- **Missable**: what can be lost for good, and what comes back later.
- **Tips**.

Flags are written like `mio_envelope_seen`. They're names for the builders, not text the player sees.

## Commands and words

Twelve commands in the whole game, each an easy verb in the て form, and four words that change any command. Each works on every old machine and gives a different result on each. The phone card for each machine records what you've tried there.

| Command | Reading | Meaning | Learned from | When |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 待って | matte | wait | Mio | Day 1, train doors |
| 開けて | akete | open | Hamada | Day 1, gate |
| 動いて | ugoite | move, work | Mori | Day 1, B2 copier |
| 止まって | tomatte | stop | Mio | Day 1 lunch in the machine room, or Monday 5 October at the rack alarm |
| 入れて | irete | put in, fill | Mori | Day 1 lunch in the kitchenette, or his first three o'clock tea |
| 出して | dashite | put out | Kenji | His step 3 (the campaign), October or November |
| 休んで | yasunde | rest | Mori | His turn (the council copies), from 9 November |
| 送って | okutte | send | Rei | Her turn (a month-end night), from late November |
| 温めて | atatamete | warm up | Kaori | Her turn (the button at 6 a.m.), from November |
| 回って | mawatte | turn | Goro | His step 3 (the bees), from week 5 |
