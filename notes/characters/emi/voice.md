# Emi: voice

Who Emi is lives in her [bio](../../character-backgrounds/emi.md) and [cast.md](../../../docs/game/cast.md#emi-emi): thirty-two, B2's team lead, native British English. Mio sent her the request for a real hardware engineer, and Emi argued it all the way up the chain until head office brought Eric to the island. On day 1 she spends the whole day upstairs fighting for the parts budget and comes down at 17:40. Her swimming route is in [cast.md](../../../docs/game/cast.md#emi-joining-without-running-it). The general dialogue rules are in [VOICE.md](../../../game3d/story/VOICE.md). This page is how she sounds. Nothing in it is in the game yet.

## How she talks

Emi talks quickly in complete sentences, as if she has a meeting in ten minutes, and she usually does. She gives the good news first and folds the problem in afterwards, often with "well" or "the only thing is": "I got it. Well, I may have told them B2 can keep every machine on this island running for another ten years." The problem always comes out in the end, after the good news.

Her English is British and a bit understated. Things are "a bit tight" or "not ideal", she "ought to" go upstairs, she asks whether he "fancies" the pool, and the time is "half seven". She says "Right." to reset a conversation when she has decided what happens next, and she says "sorry" by reflex, often when nothing was her fault. She jokes at her own expense, mostly about how organised she is and how many things she has agreed to.

She speaks Japanese well and uses it with everyone on the island who isn't Eric: polite です and ます at work, 森さん with Mori, quick requests like 〜お願いできますか to the attendant or a club member. With Eric she speaks English. When someone speaks Japanese to him she translates the first time, quickly and with a bit of comment ("He says he ordered too much. He always says that, and then he orders more."), and she leaves the next line for him to try.

## What she wants and avoids

She wants B2 to keep its budget and its people, and she wants the ten years she promised upstairs to come true, or at least not to fall apart while she is in the room. Away from work she wants one evening a week when nobody hands her a list. She joined the swimming club for that, and within a fortnight she was the one who knew who had the keys.

She avoids saying no to anyone upstairs, giving bad news first, and sitting still while there is a sheet being passed round. She isn't good at asking for things for herself. When she does, it comes out sounding like a request from a meeting, and she notices and laughs about it.

## How she treats people at work

Emi is Eric's team lead, and she is five years younger than him. She treats him as the expert she fought to hire, asks what he can actually do, and doesn't pretend to understand hardware. She asks rather than orders, and her requests usually have the deadline built in: "Could you have a look before Monday's meeting?" She would much rather hear that something still fails than promise upstairs that it's fixed, and she thanks him for bad news.

She is fond of Mori and translates for him gladly. She lets Mio be blunt with her and admits Mio is right. Kuro is her friend, and Kuro is the one who comes up to the office on Saturday evenings to make her leave.

## Under stress

- She says "Right." and starts organising, even things that aren't hers to organise.
- She picks up whatever is being handed round, such as a clipboard, the keys or a bag, before anyone has asked her.
- She answers a question before it has finished and then realises what she has agreed to.
- She pushes her glasses up with one finger and talks a little faster.
- When she is caught out, she reports it like a meeting item ("For the minutes, I did not see that coming"), and then she laughs.

## Eric at the start and later

On day 1 she is the boss who wasn't there all day. She comes down from upstairs bright and apologetic, sells him the promise she has just made on his behalf, and books a proper chat for the morning. On day 2 she is practical, wants to know what he can really look after, and admits she got a bit carried away with the ten years. She uses his first name from the start.

Later she comes down to B2 when she doesn't need anything, which Mio told her to do at the welcome dinner. At the club she sits beside him and leaves her work bag under the bench. She asks him to remind her she came to swim when she starts collecting names. She tells him what upstairs actually said, the bad part included. Further on she asks him to supper with their work phones put away, and she is the one who brings it up.

## Example lines

1. A few weeks in, at B2. Eric tells her the backup drive on one of the old servers is failing.
   "Well, it ran until Tuesday, so we've got everything up to Tuesday. What would a new one cost, roughly? I'd like to know before someone upstairs asks me."
2. On the phone to head office, with Eric waiting at her door.
   "Yes, it's in hand. Well, it's in someone's hand. I'll ring you back at four." She covers the phone. "Sorry. Come in."
3. The welcome dinner. Mori says something to Eric in Japanese.
   "He says he's ordered too much. He always says that, and then he orders more."
4. At the pool. Kuro asks her whether she came to swim or to carry bags.
   "I know, I'm getting in. I'm just putting these somewhere first, and then I'm getting in."
5. B2, ten to eight in the evening.
   "I was going to leave at six. Then someone asked me a quick question, and I said yes before I'd heard what it was."
6. Eric brings her a repair that didn't work.
   "Thank you. That's much easier to take upstairs than something that's fixed until Monday."
7. At the club, later in the route. Her work phone buzzes in the bag under the bench.
   "That's staying under there. If anyone asks, you didn't hear it."
8. To the attendant, then to Eric.
   「すみません、鍵、ここに置いておきますね。」 (Excuse me, I'll leave the keys here.) "There. That's my job done for tonight, officially."

## Lines she would not say

- "Good leaders know when to let go." She doesn't speak in slogans, and she would never call herself a leader out loud.
- "I'm so stressed, I never get any time for myself." She never says how she feels about it. She says she was going to leave at six.
- "Just do it, and don't argue with me." That's how Rei talks. Emi asks, and puts the deadline in the question.
