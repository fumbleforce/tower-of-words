# Kuro's relationship line

A plan for issue #357. It replaces the swimming-club plan in [cast.md](../../../docs/game/cast.md#kuro-being-recognised-away-from-the-counter), which was written before her current personality. Who she is lives in her [bio](../../character-backgrounds/kuro.md) and how she talks in her [voice sheet](voice.md). How points and steps work is in [systems.md](../../../docs/game/systems.md#bonds). Kisses, dates and sex belong only on this line, gated on her step, never on a day. The private scenes plug into step 4 and are written separately.

Kuro wants to be wanted and to decide what happens next. Eric gets close to her by speaking Japanese to her, noticing what she wears, answering her personal questions plainly, and now and then disagreeing with her. If he only ever agrees, she gets bored. Her line runs from the reception counter to the pool, the izakaya and her own dining table, and every invitation on it comes from her.

## Step 1: one more foreigner

Earned on day 3, when she gives him her name. Before that she is the receptionist he passes each morning. On day 2 an English greeting gets "Good evening." and nothing else, and a Japanese one gets her brushing the dust off his sleeve and teaching him あなた. She remembers which of the two he chose.

## Step 2: she chooses his seat

She reaches it at 6 points. Points come from greeting her in polite Japanese every working morning, using the words she taught him back to her, saying something about her suit, giving his own opinion when she asks for it (she asks and takes his answer, as with the label printer), and turning up at Saturday swimming. Walking past the counter without a word costs nothing, but she says so the next morning ("今日は言ってくれないんですか").

The scene is the built Saturday one at the gym after swimming, rewritten. She no longer asks which seat he wants. She pats the one beside hers by the window and tells him to sit there. Then she asks him a personal question, 「ノルウェーに、待ってる人、いるんですか。」, and watches his face. If he answers plainly, her next question is more personal. If he gets flustered, she asks a second one. From now on she touches his arm when she talks to him at work.

## Step 3: six o'clock at the pool

At 14 points, and once Eric knows 私, あなた, 六時 and 二人, the counter scene from her [sample](sample.md) plays on a working evening. She points at the clock, says 明日、六時, writes 18:00 プール on a note and says 二人, 私とあなた. Eric can agree to six or point at the seven on the clock because he works late. If he points at seven, she argues once (「七時？ ……六時のほうが、すいてますよ。」), then crosses out the time and writes his. She likes him more for it.

The scene ends at the pool the next evening, where she sets the pace and swims beside him. It is not a date yet. This replaces the built equipment scene, which was written for the earlier Kuro who wanted to stop being treated as staff.

The Japanese focus is the time and people the booking needs: 六時, 二人 and 大丈夫, all heard, typed and used in one exchange. 七時 shows clear because he points at the clock as she says it.

## Step 4: Friday night

At 24 points, at the counter on a working morning, she tells him: 「金曜の夜、空けておいてください。お店は私が予約しますから。」 This is the friendship-or-romance choice, and it is hers to offer and his to answer.

He can accept, and she says she will tell him the time later. He can ask for Saturday instead, and she argues once (「土曜？ ……金曜のほうがいいんですけど。」) and then takes it (「じゃあ、土曜。私が予約し直します。」). Either of those is a date. Or he can say no calmly and go to work. That throws her. She answers in her reception voice as if he were a visitor (「かしこまりました。いってらっしゃいませ。」), and the next morning she takes control back by setting something else: 「土曜日、泳ぎましょう。そのあと一杯。お店は私が決めます。」 That keeps the line going as a friendship.

On the date path the public scene is the dinner at the izakaya on the shop street. She orders his second drink before he has finished the first, and asks what he noticed about her that evening. Near the end she slips into plain form once, still with the さん on his name. She doesn't say she likes him. She tells him what they are doing next Friday: 「来週の金曜も、ここ。……空けといて。」

The Japanese focus is ordering: もう一杯, 同じの and すみません, which she says for both of them until he says one himself.

The answer is saved in a new flag, `romance_kuro`, which only this choice sets.

**Private:** on the date answer, the private scenes for step 4 open: the booking at the counter and the date that follows it. Both are written separately and gated on step 4 and that answer.

## Step 5: her table

Step 4 and this scene. On a Friday or Saturday evening she invites him to her apartment on the east side. Clothes cover every surface in the bedroom behind a half-open door, but the dining table is clear, with takeaway on proper plates and a bottle of wine she chose. She has an old romantic drama on and complains about what the heroine decides. Eric can agree with her or tell her the heroine was right. She likes it better when he disagrees, and she says so by refilling his glass.

On the date path she leaves the さん off his name for good when they are alone, and she tells him about the seafront apartment she wants one day, the first time she has told anyone from work. She talks about the place and never the saving: 「いつか、海の見える部屋に住むの。ベランダで、こうやって食べたいな。」 On the friendship path she invites him the same way, as her one guest who argues back about the drama, and she still decides what they eat. With him she keeps です/ます and his さん, and she goes plain only when she complains at the screen (「なんでそっち選ぶの。」).

The Japanese focus is plain-form Japanese. On the date path she switches from です/ます to plain form with him. On either path the player hears the same few things said both ways.

## After step 5

At work she stays polite and touches his arm. If he doesn't answer a message she may send something cruel late at night and treat him like a visitor the next morning. If he comes in laughing with someone, she asks who it was.
