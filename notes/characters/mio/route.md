# Mio's relationship line

A plan for issue #357. Nothing here is built beyond the step-2 and step-3 lunch scenes. Who she is lives in her [bio](../../character-backgrounds/mio.md) and how she talks in her [voice sheet](voice.md). How points and steps work is in [systems.md](../../../docs/game/systems.md#bonds). Kisses, dates and sex belong only on this line, gated on her step, never on a day. The private scenes plug into steps 4 and 5 and are written separately.

Mio wants to be left alone with her games and to keep her servers off other people's lists. Eric gets close to her by leaving her alone first, asking before he touches her racks, sending a message instead of turning up, and asking real questions about the games she plays. Her line moves from the machine room to her own room, and the last thing she lets him see is the tactics game she has shown nobody.

## Step 1: the new guy

Earned on day 1 on the train, when they introduce themselves. Catching her lunch bag and eating lunch with her in the machine room both count, and she remembers them. She calls him {gaijin} until he has eaten lunch with her once. After that she uses his name.

## Step 2: a space at the rack

She reaches it at 6 points. Points come from greeting her in casual Japanese (she said ohayō without the gozaimasu is fine), coffee from the machine, asking before he opens a rack, and staying quiet while she works. The built lunch scene marks it, on weekdays from day 5 in the machine room. She moves a cable so he can sit beside the rack and lets him stay while she eats. She still answers his questions with work, and she still leaves his messages for a day.

## Step 3: she goes out for lunch

At 14 points, the built scene plays at weekday lunch. She is checking a front vent before she eats. Eric offers to check it or tells her to go and eat, and she hands him her checklist for that one row. She comes back and says she actually got outside, "like before lunch was over. That never happens." Afterwards she eats at the east-coast bench on weekdays, and she starts texting him first, in lower case with no full stops.

## Step 4: the last boss

At 24 points, on a Saturday or Sunday evening, she texts him first: "theres a boss i lost to all week / need someone who doesnt talk the whole time / you free saturday". If he replies, he can come to her dorm room. If he turns up without replying, she opens the door halfway ("Ah... I texted you, you know. You could just answer. I'm not really dressed for people, so... next weekend, okay?") and the scene waits for next weekend.

In her room she gives him the folding chair and a spare controller, and she explains the boss the way she explains a word: what it does, when it does it, and one joke at his expense. Her work phone buzzes in the middle of the fight. It is a routine alarm on a server that is still running. She looks at it, puts it face down and says it can go on tomorrow's list. It is the first time Eric has seen her leave a working machine alone.

They win, or they lose and she wants one more try. Then comes the choice. Eric can say he will come back next weekend for the next boss, which keeps it a friendship. Or he can tell her he didn't come for the boss. She goes quiet, then answers in one loose run-on sentence that makes it clear she means yes: "Mm. ...Okay. Then next Saturday also, and, um, we don't have to play anything, so..." On the friendship answer she sends him home before midnight, because she has a match online. On the romance answer she doesn't mention the match.

The Japanese focus is game talk she says out loud while playing: 右, 左, まだ, もう一回 and 次. The player hears each one with the action on screen and types もう一回 when they want the retry.

The answer is saved in a new flag, `romance_mio`, which only this choice sets.

**Private:** on the romance answer, the private scene for step 4 opens (her first time). It is written separately and is gated on step 4 and that answer.

## Step 5: the game nobody has seen

Step 4 and this scene. On a weekend evening after step 4, she texts him: "come over later / not talking about work tho". With the game she plays closed, there is a second window she has always minimised when someone came in. This time she opens it herself. It is her tactics game: the combat runs, the art is grey boxes. She asks him to play the first map and say honestly what is wrong with it, and she argues with every note he gives. Then she writes them down.

On the friendship path, he becomes the one person who plays her builds, and she sends him a new one every few weeks with no message attached. On the romance path, the same scene ends with her bringing her mother's pickles out of the fridge, the thing she always complains about, and offering them: "My mother sent too much again, like always. Eat some, okay? ...I'm not doing my match tonight, so you can stay." When she says yes to something that matters, she does it by giving up a game. Friendship players get one more chance here to say he would like more than that, with the same clear answer as step 4, and that answer sets `romance_mio` too.

The Japanese focus is the game's own menu, in katakana: スタート, セーブ, ユニット, ターン. Her menu is the first place where the player reads katakana for something they want to do.

**Private:** on the romance path, the private scene for step 5 hangs here (the second time).

## After step 5

She still leaves his messages for a day or two and is surprised when he minds. She still guards her racks. She brings enough food for two on the days her mother has sent too much.
