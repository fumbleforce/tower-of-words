# Days 3 to 5: cold read

Claude's cold read of [days3-5-outline.md](days3-5-outline.md) (#225, commit 2f914418), as a first-time reader who has played days 1 and 2. Checked against Jørgen's brief of 2026-10-05 (C-0442), [progression.md](../docs/game/progression.md), [setting.md](../docs/game/setting.md), [cast.md](../docs/game/cast.md), the day-2 storylines and story files, [VOICE.md](../game3d/story/VOICE.md) and GUIDE (Writing, Learning rules, no text on timers).

The shape is right. Each day has one major scene and two small tickets, nothing is lost by skipping, the board is the one way into clubs, and Kotodama comes in at B2 after six, which is where its own pitch sets it. Day 2's end carries over cleanly: Eric's "I should hang one up for Monday" already sets up a weekend. Three things block building: who sees the kotodama on day 5, a door check that contradicts itself, and what moves the clock on a free day.

## Blocking

1. **Day 5, B2 after six: the team sees the kotodama with no setup.** Setting says Eric keeps it quiet and the people who see it do too; Mio asked him to on day 1, and the outline's own day 3 treats saying `matte` in front of the guard as a choice. On day 5, Kenji invites Eric to "try serving the team's drinks" and Mio "does not want a demonstration", so both already know what he can do. Nothing says how Kenji knows. Kotodama also throws cans across the room, and a swapped particle puts a person out of the machine. That is the biggest reveal in the game so far, and here it happens inside a minigame entrance. Fix: decide who knows by day 5 and stage it. Option A: Kenji has seen the B2 machine come unstuck for Eric ([vending-gift](../docs/game/stories/vending-gift.md)) and puts it down to Eric having a knack with old machines, the way Mori talks to the copier. Nobody calls it magic, and Mio's stake in the scene is keeping it that way. Option B: the team seeing it is the day's turn, written as one (Mio's reaction is about the secret, not her papers). That ties into [mio-notices](../docs/game/stories/mio-notices.md) and #226. Either works, but the outline has to choose one.

2. **Day 3, the door sign-off contradicts itself.** In step 2 the door "begins to close before the guard has collected the test control", in both report branches, and in step 4 the station accepts the repair. A door closing on an object in its threshold is the sensor fault that is supposedly being checked. On the pass branch this shows Eric's report was wrong; on the replacement branch it shows the new sensor doesn't work. The guard, who is precise, would not sign it off. Fix: give the close a reason that has nothing to do with the sensor. For example, after the check passes the guard steps inside for his control just as the car is called back into service. The doors close normally because nobody is in the doorway, and the guard is about to leave with the train. Eric holds it with `matte` or the hold button. Nothing reflects on the repair, and the stakes are easy to see.

3. **Nothing says what moves the clock on a free day.** "Choosing to spend a period on work, a club session or free time advances it". Day 3 has a free lunch and afternoon, and the swimming meeting is in the evening, so a player who does the station and one ticket can't get to evening unless some choice moves the time. It doesn't say whether a ticket costs a period, what "free time" is as something you choose, or where you choose it. Fix: add one short table saying what spends a period on days 3 to 5 (the station visit? each ticket? a club session) and the one ordinary place to let time pass (a bench, or Eric's room: "Spend the afternoon here"). "A day that skips all work must still reach evening and sleep" then holds. Every day depends on this, so it has to be settled before building.

## Should fix

4. **Day 3's major scene is the third door scene in three days, and it plays like day 2's epilogue.** It is the same carriage, door and sensor, and the same `matte` choice as day 2's optional test and day 1's platform. The brief asks these days to open up the clubs, yet the outline itself calls the swimming club's first meeting "deliberately small" because the station scene takes the major slot. Fix: make the sign-off a simple scene (the check, the hold, the guard signs) that closes T-0002, and let the board with Aoi plus the first swimming meeting be day 3's major scene. If the station stays major, build it around the guard rather than the door (see 15).

5. **The swimming club never swims.** The pool is outdoor and open only in summer (island.md), so in October the club meets in the gym for "dry training". A player who picks swimming gets a gym bench. Fix: make day 3 the club's last pool session of the year, before the pool is covered for winter. Japanese school and company clubs mark this. It gives the club a real reason to be at the water, the goggles and summer-photo find get a payoff, and the gym corner takes over from the next week. It does mean building the pool deck and pavilion now rather than later (see the build list). If that is too much for this pass, ask Jørgen whether he wants swimming in October before building a swimming club that meets in a gym.

6. **The tickets are too alike, and none uses a command.** Of six jobs, four come down to changing a setting in a menu (label format, single input, fit to page, repeat setting) and one is setting a date. Progression says tickets "often put an old machine in front of him (the kotodama)", but no ticket here offers a known word. Fix: vary the repair verbs and give at least one ticket a day an optional known command next to the ordinary fix. Examples: the karaoke terminal that runs past the row stops on `tomatte`; the frozen gym terminal starts on `ugoite`. Keep the ordinary fix as the other way, as day 3's hold button does.

7. **The Japanese doesn't ramp evenly.** Day 2 taught a pattern (-tai). Days 3 and 4 each add one word (`koko`, `isshoni`), which is flat. Day 5 then piles up a verb that has never been taught (`dashite`, which words.md lists as unused), two particles, a name with さん and katakana コーラ, which is Jørgen's weak script. Fix: teach `dashite` on day 3 or 4 at a machine. "Print it out" is a natural 出して, so a printer ticket (D3-B or D5-A) could do it, and then day 5 adds only に and を. Also say whether `koko` and `isshoni` are typed. GUIDE: a new word is learned by typing its romaji.

8. **Monday afternoon puts Mori and Kenji in club places during work.** "People have returned to their workday places", yet Mori is in the dorm common room and Kenji at karaoke in the afternoon, then they "leave for B2 before evening". This clashes with their routines (Mori's afternoon corn soup on B2). Fix: move both visits to lunch, or have a club member be the requester in the afternoon while Mori or Kenji drop by at lunch.

9. **No one says where each person is on each day.** The outline promises weekend attendance "written for each person rather than copied from Friday" but never writes it. #226 needs it to hang milestones on, and the build needs it to place people. As written, Emi has one greeting, Mio has only the Kotodama scene, and Hamada and Tama don't appear at all. Mio's stake from day 2 is the bigger gap: she told the station it was the sensor, and on the pass branch said "I still want to know what happened". T-0002 now closes on Saturday without her. Fix: add a short table (person by day and period, place, what they're doing) and one reaction from Mio after the sign-off, by text if she isn't there.

10. **Aoi and Rei aren't ready to be written.** Neither has a VOICE.md entry. Rei's language isn't decided, and day 4 step 3 depends on it: she "notices she has used three different terms he cannot follow". If she speaks Japanese, he follows none of it and there's no joke. If she has some English, that is a cast fact to record first. "Their day-2 encounters" don't exist either: Rei is in no storyline, and Aoi was only overheard on day 1's train, under the label "Woman on her phone". The board on day 3 is Aoi's first real meeting, so it should pay off her day-1 call ("どこでもいいけど、地下はいや"): where did she end up? Fix: add both to VOICE.md and cast.md, decide Rei's English, cut the day-2 reference, and say which slip Aoi takes (tennis, to match day 4).

11. **The pending-scene chooser is a new system.** "If several major scenes have accumulated, the player chooses one for the day and the others remain pending." Jørgen asked for few systems. Fix: each scene stays at its place and time (the station on any morning, tennis on any Sunday evening, B2 on any weekday evening). Walking there offers it. Drop the per-day cap for scenes the player starts, so no chooser is needed. The "budget one substantial scene a day" rule in progression.md applies to what the game offers, not to what the player chooses to walk into.

12. **Day 3 has too much in it.** It brings free periods with costs, the explicit sleep choice, the board, membership, the first club meeting, the major scene, two tickets and two finds. Progression already puts two systems on day 3 (free periods and the board). Fix: move one ticket to day 4. D3-B also suits a weekday better (see 17). Say that the first free-period choice is the one before evening, so the day has a single new decision about time.

13. **Who tells Eric about the Saturday check?** Day 3 starts with "the station's confirmation ready", but nobody says so. Day 2 used Mio's text. Fix: name the message (from Mio, or the guard through the ticket) and make it say Saturday morning is fine, so the player isn't left wondering whether work happens at weekends.
