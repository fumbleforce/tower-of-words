## Recommended order

1. Finish the selected bakery pass and sports/north-street grounds pass. Sports originals show a current visible gap; bakery has an agent's fresh baseline. Demand matched player views, close views and phone clearance, not just an aerial prettiness shot.
2. Karaoke reception, then booth; harbour quays/loading/landing, then residual Works plant/factory frontage. These have clear fixture or ground-treatment opportunities without replacing good finished rooms.
3. East lane/pocket park, remaining office rows/north courts, onsen approach/dorm-row seams. Review existing originals first, then capture only missing/current views.
4. Close the coverage gap across older polished areas: train, gate, B2 office, lift, head-office lobby, dorm upper/common spaces, canteen, print shop, konbini, terminal and pool. “Unverified” is a review task first, not automatic permission to fill rooms with props.
5. Keep genuinely unbuilt areas separate: far northern island, clinic/training/bank/other office interiors, remaining shop interiors, onsen, harbour/Works shells and canteen upper/kitchen. Existing place facts explicitly preserve those limits; neither planting nor a single interior completes them.
