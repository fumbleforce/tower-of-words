Method: story-sense (state diagnosis), story-analysis, scene-sequencing (goal → conflict → disaster; reaction → dilemma → decision), key-moments, character-arc, dialogue (text, subtext, context). Read from the branch transcripts in game/notes/transcripts/.

## State
The week is at story-sense state 4.5 / 5 ("plot without pacing / without purpose" in places): scenes mostly work on their own, and the spine (probation, the closure memo, Monday's announcement) is sound, but day one breaks the rules of setup and continuity, which spoils the magic reveal and makes the world feel stitched together.

## Setup and payoff
1. **The magic is never shown before it's used or mentioned (day 1).** The first "use kotodama" option appears at Rei's desk with no prior scene, and in the late branch a narration line jokes about "whatever magic you have" before the player has seen any. Fix: a private setup beat in the basement office. Emi mentions the coffee machine that only works if you hit it; later the player is alone with it, hitting it does nothing, and a whispered 動いて makes it pour. Nobody sees. This teaches the verb form (te-form request) and the secret at once, and pays off at Rei's desk and in day 2's copier scene.
2. **Characters greet the player as a returning visitor on the player's first visit.** Day 2: Kaori (「また来たね」) and Goro (「おや、また来たね」) both assume the player came on Monday, but day 1 lunch is either canteen or rooftop, never both. Fix: neutral greetings.
3. **Mio's arc skips a step.** Day 1 lunch: two hours after 「話しかけないで」 she invites the player to play "tonight", which never happens; day 2 she invites them again for the weekend. Fix: day 1 ends with a softer opening (「今度、対戦しよ」), so the day 2 invitation is the real step.

## Continuity (place, people, time)
4. **Teleport, day 1, Rei → basement.** After the sales scene the next line is Emi in the basement office with no transition. Fix: a short transition beat (back down in the elevator) at the start of office2 with a time step.
5. **Teleport, day 1, the bar.** Ishibashi's "You again. Card." happens inside the bar scene by swapping the background to the gate and back. Fix: make it an evening beat at the gate first (leaving the building), then walk to the bar.
6. **Day 4: Emi appears before she's back.** She's "out until five", but the finish scene has no time or arrival beat. Fix: set the clock after five and narrate her return.
