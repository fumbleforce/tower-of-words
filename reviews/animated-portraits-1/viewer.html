Animated dialogue portraits: Mio and Kuro

A small idle loop on the approved neutral portrait, shown where the game shows it: the dialogue stage at its real size, desktop 1366x860 or phone 390x844, over a game capture (the train for Mio, the office for Kuro). "Still" is the game today (the picture with its CSS breathing). "Video" is Wan 2.2 VACE driven by a skeleton, cut out frame by frame. "Puppet" moves the picture's own pixels in Three.js. Nothing here is in the game.
