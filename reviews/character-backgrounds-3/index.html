Mio

Mio prefers games to small talk. She wants to finish her work and have the rest of the day to herself. She gets annoyed when people interrupt her. She has little patience for someone who keeps talking after she has stopped answering.

She works as B2's programmer and looks after the old servers. She knows what she is doing. She can also be rude to someone who knows less. Kenji sometimes gets a sarcastic answer when he needs an explanation.

Privacy matters to her. She does not want people looking over her shoulder or asking what she is doing all evening. Being alone is often exactly what she wants.

There are exceptions. She likes Mori. She has a few people she makes time for. She might invite one of them into a game she usually plays alone. She can enjoy sitting beside someone without feeling any need to talk.

She talks more about games she likes. She argues about bad decisions and gets absorbed in difficult problems. She can forget that the person beside her wanted a short answer.

Getting close to Mio takes patience. She does not reward someone for pestering her into going out. An invitation from her means she wants that person's company. It does not mean she wants company every night.

Kenji

Kenji means well and gets confused easily. He wants to help. He sometimes says yes before he understands what he has been asked to do. He often looks as though he has missed part of the conversation. When he rushes to help, he knocks things over.

He is the youngest engineer on B2. He speaks casual Japanese and tries to use his small amount of English. He likes Mori. Mio makes him nervous when she is impatient with him.

He is easy to push around. Rei can leave him trying to follow instructions he does not understand. Kuro can talk him into a favour before he has considered saying no. He might promise both of them the same afternoon.

He can make things worse by hiding a small mistake. He hopes to fix it before anyone notices. Sometimes he only succeeds in delaying the moment when someone else has to help.

Kenji is kinder than several of the people around him. He will stay with someone who is struggling. He does not need to prove that he knows more than they do. His company can be a relief after an argument with Rei or an impatient answer from Mio.

He does better when someone gives him time to ask questions. He remembers people who explain things without making him feel stupid. He may still ask them the same question twice.

Kuro

Kuro is used to getting her way. She flirts openly and enjoys making people flustered. If she fancies someone, she will ask them out. She will probably choose where they are going too.

She works at head-office reception and knows how to put a visitor at ease. Away from work, she expects more than polite conversation. She wants to be wanted. She likes deciding how far things go.

Kuro can be selfish. She expects attention she does not always return. She can get jealous before she has decided whether she wants a relationship. She sometimes uses charm to get a favour instead of asking for it.

She does not read everyone correctly. She can mistake politeness for interest. Rejection embarrasses her. She might make a cutting remark and regret it later. She can also enjoy someone who stands up to her.

Her directness can be fun. Nobody has to spend weeks guessing whether she is interested. A swim together might become a challenge. Dinner might become an argument they both enjoy. She teases her friends and likes dragging them into plans they would never make themselves.

Rei is unlikely to let Kuro make every decision. Kenji might agree before he has thought it through. Mio might simply refuse to go. She is used to persuading people. She has less patience for someone who keeps her guessing.

Rei

Rei takes charge before anyone asks her to. She makes a plan and expects people to follow it. She is brusque with strangers and can be abrasive with people she knows. She gets impatient when someone hesitates.

She works in Sales and likes a serious game of tennis. She wants to win. Her confidence can get a hesitant group moving. She will make a difficult decision while everyone else is still arguing.

She is also a poor loser. Being proved wrong in front of others makes her defensive. She might keep pushing a bad plan because changing it would mean admitting a mistake. She can blame a partner for failing to follow instructions that were wrong in the first place.

She mistakes hesitation for incompetence. She can dismiss Kenji before hearing the useful part of his answer. She is bad at asking for help. People sometimes stop offering it because they are tired of being ordered around.

Rei respects someone who can do what they claim. She can also resent needing them. She likes a capable partner. She gets angry when that partner ignores her instructions.

Kuro wants to choose what happens between people. Rei wants to decide how the job gets done. They can clash even when they want the same result.
