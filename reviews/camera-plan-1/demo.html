Controls and prototype limits

Desktop: click the scene, then WASD or arrows to walk, Shift to run, right-drag to look. Wheel zooms in free orbit. On a phone, choose a touch layout above. Follow returns behind you after walking; free orbit holds your angle. Reset view restores the chosen distance and your facing.

This demo uses the actual places and walking collision. Camera obstruction checks pull the view in near walls; the train camera stays below its roof. Rooms still have the overview's cutaway walls, and story, conversation, and pin behavior are not demonstrated. Demo progress and settings last only until this frame reloads. The game's default camera is unchanged.
