Controls and prototype limits

Desktop: click Play to capture the mouse. Move the mouse to look, WASD or arrows to move, Shift to run. The character turns toward the direction you move. Esc releases the mouse for these controls. The view follows the character at the selected distance. Walking forward keeps the camera behind your direction of travel; moving sideways or back does not make the view swing around. Reset view explicitly puts it behind the character.

This demo uses the actual places and walking collision. Camera obstruction checks pull the view in near walls; the train camera stays below its roof. Rooms still have the overview's cutaway walls, and pins and authored story transitions are not demonstrated. Scene camera frames two actual nearby characters and pauses player movement; it previews framing only, with no conversation playing. Demo progress and settings last only until this frame reloads. The game's default camera is unchanged.
