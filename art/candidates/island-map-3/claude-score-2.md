Images: .claude/worktrees/codex-island-map-3/art/candidates/island-map-3/ (07-overview, 08-west, 09-east). Checked at full size and 2x crops. The asks are Jørgen's round-2 feedback (reviews/island-map-2).

## 07-overview

- Shower building attached to pool: partial. The block is still on the deck's north edge with doors onto it, but it hasn't changed from 01 and still reads as a small office.
- Gendered onsen areas: pass. There are two bath courtyards with a blue gate and a red gate. The divider between the pools reads as a lattice screen, not a solid wall.
- Continuous, less cutesy shopping street: pass. A glass canopy now runs over a lane between two rows of shops, with signs (ramen, yakitori) facing into it. The south row is separate boxes with gaps, and their awnings face the seaside promenade, so it isn't clear whether the south side of the covered lane has shopfronts.
- Attached, clustered apartments: pass. The cluster is unchanged from 01: joined blocks, bridges and courts.
- Less idyllic: partial. The pastel cottages are still gone. The new street is lit with warm glowing windows and lanterns, and the whole island is still clean, saturated and tree-lined.
- SE stair landing: pass, by removal. The seawall stair that ended on boulders is gone, and a railing runs along the promenade. The stair south of the arcade ends on a paved slab at the sand's edge.
- Bar frontage: pass. The east end of the street is now a dark-timber izakaya with a 居酒屋 sign, blue noren and two red lanterns, facing a small paved square.
- Van on the promenade: fixed. There is no vehicle on the promenade.
- Path to the red onsen gate: partial. The gate opens onto a small paved patch with a lamp above the rocks. A lower fenced terrace runs west from it, but I can't see where it joins the promenade.

- Less idyllic: partial. It is warm-lit, clean and tidy. Parked cars and utility boxes behind the offices help a bit.

## Continuity across the three

Pass. The station, HQ, canteen, fountain, covered street with the izakaya at its east end, beach stairs, gym, pool, court, university, apartments and onsen are in the same places with the same shapes in all three. The seawall stair is removed in all views that show that coast.

## Remaining defects, worst first

1. The pool block still reads as an office, with no showers or changing rooms. That minor defect is unchanged.
2. On the covered street, the south row faces away from the covered lane toward the promenade, with gaps between units. From above, whether both sides of the lane have shops can't be told, and the south row still reads a little broken up.
3. The finish is still idyllic: bright, clean and green, with warm lantern light added to the street. There is no wear, wiring, laundry or signage clutter outside the street. The path to the red onsen gate is also still unclear.

Last round's three worst are resolved: the SE stair is removed, the bar has an izakaya frontage, and the street is covered with shops in two rows. The van is gone.
