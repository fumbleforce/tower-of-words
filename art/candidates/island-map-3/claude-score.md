# island-map-3 scorecard

Images: .claude/worktrees/codex-island-map-3/art/candidates/island-map-3/ (01-overview, 02-west, 03-east). Checked at full size and 2x crops. Asks are Jørgen's round-2 feedback (reviews/island-map-2).

## 01-overview

- Shower building attached to pool: pass. A flat-roofed block sits on the pool deck's north edge with doors onto the deck. It looks like a small office block, though, with nothing that says changing rooms or showers.
- Gendered onsen areas: pass. There are two walled bath courtyards with a wall between them, a blue curtain on the left gate and a red one on the right. The red gate faces the seaward ledge, and no path to it is clear.
- Continuous, less cutesy shopping street: partial. The pastel gabled cottages are gone, replaced by one continuous row of plain two-storey shops. The glass vault sits on the shop roofs rather than over the walkway, so the street in front is open, not an arcade, and shops line only one side.
- Attached, clustered apartments: pass. There are five mid-rise blocks joined by corridor bridges around narrow courts, with bike shelters in the courts.
- Less idyllic: partial. The cherry trees and cottages are gone, and there are now parked cars, vans, rooftop AC units and grey paving. It is still bright, saturated and spotless, with trees everywhere and no wear, stains, signs, wires or laundry.
- SE stair landing: fail. The left stair (about x1050, y830) runs down the seawall straight onto boulders, with no landing or path at the bottom. The right corner platform (about x1290, y860) also ends at the rocks.
- Bar frontage: fail. The pinned unit at the arcade's east end is a shop with a red-and-white striped awning. It reads as a cafe or grocer: no sign, lanterns, noren or night look.

## 02-west

- Shower building attached to pool: n/a (out of frame).
- Gendered onsen areas: n/a (out of frame).
- Continuous, less cutesy shopping street: partial. Same as the overview: a continuous row with a glass roof on top of the shops, the walkway open, and a kink with a second arch halfway along. A delivery van is parked on the pedestrian promenade among the planters, and no road reaches it.
- Attached, clustered apartments: n/a (out of frame).
- Less idyllic: partial. The office blocks, parking and AC units read as a working town. It still has the same clean, sunny, tree-lined finish.
- SE stair landing: n/a (out of frame). The two beach stairs here do land on sand.
- Bar frontage: fail. It is the same striped-awning unit as in the overview, still with nothing bar-like.

## 03-east

- Shower building attached to pool: pass. The same block is on the deck edge with doors onto it. As in the overview, it doesn't read as showers or changing rooms.
- Gendered onsen areas: pass. The two courtyards are split by a wall, with blue and red curtained gates. The red gate still opens onto a narrow ledge above the rocks.
- Continuous, less cutesy shopping street: partial. Only the east end is in frame: the same row with the glass roof over the shops.
- Attached, clustered apartments: pass. It matches the overview, with bridges between blocks at several floors and courts with shelters.
- Less idyllic: partial. It has the same finish as the others: clean, green and bright.
- SE stair landing: n/a. The SE stairs are out of frame. The viewpoint in frame (about x1360, y720) has a kiosk box and a doorway into the seawall that leads nowhere visible.
- Bar frontage: fail. It is the same striped awning, bottom left.

## Continuity across the three

Pass. The station, monorail, HQ with its link bridge, canteen, fountain, clock, arcade with its mid arch, beach and stairs, gym, pool block, court, university, apartment cluster and onsen are in the same places, with the same shapes and colours in all three views. I found no moved or missing landmark. The overview is clearly an edit of round 2's image (same coast, rocks, roads and office blocks), so the layout carries over.

## Three worst defects

1. The SE seawall stair (overview, about x1050, y830) ends on boulders with no landing. That is the known open item, and it is not fixed.
2. The bar has no frontage at all. It is a striped-awning shop that reads as a cafe, the same in all three images.
3. The "arcade" is a glass roof on top of the shop buildings, and the street in front is open, so the covered, continuous street he asked for is only half there. Second to this: the finish is still too clean and saturated for "less idyllic".
