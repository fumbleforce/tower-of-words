# Staging note: Mori and Hamada base portraits, round 1 (#662)

Brief, Jørgen's words (issue #662, 2026-10-16): "rework the sleeping man on the train (Hamada) and the old man in B2 (Mori) into more viable romance characters; now they read as caricatures, at least the portraits. Keep their ages." From the round brief: believable, attractive mature men a player could fall for: well-kept, real faces, expressive eyes, good posture, tasteful clothes that fit their jobs (Mori: neat cardigan or suit, warm; Hamada: navy suit, a little rumpled is fine, but handsome, not a tired cartoon).

The one change: the face words in each man's look. Mori's "a lined, kind face" and Hamada's "a thin, tired face" are what the old portraits drew as caricature (deep wrinkles, squint, gaunt cheeks). Both become a handsome, well-kept mature face with expressive dark eyes. Hair, build word, outfit and age stay as the cast Look lines have them. The portrait style preset (house style, Mio and Kuro anchors) replaces the older pre-style-align look of npc-base-1.
