# Dorm route, option dorm-route-claude

Jørgen's ask: "we dont need to build out most of the locations, just need to add the section of the island outdoors to connect our work office location to the dorms, and visualize dorms".

This option walks Eric home along the sea. He leaves head office by the front doors, passes the bike racks, goes down to the promenade, walks east past the shopping street's seaward shop fronts (the konbini is the last one), then turns up a small courtyard to the dorm door. The coin laundry and the sento sit side by side on the promenade, next to the dorm. The izakaya is on the courtyard. The covered shopping street keeps both rows, as on island-03; the walk passes its sea side instead of going through it, because the game camera looks north and from above the covered lane is mostly roofs (see attempts/04-walk.png).

The walk is about 56 game units (about 85 m), roughly 37 seconds at Eric's walking speed. No cars; bikes at head office, outside the konbini and in a shelter by the dorm.

The dorm is two blocks with a 2 m slot between them. Eric's room is on the ground floor at the back of block A, and its window looks straight at block B's bare concrete wall. Inside: a single bed, a desk under the window, a built-in closet, an air-con unit and his moving boxes.

The pictures are built from the game's own 3D code (props, surface patterns, soft baked light and the colour grade), so they look like the game, with Eric's model for scale. Time is about 18:10, just after sunset, when he'd walk home on day 1. Positions follow island-03 (art/candidates/island-map-3/07-overview.png): head office by the station in the west, the shopping street south of the fountain square, the dorm blocks at its east end.
