# Finds and early personal scenes

Story authored; placement, dispatch, voices and physical checks remain to build. These packs accompany days 3 to 5 and the recurring meetings that follow. They do not register themselves or enable later days. Each day's index exports the descriptors for its builder.

[Flavor finds](../../../../game3d/story/days3-5-finds.js) supplies twelve short in-place interactions at existing nooks. Each entry owns its first day, last day if any, condition, target and exact physical handling. Paper and object details are left where found. `flavor_<id>_seen` suppresses repeats; no inventory photo, album total, clock, wallet, ticket or bond changes. The single seasonal pool find expires after the October swim. The [placement contract](../../../../game3d/story/days3-5-finds-README.md) covers readable notes and restoration.

[Personal scenes](../../../../game3d/story/milestones/index.js) supplies 27 offers for the ten named NPCs: a step-2 expression of existing friendship and a step-3 scene for each, plus optional names and recurring-time variants. The [cast plots](../../cast.md#personal-plots-and-bond-milestones) own their content and progression. The pack's [build contract](../../../../game3d/story/milestones/README.md) owns eligibility, staging and dispatch. No scene grants friendship points or creates a second gate at step 2. Only a finished step-3 scene calls `bondStep`; refusal leaves the scene available. Completion is remembered once, and a daytime visit's stated period cost occurs after physical release and before its final save.

Japanese dialogue uses the same blurred presentation as day 3, with names readable and context carried by physical actions or a present translator. Proper names are spoken before their labels change. Protagonist names and address use `{mc.*}` tokens. Engine integration must preserve introduction and major-scene priority, safe returns, membership and calendar conditions, one-time completion, and the state of unrelated repairs.
