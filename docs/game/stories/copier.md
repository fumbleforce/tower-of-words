# Repair request #1: the copier

Eric's first repair request is the B2 copier, which eats paper. Mori opened the request on 1 April 1996, when he was new, and nobody closes it; three companies came to look and all said buy a new one. Mori has said 動いて to it every morning for thirty years. Today it works for Eric. Built.

## Cast

`mio`, `mori`, `eric`

## Beats

1. As soon as the chair is back, Mio gives Eric the request (she switched his screen to English): "REPAIR REQUEST #1. Copier, B2 copy room. Eats paper. Opened 1 April 1996." She shouts to Mori to show the 外人 the copier; Mori corrects her gently to 外国の方, and she passes it on, embarrassed. Mori heads for the copy room. If Eric talks to Mio before the repair, she tells him it is across the corridor and Mori has gone ahead. Mori answers an interruption with his usual bow.
2. In the copy room the copier jams: また食べられました. Mori bows to it and says 動いて. Nothing. He shrugs, then rolls his hands over each other like winding up an old engine, and says it slowly. The typing prompt carries the hand gesture and asks Eric to try the word on the copier. The kotodama effect; the copier prints a neat stack.
3. Mori, whispering: …三十年. He puts a finger to his lips, bows, and goes back to his desk.
4. Eric tells Mio. "Three companies came to look at it." Eric: "I asked it nicely." Mio: "...Asked it nicely. Like the doors this morning?" She closes the request. It's lunch ([`lunch`](lunch.md)).
5. After that, 動いて to the copier prints one blank sheet, very neatly.
