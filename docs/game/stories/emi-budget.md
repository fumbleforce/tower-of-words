# Emi's budget

Emi, B2's team lead, spends all of Eric's first day upstairs at head office, arguing for B2's parts budget (Jørgen, 2026-09-28). She gets it by promising more than B2 can do. Built for day 1; what her promise costs later isn't decided.

## Beats

2. At 17:40, when Eric sits down at his desk in the afternoon, Emi comes down in the lift: "You must be Eric. Emi, I run this lot." She's been upstairs since seven begging for money. She got it: "I may have told them B2 can keep every machine on this island running for another ten years. With the right contractor, I said." Tomorrow she and Eric should have a proper chat about what he can actually do. She walks into her own office and sits at her desk, facing the doorway, and stays there for the rest of day 1 (Jørgen, 2026-10-05: "in day 1, emi should be going to her office, not back to the elevator").
