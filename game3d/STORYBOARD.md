# Day one: storyboard

One sitting, about 10 minutes. Three places in a row: the train (side/train, brought in as it is, recoloured to the muted palette), the lobby with the security gate, and the office floor. You play Mio on her first morning at Amakawa.

The point is a small, funny first day with people worth meeting and things worth poking. Japanese comes in as a handful of spoken commands that Mio overhears where they happen naturally. Saying one of them out loud makes the world do it, a little too well. Nobody else can do that, and nobody quite notices.

## Commands

Four, one per story beat. Each is shown every time with its reading and English: 待って (matte, wait). Voiced in Japanese.

| Command | Picked up from | First use | Opens up |
|---|---|---|---|
| 待って (matte, wait) | Aoi, shouting at the closing train doors | the doors stop so Kuroda can get off | lets you freeze things for a joke (the office clock, the fan) |
| 開けて (akete, open) | Kuroda, pleading with a jammed flap gate | the gate springs open | the machine room door, the vending machine |
| 来て (kite, come) | Emi, calling Mio over | Mio's missing chair rolls back across the office | the cat, the lift |
| 動いて (ugoite, move / work) | Yui, begging the copier | the copier prints 30 copies in four seconds | the coffee machine, the fan, the plants |

How to say one: the Say button (bottom right, or F) lists the commands you know. It works on whatever is nearest; its name shows above it. When the story wants one, the line itself offers it as a big button with the Japanese, the reading and the English. There are no tests and nothing is locked behind remembering a word. Everything reacts to every command, usually with a joke.

## Cast

- Mio (you): new hire, headphones, quiet. The narration is short and in the second person.
- Aoi: sales, second year, pink hair, talks fast. Rides the same train. The station cat ignores her every single morning and it hurts.
- Tama: a calico cat who rides the monorail to the head office every day, because the guard feeds her.
- Mr. Kuroda: accounts. Falls asleep on trains, late through every gate. Polite, a bit hopeless.
- Mr. Ishibashi: the guard. Strict, dry, secretly soft on the cat. His flap gate is older than some staff and he's embarrassed by it.
- Emi: Mio's team lead. Warm, fast, a list-maker. Wants the new hire settled before Mr. Mori's 10:00 meeting.
- Mr. Mori: section chief. Four words at a time. Has one rule for new people: don't touch the copier.
- Kenji: borrowed Mio's chair because his broke. Doesn't see the problem.
- Yui: needs 30 copies for the 10:00 meeting. The copier has other plans.
- Sota: in the kitchenette, waiting on a coffee machine that has been broken for a week.
- Nao and Hiro: busy at their desks; a line each.

## Controls

Tap or click the floor to walk (the path goes round furniture), or WASD and the arrow keys. Tap a person or thing to walk up and talk or look; with keys, E or Space when its name shows. Say button (or F) to use a command on the nearest thing. Dialogue: tap anywhere or Space to go on. Desktop: dialogue bottom centre, goal top left, commands top right. Phone: dialogue full width at the bottom, Say button above it on the right.
