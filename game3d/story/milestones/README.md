# Early personal scenes

Authored nodes for [the cast plots](../../../docs/game/cast.md#personal-plots-and-bond-milestones), supporting the days 3–5 places and their recurring meetings. `index.js` exports `SCENES`, `NODES` and the requested hook. Each person's file owns the words. Claude supplies placement and dispatch; nothing here registers itself or changes the standing bond model.

All ten named NPCs have a step-2 moment and a step-3 scene. Later steps 4–5 remain in the cast plans, outside this early-days writing pass. The pack also supplies optional proper-name exchanges for the guard, Hamada and Tama. Existing day introductions cover the others. Do not replace known introductions or the built day-1 relationship scenes. At load, any old save that actually completed the same authored moment should seed its matching memory flag rather than replay it.

Step 2 requires the existing friendly threshold and gives no points or gate. Step 3 requires `bondready_<id> == 3`, then calls `bondStep` only after the scene's turn completes. No scene is locked behind a gift, repair, command or reveal. Membership gates only club meetings; Sunday sketching, the bakery, station, B2 and dorm courtyard remain ordinary place visits. Kenji's lunchtime singing is unaccompanied and can happen with his selector request still open.

Offer a scene through Talk after the day's first introductions/major club scene have finished. Walking past does not consume it. A refusal sets no completion flag. Prioritise a ready step-3 scene over its step-2 expression; step 2 is not an additional progression gate. Each completed scene records a memory and stops offering itself. A scene completion is not a romantic choice. No shared tennis choice or ticket sets these milestone flags.

Daytime substantial visits state the cost before starting and advance exactly one period after the last line and safe physical release. Evening scenes leave the period alone. The photograph and singing variants have separate daytime/evening entry nodes so deferred club visits never accidentally advance time. Continue in an incomplete scene restores safe placement and reoffers it, without a gate or period change. The builder must save the final flag, bond gate and period transition as one completion checkpoint.

Earliest eligible meetings: day 3 station lunch and the optional post-swim Emi seat; day 4 afternoon Mori sketching, evening tennis, Hamada's booking and Tama at home; day 5 weekday B2 lunch and Kenji's booth visit. Kuro's preferred window seat belongs to the gym, so it first appears at the next Saturday meeting. Club-only step-3 scenes for art, karaoke and winter swimming naturally wait for their real Tuesday, Wednesday and Saturday sessions. No extra Monday club session is invented.

The recovery rewrite and independent cold read are recorded in [day 4’s cold read](../day4/COLD-READ.md#recovery-validation-6-october). Its authoring check covers 27 offers and 194 branches, including refusals, single-use gates, daytime costs and both protagonists.
