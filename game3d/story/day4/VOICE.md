The old draft's main faults were dialogue D3 (English glosses carrying the scene instead of actions), D5 (repair instructions without an encounter) and missing physical context. The repair request is now spoken before the choice, and the fan's lever is shown first. The court uses a separate practice area while the match finishes.

Cliche defaults considered: a secretly brilliant beginner, an automatic flirt, a basket-carrying helper with no turn, friendship decided by the match score, and a teacher who suspends everyone else's evening. The scene needs an invitation, two ways to join in and room for Aoi to play. Rei's doubles game keeps going; Aoi retrieves her own ball and asks to try again. Neither choice advances a bond step. There is no score for the player.
