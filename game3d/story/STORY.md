# Day one

The intro day only. Dialogue data: train.js, gate.js, office.js, transitions.js (format in FORMAT.md). How people talk: VOICE.md. Engine needs: REQUESTS.md. Critic scores: REVIEW.md. About 15 to 20 minutes.

## Premise

Eric, a tired Nordic IT engineer, starts a support contract: he's here to help keep Amakawa's ancient systems running, because the company refuses to replace any of them. He's based in the IT support room on B2 with a small team. He barely speaks Japanese. Only Mio on the team speaks some English, and she's busy. The island's old systems, installed in the nineties, answer to spoken commands (kotodama), but only when Eric says them. Nobody knows why, Eric included.

## Language on day 1

- Mio speaks loose, imperfect English (VOICE.md). Kenji has a few words. Everyone else speaks Japanese. Lines Eric can't follow are marked `overheard: true`: they show and sound as gibberish, with only the words he has learned (and loanwords he'd catch by ear) shown clearly. Dialogue and gestures carry what he needs; narration only covers what the scene can't show.
- Each new word ends with Eric typing its romaji (`{ do: 'type' }`). Choice buttons are for real choices.
- Greetings (from Mio's lesson on the train): おはようございます (ohayō gozaimasu, good morning), よろしくおねがいします (yoroshiku onegaishimasu, nice to meet you), すみません (sumimasen, excuse me / sorry). He says them to people, and each person reacts in their own way. That reaction is the day-1 bonding.
- Commands (kotodama), four on day 1: 待って (matte, wait), 開けて (akete, open), 動いて (ugoite, work), and one from the lunch partner. Japanese, reading and English always shown together.

## The day

1. **Train (commute).** Eric sits next to Mio, coming back from a night at her mother's on the mainland with a bag of pickles. She sees B2 on his card: he's the IT contractor Mori told them about, first day, from Norway, and he speaks no Japanese. She doesn't love strangers (headphones half on, calls him 外人 instead of his name), but she'd rather not watch him embarrass B2, so she teaches him on the ride. おはようございます: he tries it on her, then on the cat (his first use of the Say button). よろしくおねがいします: they did just meet. At the station, すみません gets a woman out of the aisle ("you'll get lost today, just say it and point"). Mio points out the man asleep at the end of the bench (he sleeps every morning and sometimes misses the stop). At the station the doors start closing on him; Mio yells 待って at them out of habit and nothing happens, then Eric says it and the doors freeze mid-close, with a shimmer and the lights humming. The man wakes and stumbles out apologising. Mio says doors don't do that, and asks him not to tell anyone.
2. **Gate.** Mio's phone buzzes (a server down) and she runs ahead. Eric's card isn't live until nine. The guard, Mr. Ishibashi, only speaks Japanese and shows nine on his fingers. Mr. Hamada, the sleeping man, runs in and the new gate shuts on his briefcase; he begs it: 開けて. Two ways through: say 開けて, or すみません and a mime for the guard.
3. **Lift.** Two people from Sales talk in Japanese; Eric catches "コンサルタント" and "B2". Nobody explains.
4. **Office, morning.** Mori, the oldest on the team, meets him at the lift with a formal bow; よろしくおねがいします matters to him. Kenji, casual, has borrowed Eric's chair. Mio gives him his first ticket: the B2 copier, which has been eating paper since 1996. Mori says 動いて to it out of politeness, as he always has. Eric says it, and it works.
5. **Lunch (the one choice).** Eat with Mio in the machine room (English, the only real conversation of the day; she teaches 止まって when a rack alarm goes off), or with Mori in the kitchenette (almost no shared language, tea; he teaches 入れて to the thermos pot). Reaching the first bond step plays that scene.
6. **Afternoon.** One gift moment: a drink from the vending machine for someone. Each person answers in their own way.
7. **Evening.** Mio, leaving, lists what she's seen (the train doors, the copier, and whatever else he did) and asks how he's doing it. End.

## People Eric meets

- **Mio** (25): the team's programmer and the only one with English. Dry, low-energy, busy; keeps strangers at a distance and calls Eric 外人. Mori quietly corrects her to 外国の方 (the polite word). A flicker of warmth by the end of the train ride, no more. Likes canned coffee.
- **Mr. Ishibashi** (64): the gate guard. Strict, fair, polite Japanese. Likes a proper おはようございます.
- **Mr. Hamada** (54, Accounts): asleep on every train, late through every gate, says すみません constantly.
- **Mr. Mori** (58): a former manager, now on the team. Formal, kind, makes the tea. Likes corn soup.
- **Kenji** (29): the team's other engineer. Casual, a bit sheepish about the chair. Likes canned coffee.
- **Tama**: a calico cat who rides the train to work.

Register: casual inside the team, polite set phrases with the guard and Mori. No em dashes.
