# Day 5 working voice sheet

The main [voice sheet](../VOICE.md) governs this day. This pass uses dialogue, story-sense, humanizer and cliche-transcendence, with landed day 3's Japanese presentation.

| Speaker | What they want here | Delivery and comprehension |
|---|---|---|
| Kenji | Buy the team drinks and check songs for the club | Learner English, short connected requests. He can ask a simple question without a string of comic nouns. At the selector he asks for help before a ticket starts. His first drink reaction follows the can landing in his hand. |
| Mori | Set out art paper and share tea | Polite Japanese. He bows at introductions, points to the club card and makes room. Mio speaks Japanese to him about the demonstration and keeping it quiet, then tells the player what he agreed to. |
| Mio | Finish work and understand what the player is choosing | Loose English to the player; Japanese to Mori when he needs to understand her. She asks before the demonstration and translates only the information needed for the next action. |
| Kuro | Print usable visitor labels | Short, courteous English. She asks for help, shows the failed label and checks the corrected print. She already has reception work to finish. |
| Emi | Check in before going upstairs | Complete English sentences. She thanks the player only for closed requests. Her meeting is her own concern. |
| Aoi | Eat lunch and sit down after work | Ordinary Japanese, an open lunch box and a seat offered by moving her bag. No verbatim English interpretation of an unseen phone call. |
| Rei | Collect lunch and walk after work | Practical English. No poetic explanation of what her legs believe. |
| Player | Choose whether to show the ability | Short whole sentences, with explicit agreement or deferral. Protagonist tokens carry names and pronouns. |

The draft's problems were dialogue D3 (subtitles delivering unseen information), D2 (fragment chains) and scene context: Mori was being asked to agree in a language he does not speak. The revised scene has a visible room check, a Japanese request to Mori and a visible nod before play begins. The private conversation stays within B2. Deferral leaves the invitation available.

Default versions considered: the boss orders a demonstration, everyone already knows the secret, a secret society explains the ability, the reveal becomes a speech about destiny, or every coworker stops work to admire the player. The scene needs a voluntary first delivery with two new witnesses. Kenji is buying drinks; Mori has tea and art supplies to deal with; Mio wants to know who will see it. Their existing evening provides the occasion. No one explains the cause of the ability, and no bond step rewards it.
