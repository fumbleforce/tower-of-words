Round 6: seven harder scenes, four Anima models

Scenes range from simple to very complex, to test both prompt adherence and how forgiving each model is when a prompt asks too much. Prompts apply the lessons from round 5: every held object has a hand, emotions stay with their character, bystanders are short and face somewhere, and the main character is described as fair-skinned and slim. Click an image for full screen; arrow keys step through, Esc closes.

Settings: Euler A, 30 steps, CFG 5, seed 11. 1216×832 except the jog (896×1152).
Negative: worst quality, low quality, early, old, score_1, score_2, score_3, artist name, blurry, jpeg artifacts, bad anatomy, bad hands, missing fingers, extra fingers, long fingernails, claws, merged limbs, extra limbs, floating objects, text, watermark, child, loli, gore, blood
Why JANIMA as the fourth model. On Civitai's Anima listings for September, JANIMA v1.0 (2.9B, released 8 September) has the best rating-to-download ratio of the popular Anima checkpoints: about 2,060 thumbs-up on 21,000 downloads, against about 1,050 on 28,000 for RDBT. It is a finetune of the Anima base aimed at cleaner anatomy and more coherent composition, from the author of the well-regarded JANKU SDXL line. RDBT was already on its latest version (b1 v2.3).
Summary. RDBT is still the most consistent for drama and action. JANIMA is the surprise: it is the only model that understood the copy-room scene and drew the cleanest cabin group, but it also produced the worst image of the round (Rei standing on the pool). Aesthetic is dependable but adds blush and stubble the prompt asked it not to. One Obsession has the nicest painterly style and falls apart first in crowded scenes. Worth considering: RDBT as the default and JANIMA for scenes with several interacting characters.
